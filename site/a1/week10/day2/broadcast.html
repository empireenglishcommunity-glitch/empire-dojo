📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Nour
Yesterday was my birthday. I was very happy. My family was at home with me. My mother was in the kitchen all morning. There was a big cake on the table. It was chocolate. My friends were here at six o'clock. We were together for three hours. There were ten people in my small flat! My little brother was very excited. It was a good day. Today I am tired, but I am happy.
