My Birthday Yesterday

عيد ميلادي إمبارح

حكاية عن عيد ميلاد إمبارح بصيغة was/were.

📄 Read / اقرا

Yesterday was Friday.

It was my birthday.

I was very happy.

My family were with me at home.

There was a party.

My friends were there too.

The food was delicious.

My mother was in the kitchen all day.

Last week I was sick, but yesterday I was fine.

It was a wonderful day.

Why was it wonderful?

Because my family were together.
