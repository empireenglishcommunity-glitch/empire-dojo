My Family

عيلتي

الكاتب بيوصف أبوه وأمه وأخوه وأخواته وجدته.

📄 Read / اقرا

This is my family.

My father is Ali.

He is fifty years old.

My mother is Mona.

She is a kind woman.

I have got one brother and two sisters.

My brother's name is Youssef.

He is a baby.

He is very young.

My sisters are Nour and Hana.

We have got a big family.

My grandmother lives with us.

She is old, and we love her.
