📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Possessive adjectives + 'have got'

صفات الملكية و have got

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

my/your/his/her/our/their; talk about family and belongings with have got / has got.

صفات الملكية والكلام عن العائلة والممتلكات بـ have got.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

Subject + have got / has got + object | my / your / his / her / our / their + noun

[He] + [has got] + [two brothers]

⚡ Possessive BEFORE the noun. I/you/we/they → have got · he/she/it → has got.

صفة الملكية قبل الاسم. و has got مع he/she/it فقط.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Use possessive adjectives to show who something belongs to, and have got / has got to talk about family and belongings.

نستخدم صفات الملكية لبيان مين صاحب الشيء، و have got / has got للكلام عن العائلة والممتلكات.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic attaches possession as a suffix (كتابي = 'book-my'), so the separate word order 'my book' feels reversed. Also Arabic uses 'عندي' (at me) rather than a 'have' verb.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

This is my sister.

هذه أختي

[my] + [sister]

He has got two brothers.

عنده أخوان

[He] + [has got] + [two brothers]

Their house is big.

بيتهم كبير

[Their] + [house] + [is big]

I have got a small family.

عندي عائلة صغيرة

[I] + [have got] + [a small family]

Her mother is a doctor.

أمها طبيبة

[Her] + [mother] + [is a doctor]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ The sister my is tall.

✅ My sister is tall.

The possessive comes BEFORE the noun in English.

❌ He have got two sons.

✅ He has got two sons.

he/she/it use HAS got.

❌ I have got a brother his name Ali.

✅ I have got a brother. His name is Ali.

Do not drop 'is' — see week 1.

✍️ Practice / تمرين

Fill in the blank. Completing all of them marks this exercise done. / املا الفراغ. لما تخلص كلهم التمرين يتحسب.

This is ___ father (of me).

She ___ got three children.

___ house is near the school (of them).

We ___ got a big family.

What is ___ name (of you)?

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

MY book, not 'book my'.

🎙️ Use it when you speak / استخدمها وانت بتتكلم

Describe your family: 'I have got two sisters. My father is a teacher. Their names are ___.'

Completes automatically when you finish the practice. / بيتقفل تلقائيًا لما تخلص التمرين.
