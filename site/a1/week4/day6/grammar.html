📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Place prepositions + 'this/that/these/those'

حروف المكان وأسماء الإشارة

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

in / on / under / next to / behind / between + place | this·that (singular) · these·those (plural)

[The book] + [is on] + [the table]

⚡ Near → this/these · Far → that/those. IN a box, ON a surface, AT a place.

القريب this/these والبعيد that/those. و IN للداخل و ON للسطح و AT للمكان.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Use place prepositions to say WHERE something is, and this/that/these/those to point at things near or far.

نستخدم حروف المكان لتحديد مكان الشيء، وأسماء الإشارة للإشارة للقريب والبعيد.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic demonstratives agree with gender (هذا/هذه) but not with distance in the same way, and Arabic uses fewer place prepositions, so 'on/in/at' get interchanged.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

The book is on the table.

الكتاب على الطاولة

[on] + [the table]

This is my room.

هذه غرفتي

[This] + [is] + [my room]

Those are my shoes.

تلك أحذيتي

[Those] + [are] + [my shoes]

The car is behind the house.

السيارة خلف المنزل

[behind] + [the house]

The chair is between the bed and the window.

الكرسي بين السرير والنافذة

[between] + [A and B]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ This are my shoes.

✅ These are my shoes.

Plural needs THESE (near) or THOSE (far).

❌ The book is in the table.

✅ The book is on the table.

ON a surface, IN a container.

❌ I am in home.

✅ I am at home.

'At home' is a fixed expression.
