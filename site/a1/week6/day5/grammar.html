📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Present simple (he/she/it) -s + some/any

إضافة -s للغائب المفرد و some/any

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

He/She/It + verb + S | Questions: Does + he/she/it + base verb | some (+) / any (− ?)

[She] + [likes] + [tea]

⚡ he/she/it → verb + S. After DOES/DOESN'T → base verb. SOME in (+), ANY in (−) and (?).

he/she/it تأخذ الفعل + S. وبعد does/doesn't الفعل يرجع مجرد. some في الإثبات و any في النفي والسؤال.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Add -s to the verb for he/she/it. Use 'some' in positive sentences and 'any' in negatives and questions.

نضيف -s للفعل مع he/she/it. ونستخدم some في الإثبات و any في النفي والسؤال.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

The third-person -s has no Arabic equivalent and is the most commonly dropped ending at A1. Also, once 'does' is used, learners often keep the -s twice ('does she likes').

💡 Examples / أمثلة

She likes tea.

هي تحب الشاي

[She] + [likes] + [tea]

Does he eat meat?

هل يأكل لحم؟

[Does] + [he] + [eat]?

We have some bread.

عندنا بعض الخبز

[some] + [bread]

There isn't any milk.

مافيش لبن

[isn't] + [any] + [milk]

He doesn't drink coffee.

هو لا يشرب قهوة

[He] + [doesn't] + [drink]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ She like tea.

✅ She likes tea.

he/she/it need the -s ending.

❌ Does she likes tea?

✅ Does she like tea?

After DOES the verb goes back to base form.

❌ I don't have some money.

✅ I don't have any money.

Use ANY in negatives and questions.
