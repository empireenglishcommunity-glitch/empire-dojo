At the Market

في السوق

كريم بيسأل عن الأسعار في السوق ويشتري.

📄 Read / اقرا

Karim needs a new shirt.

He goes to the market.

The shop is open.

"Good morning.

I'd like this blue shirt, please.

How much is it?"

"It is one hundred pounds."

"That is expensive!

And how much are these shoes?"

"The shoes are cheap.

They are fifty pounds."

Karim buys the shoes.

He pays with cash.

"Here you are."

"Thank you."
