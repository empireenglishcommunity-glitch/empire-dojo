Market announcer
Welcome to the market. Listen to today's prices. Bread is three pounds. How much is the rice? The rice is twelve pounds for one kilo. The tomatoes are eight pounds. The eggs are forty pounds for thirty eggs. Tea is twenty-five pounds. We have some apples today, and they are fifteen pounds. The market is open from seven in the morning to seven in the evening. Come early!
