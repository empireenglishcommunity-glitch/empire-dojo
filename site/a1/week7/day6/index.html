💬 This Week's Pattern / نمط الأسبوع

'How much / how many' + shopping language

how much / how many ولغة الشراء

How much + uncountable | How many + countable plural | I'd like + noun

📍 Use 'how much' for price and for things you cannot count, 'how many' for things you can count, and 'I'd like' to order politely.
