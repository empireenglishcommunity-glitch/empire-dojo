📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

'can/can't' (ability) + like/love + -ing

can/can't للقدرة و like/love مع الفعل+ing

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Express ability (can/can't) and preferences (like/love/hate + verb-ing).

التعبير عن القدرة (can/can't) والتفضيلات (like/love + الفعل+ing).

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

Subject + can / can't + base verb | Subject + like / love / hate + verb-ing

[I] + [can] + [swim] · [She] + [likes] + [reading]

⚡ can + base verb (no 'to', no -s). like/love/hate + verb-ing.

can مع الفعل المجرد (بدون to وبدون s). و like/love مع الفعل+ing.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Use can/can't for ability, and like/love/hate + -ing to talk about hobbies and preferences.

نستخدم can/can't للقدرة، و like/love/hate مع الفعل+ing للكلام عن الهوايات والتفضيلات.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Learners often add 'to' after can ('I can to swim') because Arabic uses a subjunctive linker. After like/love the English verb takes -ing, not the infinitive.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

I can swim.

أقدر أعوم

[I] + [can] + [swim]

He can't drive.

هو لا يقدر يسوق

[He] + [can't] + [drive]

She likes reading.

هي تحب القراءة

[She] + [likes] + [reading]

We love playing football.

نحب لعب الكرة

[We] + [love] + [playing]

Can you speak English?

هل تتكلم إنجليزي؟

[Can] + [you] + [speak]?

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ I can to swim.

✅ I can swim.

Never put 'to' after can/can't.

❌ He can swims.

✅ He can swim.

After can, the verb has no -s.

❌ I like to swimming.

✅ I like swimming.

like + verb-ing (no 'to').

✍️ Practice / تمرين

Fill in the blank. Completing all of them marks this exercise done. / املا الفراغ. لما تخلص كلهم التمرين يتحسب.

I ___ (can) play the guitar.

She ___ (not can) cook.

They love ___ (swim).

___ you speak Arabic?

He likes ___ (read) books.

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

CAN + bare verb. LIKE + -ING.

🎙️ Use it when you speak / استخدمها وانت بتتكلم

Talk about yourself: 'I can swim but I can't drive. I love reading and I like playing football.'

Completes automatically when you finish the practice. / بيتقفل تلقائيًا لما تخلص التمرين.
