What Ahmed Can Do

أحمد بيقدر يعمل إيه

أحمد بيحكي عن قدراته وهواياته ووقت فراغه.

📄 Read / اقرا

Ahmed can swim very well.

He goes to the sea every weekend.

He can't drive a car, because he is young.

He loves playing football with his friends.

In his free time he likes reading and drawing.

He can sing a little, but he can't dance.

His sister can ride a bike.

She enjoys music.

Together they play games.

It is fun.
