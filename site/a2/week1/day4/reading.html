Nadia's Busy Weekend

عطلة نادية المزحومة

نادية بتحكي كل اللي عملته السبت والأحد.

📄 Read / اقرا

Last weekend was very busy for Nadia.

On Saturday morning she cleaned her room and helped her mother in the kitchen.

They cooked rice and chicken together.

In the afternoon she visited her grandmother.

They talked for two hours and listened to old music.

Nadia walked home at six o'clock.

On Sunday she studied English for three hours, because she wanted a good mark.

Then she called her friend Mona.

They watched a film and enjoyed it a lot.

Nadia finished her homework late at night.

She was tired, but she was happy.
