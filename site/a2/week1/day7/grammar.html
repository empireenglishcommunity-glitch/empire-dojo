📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Past simple — regular verbs (-ed)

الماضي البسيط — الأفعال المنتظمة (-ed)

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Talk about finished past actions with regular -ed verbs; time expressions (yesterday, last week, ago).

الكلام عن أحداث ماضية منتهية بالأفعال المنتظمة (-ed) وتعبيرات الوقت.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

Subject + verb-ed + (object) + past time expression

[I] + [worked] + [yesterday]

⚡ Regular past = verb + -ed, for a finished action at a known past time.

الماضي المنتظم = الفعل + -ed، لحدث منتهٍ في وقت ماضٍ معروف.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Use the past simple with -ed for finished actions at a definite time in the past (yesterday, last week, in 2019).

نستخدم الماضي البسيط بـ -ed للأحداث المنتهية في وقت محدد في الماضي.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic marks the past inside the verb, so learners forget the -ed ending in English ('I work yesterday') or spell it but drop it in speech.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

I watched a film yesterday.

شاهدت فيلمًا أمس

[watched] + [yesterday]

We played football last week.

لعبنا كرة القدم الأسبوع الماضي

[played] + [last week]

She worked late.

عملت لوقت متأخر

[She] + [worked] + [late]

They lived in Cairo.

عاشوا في القاهرة

[lived] + [in Cairo]

I wanted to help.

أردت أن أساعد

[wanted] + [to help]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ I work yesterday.

✅ I worked yesterday.

Finished past action needs -ed.

❌ She played not.

✅ She didn't play.

Negatives use didn't + base verb (week 2).

❌ I watchd a film.

✅ I watched a film.

Keep the -ed; pronounce it as /t/ here.
