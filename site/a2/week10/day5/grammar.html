📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Present perfect for experience (ever / never, been)

المضارع التام للخبرة (ever / never, been)

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Talk about life experience without a specific time using have/has + past participle; ever/never; been to.

الكلام عن الخبرات الحياتية بدون وقت محدد بـ have/has + التصريف الثالث؛ ever/never؛ been to.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

Subject + have/has + past participle · ever (?) · never (−) · been to

[I] + [have been] + [to Cairo]

⚡ Experience (no time) → have/has + past participle. ever (?), never (−), been (there & back).

خبرة بدون وقت → have/has + التصريف الثالث. ever للسؤال، never للنفي، been (راح ورجع).

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Talk about life experiences without saying WHEN: have/has + past participle; ever in questions, never in negatives.

للكلام عن خبرات الحياة بدون تحديد الوقت: have/has + التصريف الثالث؛ ever في السؤال، never في النفي.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

The present perfect has no direct Arabic equivalent, so learners use the past simple ('Did you ever go…?') and confuse 'been' (visited and returned) with 'gone' (went and still there).

💡 Examples / أمثلة

Have you ever been to London?

هل سبق أن ذهبت إلى لندن؟

[Have] + [ever been]?

I have never eaten sushi.

لم يسبق أن أكلت سوشي

[have never eaten]

She has been to three countries.

زارت ثلاث دول

[has been to]

We've seen that film.

شاهدنا ذلك الفيلم

[have seen]

He has just arrived.

لقد وصل للتو

[has just arrived]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ Did you ever been there?

✅ Have you ever been there?

Experience → present perfect, not did.

❌ I have ever eaten sushi.

✅ I have never eaten sushi.

'ever' is for questions; 'never' for negatives.

❌ She has gone to Cairo. (and is back)

✅ She has been to Cairo.

'been' = visited & returned; 'gone' = still there.
