🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

Which rule does the manager call a safety rule?

أنهي قاعدة قال عليها المدير إنها قاعدة أمان؟

Arriving before eight thirty
Not using your phone on the factory floor
Taking your break between one and two

❓ Question / سؤال 1

Who must you call if you are going to be late?

تكلّم مين لو هتتأخر؟

A colleague
Your manager
Anyone on the factory floor

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What does he say about clothes?

قال إيه عن اللبس؟

You must wear a suit
You don't have to wear a suit, but wear something clean and simple
Clothes are not important at all

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What must you do if a machine sounds strange?

تعمل إيه لو ماكينة صوتها غريب؟

Stop it and tell someone immediately
Wait until your break
Ask a colleague to guess

🔒 Answer the main-point question above to unlock the detail questions. / جاوب على سؤال الفكرة الأساسية فوق عشان تفتح أسئلة التفاصيل.

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Manager
Welcome to the team. Before you start, there are a few things you have to know. You must arrive before eight thirty. If you are going to be late, you have to call your manager, not a colleague. You don't have to wear a suit, but you should wear something clean and simple. You must not use your phone on the factory floor. That is a safety rule, not a suggestion. You should ask questions in your first week. Nobody expects you to know everything, and it is much better to ask than to guess. If a machine sounds strange, you must stop it and tell someone immediately. You have to take your break between one and two, and you should eat something, because people who skip lunch make more mistakes in the afternoon. Good luck.
