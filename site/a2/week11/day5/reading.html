Rules for New Workers

قواعد للموظفين الجدد

قواعد الشغل للموظف الجديد.

📄 Read / اقرا

Welcome to your new job.

Please read these rules.

You have to arrive on time; the office opens at eight o'clock.

You mustn't be late for a meeting with a customer.

You have to wear the uniform, but you don't have to wear it on Friday.

You should ask your boss if you have a problem.

You mustn't use your phone during a meeting.

You have to finish your work before the deadline.

You should take a break for thirty minutes at one o'clock.

If you are sick, you must call the office early.

Good luck!
