📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Presenter
Our last story this week comes from Tarek in Tanta. Tarek wrote to us a year ago, because he wanted to change his job but he was afraid. He was working in his uncle's shop, and although the money was enough, he was bored. So he decided to study in the evening. First he studied for two hours after work, but he was too tired, so he changed his plan. He started waking up at five and studying before work instead. It was hard at the beginning, but after three months it became normal. Then, in March, he applied for a job in a small company in Tanta, and he got it. He earns a little less than before; however, he says he is much happier, because he is learning something new every week. Tarek, thank you for writing. And if you are waiting for the perfect moment, it is probably going to be a Tuesday morning at five.
