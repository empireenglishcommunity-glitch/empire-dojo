📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Past simple — irregular verbs + did/didn't

الماضي البسيط — الأفعال الشاذة و did/didn't

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Common irregular past forms (went, saw, had, made) and questions/negatives with did / didn't.

الأفعال الشاذة في الماضي والأسئلة والنفي بـ did / didn't.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

Positive: irregular past (went, saw) · Negative: didn't + base · Question: Did + subject + base?

[Did] + [you] + [see]? → [I] + [saw]

⚡ Irregular = new word (went, saw, bought). After did/didn't → base verb.

الشاذ = كلمة جديدة (went, saw). وبعد did/didn't → الفعل المجرد.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Use irregular past forms for finished actions; use did/didn't (+ base verb) for questions and negatives.

نستخدم صيغ الماضي الشاذة للأحداث المنتهية، و did/didn't مع الفعل المجرد للأسئلة والنفي.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Learners add -ed to irregular verbs ('goed') and keep the past form after 'did' ('Did you went?'). After did/didn't the verb must return to its base form.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

I went to Cairo.

ذهبت إلى القاهرة

[went] (irregular)

Did you see him?

هل رأيته؟

[Did] + [you] + [see]?

They didn't come.

لم يأتوا

[didn't] + [come]

She bought a ticket.

اشترت تذكرة

[bought] (irregular)

We took the train.

أخذنا القطار

[took] (irregular)

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ I goed home.

✅ I went home.

'Go' is irregular: go → went, no -ed.

❌ Did you went?

✅ Did you go?

After 'did', use the BASE verb.

❌ She didn't came.

✅ She didn't come.

After 'didn't', use the base verb.

✍️ Practice / تمرين

Fill in the blank. Completing all of them marks this exercise done. / املا الفراغ. لما تخلص كلهم التمرين يتحسب.

I ___ (go) to the market.

___ you ___ (see) the film?

They ___ (not come) to the party.

She ___ (buy) a new phone.

We ___ (take) a taxi.

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

DID/DIDN'T → base verb, always.

🎙️ Use it when you speak / استخدمها وانت بتتكلم

Ask and answer: 'Did you go out yesterday?' 'Yes, I went to a café and saw a friend.'

Completes automatically when you finish the practice. / بيتقفل تلقائيًا لما تخلص التمرين.
