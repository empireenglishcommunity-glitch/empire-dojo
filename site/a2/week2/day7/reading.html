Our Trip to Alexandria

رحلتنا لإسكندرية

حكاية رحلة العيلة لإسكندرية بالقطر.

📄 Read / اقرا

Last month my family went to Alexandria by train.

We left home very early and arrived at nine o'clock.

We took a taxi to the hotel.

The hotel was small but clean.

In the morning we saw the sea and swam for an hour.

My father bought fish for lunch and my mother made a salad.

We ate on the beach.

In the afternoon I met a boy from Cairo and we played football together.

I didn't want to go home!

We came back on Sunday night, and I wrote about the trip in my notebook.
