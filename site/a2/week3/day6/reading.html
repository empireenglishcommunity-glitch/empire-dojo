Cairo and My Village

القاهرة وقريتي

مقارنة بين القاهرة والقرية.

📄 Read / اقرا

I live in a small village, but my brother lives in Cairo.

Cairo is much bigger and busier than my village.

The streets there are more crowded, and life is faster.

Food and rent are more expensive in the city.

My village is quieter and cleaner, and the air is better.

In summer Cairo is hotter than my village.

My brother says the city is more interesting, because there is more work and more to do.

I think my village is safer for children.

For me the village is nicer, but Cairo is the best place to find a job.
