The Most Famous Places

أشهر الأماكن

أشهر وأحسن الأماكن في مصر.

📄 Read / اقرا

Egypt has some of the most famous places in the world.

The Nile is the longest river in Africa.

The Pyramids of Giza are the oldest buildings in the country, and they are the most popular place for visitors.

The Egyptian Museum in Cairo is one of the best museums in the world.

For me, the most beautiful city is Aswan, because it is the quietest.

Summer is the hottest time to travel, so spring is the easiest.

The cheapest way to travel between cities is the train.

The busiest day at the Pyramids is Friday, so go early.
