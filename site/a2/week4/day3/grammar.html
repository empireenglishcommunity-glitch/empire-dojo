📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Superlative adjectives (the -est / the most)

صفات التفضيل (the -est / the most)

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

the + short adj + -est · the most + long adj · one of the + superlative + plural

[It] + [is the biggest] + [city]

⚡ the + -est (short) / the most (long). Irregulars: the best, the worst.

the + -est للقصيرة / the most للطويلة. والشاذ: the best, the worst.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Say which is number one in a group: short adjectives take 'the -est', longer ones take 'the most'.

للتعبير عن الأعلى في مجموعة: الصفات القصيرة the -est، والطويلة the most.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Learners omit 'the' before superlatives (Arabic marks it differently) and mix '-est' with 'most' ('the most biggest').

💡 Examples / أمثلة

It is the biggest city.

هي أكبر مدينة

[the biggest]

This is the most beautiful place.

هذا أجمل مكان

[the most beautiful]

He is one of the best players.

هو من أفضل اللاعبين

[one of the best]

That was the worst day.

كان أسوأ يوم

[the worst] (irregular)

It's the cheapest hotel here.

هو أرخص فندق هنا

[the cheapest]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ It is biggest city.

✅ It is the biggest city.

Superlatives need 'the'.

❌ The most biggest.

✅ The biggest.

Use either -est OR most, never both.

❌ He is the most good.

✅ He is the best.

'Good' is irregular: the best.
