Weather presenter
And now the weather. Tomorrow will be warm and dry in Cairo, with a high of thirty-one degrees. There will be some wind in the afternoon, but it will not be strong. On Wednesday the sky will be cloudy in the morning, and it will probably clear by midday. Thursday will be the hottest day of the week, thirty-six degrees, so please drink plenty of water and stay inside between twelve and three. In Alexandria it will be cooler, about twenty-six, and there will be a chance of light rain on Thursday evening. The weekend will be pleasant everywhere. Temperatures will fall to twenty-eight, and it will feel much more comfortable. That's all from me. Have a good week.
