📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Present continuous (now) vs present simple

المضارع المستمر (الآن) مقابل المضارع البسيط

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Contrast actions happening now (am/is/are + -ing) with habits and routines (present simple).

الفرق بين الأحداث اللي بتحصل دلوقتي (am/is/are + -ing) والعادات.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

Now: am/is/are + verb-ing · Routine: present simple (base / -s)

[I usually walk], but [I'm taking the bus now]

⚡ Now → am/is/are + -ing. Every day/usually → present simple.

دلوقتي → am/is/are + -ing. كل يوم/عادةً → المضارع البسيط.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Use the present continuous for actions happening now; use the present simple for habits and routines.

نستخدم المضارع المستمر للأحداث اللي بتحصل دلوقتي، والبسيط للعادات والروتين.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic uses one present form for both, so learners say 'I read now' for a current action or 'I am going every day' for a habit.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

I usually walk, but today I'm taking the bus.

عادةً أمشي، لكن اليوم أركب الأتوبيس

[usually walk] vs [am taking]

She is cooking now.

هي بتطبخ دلوقتي

[is cooking] (now)

What are you doing?

ماذا تفعل؟

[are] + [you doing]?

He works in a bank.

هو يعمل في بنك

[works] (routine)

They are studying at the moment.

هم بيذاكروا الآن

[are studying]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ I read now.

✅ I'm reading now.

Action happening now → continuous.

❌ I am going to the gym every day.

✅ I go to the gym every day.

Habit → present simple.

❌ She is knowing the answer.

✅ She knows the answer.

State verbs (know, like, want) are not usually continuous.

✍️ Practice / تمرين

Fill in the blank. Completing all of them marks this exercise done. / املا الفراغ. لما تخلص كلهم التمرين يتحسب.

Be quiet — the baby ___ (sleep).

I ___ (go) to work by bus every day.

What ___ you ___ (do) right now?

She usually ___ (drink) tea, but today she ___ (drink) coffee.

They ___ (watch) TV at the moment.

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

RIGHT NOW → -ING. USUALLY → simple.

🎙️ Use it when you speak / استخدمها وانت بتتكلم

Contrast now and usually: 'I usually study at night, but right now I'm relaxing.'

Completes automatically when you finish the practice. / بيتقفل تلقائيًا لما تخلص التمرين.
