In the Café Right Now

في الكافيه دلوقتي

وصف اللي بيحصل في الكافيه في اللحظة دي.

📄 Read / اقرا

I am sitting in a café near my house.

I usually come here on Fridays.

Right now I am drinking tea and writing in my notebook.

Outside it is raining, so the street is quiet.

A man is standing near the door.

He is wearing a black jacket and he is talking on the phone.

Two students are sitting near the window.

They are reading and they are waiting for their teacher.

These days the café is busier than before, because the coffee is cheap and the internet is fast.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What is the writer doing right now?

الكاتب بيعمل إيه دلوقتي؟

Standing near the door
Drinking tea and writing

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What is the man near the door wearing?

الراجل اللي جمب الباب لابس إيه؟

A black jacket
A white shirt
New shoes

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What are the two students waiting for?

الطالبين مستنيين إيه؟

Their teacher
Their food
The rain to stop

❓ Question / سؤال 4

Why is the café busier these days?

الكافيه أزحم الأيام دي ليه؟

The coffee is cheap and the internet is fast
It is bigger now
It is always raining
