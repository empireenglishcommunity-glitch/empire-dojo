The Doctor's Instructions

تعليمات الدكتورة

عمر بيتبع تعليمات الدكتورة فيخف.

📄 Read / اقرا

Omar went to the doctor because he had a headache and a fever.

The doctor listened carefully and then spoke slowly.

'Take this medicine twice a day, after food,' she said.

'Drink water often and sleep well.

Don't work hard for three days.

Walk quietly for twenty minutes every day, but don't run fast.'

Omar wrote the instructions carefully in his phone.

'If the pain continues after three days, come back quickly,' the doctor said.

Omar felt better easily after two days, because he followed the instructions well.
