📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Recorded message
Thank you for calling Doctor Samir's clinic. Please listen carefully to this message. The clinic is open daily from nine until five, and on Friday we close early, at one o'clock. If you have an appointment, please arrive ten minutes early and bring your card. If you are waiting for test results, do not come to the clinic; we will send them to you by message. If you are feeling very unwell, or if you are breathing badly, do not wait for an appointment. Go straight to the hospital on Nile Street. After the beep, please speak slowly and clearly, and leave your name, your phone number and the reason for your call. We answer messages quickly, usually on the same day.
