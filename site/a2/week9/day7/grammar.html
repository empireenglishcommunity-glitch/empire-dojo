📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Adverbs of manner (well, quickly, carefully)

ظروف الحال (well, quickly, carefully)

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

adjective + -ly → adverb · irregular: good → well · usually after the verb/object

[She] + [speaks] + [English] + [well]

⚡ How? → adjective + -ly (after the verb). Irregular: good → well; hard stays 'hard'.

كيف؟ → الصفة + -ly (بعد الفعل). والشاذ: good → well؛ و 'hard' تبقى كما هي.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Use adverbs of manner to describe HOW an action is done; most add -ly, but 'good' becomes 'well'.

نستخدم ظروف الحال لوصف كيفية أداء الفعل؛ معظمها بـ -ly، لكن 'good' تصير 'well'.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic often uses the adjective form for manner, so learners say 'She speaks good' instead of 'well', and place the adverb before the verb.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

She speaks English well.

تتحدث الإنجليزية جيدًا

[speaks] + [well]

He drives carefully.

يقود بحذر

[drives] + [carefully]

Please walk quickly.

من فضلك امشِ بسرعة

[walk] + [quickly]

They worked hard.

عملوا بجد

[worked] + [hard]

She sang beautifully.

غنّت بشكل جميل

[sang] + [beautifully]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ She speaks English good.

✅ She speaks English well.

Manner of a verb → adverb 'well', not 'good'.

❌ He carefully drives fast.

✅ He drives carefully.

Manner adverb usually goes after the verb/object.

❌ They worked hardly.

✅ They worked hard.

'hardly' means 'almost not' — a different word.
