Present perfect vs past simple (for / since)

المضارع التام مقابل الماضي البسيط (for / since)

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Contrast finished past (past simple) with experience/unfinished time (present perfect); for/since, already/yet/just.

الفرق بين الماضي المنتهي والخبرة/الزمن غير المنتهي؛ for/since وalready/yet/just.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

have/has + past participle (unfinished/result now) · past simple (finished time) · for + period · since + point

[I've lived here] + [for five years] // [I lived there] + [in 2019]

⚡ Still connected to now → present perfect (for/since). Finished time (yesterday, in 2019) → past simple.

متصل بالحاضر → مضارع تام (for/since). وقت انتهى (yesterday) → ماضٍ بسيط.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Present perfect: an action connected to now (still true, or the result matters), often with for/since/just/already/yet. Past simple: a finished action at a finished time (yesterday, in 2019, last week).

المضارع التام: فعل متصل بالحاضر (لسه مستمر أو نتيجته مهمة)، وغالبًا مع for/since/just/already/yet. الماضي البسيط: فعل انتهى في وقت انتهى (yesterday، in 2019).

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic has no present perfect, so learners use the past simple for everything ('I live here since 2019' or 'I lived here for five years' when they still live here). They also mix 'for' (a period) with 'since' (a starting point).

💡 Examples / أمثلة

I have lived here for five years.

أعيش هنا منذ خمس سنوات (ولسه)

[have lived] + [for + period]

She has worked here since 2020.

تعمل هنا منذ 2020

[has worked] + [since + point]

We visited Cairo last summer.

زرنا القاهرة الصيف الماضي

[past simple] + [finished time]

Have you finished your homework yet?

هل أنهيت واجبك بعد؟

[Have + finished] + yet?

He has just left the office.

لقد غادر المكتب للتو

[has just left]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ I live here since 2019.

✅ I have lived here since 2019.

Still true now → present perfect, not present simple.

❌ I have seen him yesterday.

✅ I saw him yesterday.

'yesterday' is a finished time → past simple.

❌ I have worked here since five years.

✅ I have worked here for five years.

'for' = a period; 'since' = a starting point.

✍️ Practice / تمرين

Fill in the blank. Completing all of them marks this exercise done. / املا الفراغ. لما تخلص كلهم التمرين يتحسب.

I ___ (live) here for ten years and I'm still here.

___ you finished the report yet?

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

Finished time word → past simple. 'For a period, since a point' → present perfect.
