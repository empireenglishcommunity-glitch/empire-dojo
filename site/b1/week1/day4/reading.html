News From Yasmin

أخبار من ياسمين

إيميل من ياسمين بتحكي فيه اللي حصل معاها مؤخرًا.

📄 Read / اقرا

Hi Karim, I haven't written for a long time, so here is my news.

A lot has happened lately.

I have just finished my first year at university, and I have already started a part-time job in a bookshop.

I have been there since March.

The biggest change is that we moved to a new flat in February.

I lost my old room, which I loved, but the new area is much quieter.

So far I have met three of the neighbours and they seem kind.

I have improved my English a lot this year, because I decided to read one article every morning.

I didn't enjoy it at the beginning, but recently it has become a habit.

Last week I even won a small prize in a writing competition at the university!

What about you?

Has anything changed since we last met?

Write soon and tell me everything.

me everything. Yasmin
