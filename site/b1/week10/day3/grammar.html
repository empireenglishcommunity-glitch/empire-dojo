📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Relative clauses (who / which / that / where / whose)

جُمل الوصل (who / which / that / where / whose)

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

noun + who (people) / which (things) / that (both) / where (places) / whose (possession) + clause

[the man] + [who lives next door] + [is a doctor]

⚡ People → who · things → which · either → that · places → where · possession → whose. Never repeat the object pronoun.

أشخاص → who · أشياء → which · الاثنان → that · أماكن → where · ملكية → whose. لا تُعِد ضمير المفعول.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

To add information about a noun inside one sentence instead of two: who for people, which for things, that for either (informal), where for places, whose for possession. Defining clauses take no commas; extra-information clauses take commas and cannot use 'that'.

لإضافة معلومة عن اسم داخل جملة واحدة بدل جملتين: who للأشخاص، which للأشياء، that للاثنين، where للأماكن، whose للملكية. الجملة المحدِّدة بدون فواصل، والإضافية بفواصل ولا تستخدم that.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic relative clauses repeat the object as a pronoun (الرجل الذي رأيته), so learners say 'the man who I saw him'. They also use 'which' for people and 'who' for things, and add commas to defining clauses.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

The man who lives next door is a doctor.

الرجل الذي يسكن بالجوار طبيب

[noun] + who + [verb]

This is the book which changed my life.

هذا الكتاب الذي غيّر حياتي

[noun] + which + [verb]

That's the café where we first met.

هذا المقهى الذي تقابلنا فيه أول مرة

[place] + where + [clause]

She's the student whose project won.

هي الطالبة التي فاز مشروعها

[noun] + whose + [noun]

My brother, who lives in Dubai, is visiting us.

أخي، الذي يسكن في دبي، سيزورنا

[noun], who ..., (extra info)

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ The man who I saw him was tall.

✅ The man who I saw was tall.

Don't repeat the object pronoun after the relative pronoun.

❌ The book who I read was great.

✅ The book which/that I read was great.

'who' is for people; things take which/that.

❌ My brother, that lives in Dubai, is visiting.

✅ My brother, who lives in Dubai, is visiting.

'that' cannot be used in extra-information clauses with commas.

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

One noun, one relative pronoun, no extra 'him/it'.

🎙️ Use it when you speak / استخدمها وانت بتتكلم

In accent week 10 you practised weak who/that inside the flow. Say: 'the MAN whu lives next DOOR' — the relative pronoun is light and unstressed, gluing the two ideas together.
