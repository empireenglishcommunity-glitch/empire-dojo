💬 This Week's Pattern / نمط الأسبوع

Reported speech (say / tell + backshift)

الكلام المنقول (say / tell + تغيير الزمن)

said (that) + clause · told + person + (that) + clause · tense shifts back one step · asked if / asked + wh-

📍 To report what someone said. The verb usually shifts back one tense (am → was, will → would, is going → was going), and pronouns/time words change. Use 'said' without a person and 'told' WITH a person. Questions become 'asked if' or 'asked + question word' in statement order.

💡 "He said (that) he was tired."
