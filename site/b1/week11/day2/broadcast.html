📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Presenter
Welcome to the week in review. On Sunday the transport minister said that fuel prices would not rise before the summer. On Monday his office told a newspaper that he had been talking about diesel only. By Wednesday, petrol was up nine per cent. Layla, what happened?
Analyst
Two things happened. First, he said something narrower than people heard. He told the conference that there were no plans for an increase, and 'no plans' is not a promise. Second, nobody asked him the obvious follow-up question. If a journalist had asked him whether he meant petrol as well, we would have known on Sunday. Instead, three papers reported that he had promised prices would stay the same, and one reported that he had refused to comment, which was not true either. He did comment. He was simply careful.
Presenter
So who was wrong?
Analyst
Everybody was a little wrong, which is the most uncomfortable answer there is.
