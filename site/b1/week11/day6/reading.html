What the Report Said

خبر بينقل تصريحات ناس مختلفة عن مشروع.

📄 Read / اقرا

A report about the new bus line was published yesterday, and it has already caused an argument.

The transport company announced that the line would open in June.

Its director explained that the work had taken longer than expected, and admitted that costs had risen by twenty per cent.

He promised that no ticket price would change this year.

Local shop owners complained that the building work had reduced their sales.

One owner said that she had lost half of her customers since January.

She added that nobody from the company had spoken to her.

A journalist asked whether the June date was realistic.

The director replied that it was, but he mentioned that two bridges still needed testing.

The city council suggested that a meeting should be held next month.

It told residents that their questions would be answered there.

Several rumours about a second delay have appeared online, but so far nobody has confirmed them.

confirmed them.
