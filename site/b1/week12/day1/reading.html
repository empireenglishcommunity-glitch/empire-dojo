How Cotton Becomes a Shirt

إزاي القطن يبقى قميص

شرح مراحل تحويل القطن لقميص.

📄 Read / اقرا

The shirt you are wearing was probably made in four countries.

Here is how it happens.

First, cotton is grown in a warm country and picked, usually by machine.

Then it is cleaned in a factory and turned into thread.

The thread is dyed before it is woven, because colour is added more easily at that stage.

Next, the cloth is exported to a second factory, where it is cut by a machine that follows a design.

The pieces are sewn together by workers, and the buttons are added by hand.

Each shirt is checked before it is packed.

Finally, the shirts are sold to shops in many countries.

A label is printed with the name of the last country, so people often think the whole product was produced there.

The process is fast, but it is not simple.

When something is made this cheaply, we should ask which stage was made cheap, and who paid for it.
