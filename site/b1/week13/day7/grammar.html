📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Quantifiers and degree (too / enough / a bit / too much-many)

كلمات الكمية والدرجة (too / enough / a bit / too much-many)

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Express quantity and degree precisely: too + adj, adj + enough, both/neither/either, a bit/a lot.

التعبير الدقيق عن الكمية والدرجة: too + صفة، صفة + enough، both/neither/either، a bit/a lot.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

too + adj (+ to + verb) · adj + enough · enough + noun · too much + uncountable · too many + countable · a bit / a little + adj

[too hot to drink] // [big enough] // [enough time] // [a bit tired]

⚡ too = a problem · very = just high · enough AFTER adjectives, BEFORE nouns · too much (uncountable) / too many (countable).

too = مشكلة · very = درجة عالية · enough بعد الصفة وقبل الاسم · too much لغير المعدود و too many للمعدود.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

'too' = more than you want (a problem). 'enough' = the necessary amount: AFTER adjectives (big enough) but BEFORE nouns (enough time). 'too much' with uncountable nouns, 'too many' with countable. 'a bit / a little' softens an adjective.

'too' = أكثر من اللازم (مشكلة). 'enough' = الكمية الكافية: بعد الصفة (big enough) وقبل الاسم (enough time). 'too much' لغير المعدود و'too many' للمعدود. 'a bit / a little' تخفّف الصفة.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic uses جدًا for both 'very' and 'too', so learners say 'too good' when they mean 'very good'. They also put 'enough' before adjectives ('enough big') copying كفاية، and mix much/many because Arabic كثير covers both.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

This coffee is too hot to drink.

هذه القهوة ساخنة جدًا للشرب

[too + adj + to + verb]

The room isn't big enough.

الغرفة ليست كبيرة بما يكفي

[adj + enough]

We don't have enough time.

ليس لدينا وقت كافٍ

[enough + noun]

There were too many people there.

كان هناك أشخاص كثيرون جدًا

[too many + countable]

I'm a bit tired today.

أنا متعب قليلاً اليوم

[a bit + adj]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ The room isn't enough big.

✅ The room isn't big enough.

'enough' goes AFTER an adjective but before a noun.

❌ The film was too good!

✅ The film was very good!

'too' means there is a problem; for praise use 'very'.

❌ There was too many traffic.

✅ There was too much traffic.

'traffic' is uncountable → too much, not too many.

✍️ Practice / تمرين

Fill in the blank. Completing all of them marks this exercise done. / املا الفراغ. لما تخلص كلهم التمرين يتحسب.

This tea is ___ hot to drink.

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

Adjective first, then 'enough'. Noun last, after 'enough'. 'too' warns, 'very' praises.

🎙️ Use it when you speak / استخدمها وانت بتتكلم

In accent week 13 you practised strong 'too' /tuː/ vs weak 'to' /tə/ and 'enough' stress. Say: 'too TIRED tə WALK' — first word strong, second weak — and 'big eNOUGH' with second-syllable stress.
