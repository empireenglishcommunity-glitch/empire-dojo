🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

What is the teacher's main message?

إيه رسالة المدرّسة الأساسية؟

English is mainly useful for getting a better job
Students should have studied harder during the course
Practice will stop unless they decide now what will remind them

❓ Question / سؤال 1

Why does she say the students really kept going?

ليه قالت إن الطلبة كمّلوا فعلًا؟

They started enjoying being understood
They wanted a better job
Their teacher pushed them

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What does she recommend about the Friday meetings?

نصحت بإيه بخصوص لقاءات الجمعة؟

Stop them once attendance falls
Keep them even when only two people come
Replace them with daily meetings

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What does she say about waiting to feel ready?

قالت إيه عن الانتظار لحد ما تحس إنك جاهز؟

Nobody feels ready, so do not wait for it
Wait until you are genuinely confident
You should have felt ready by week one

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Teacher
This is the last morning, so I want to say one thing and then let you go. When you started, most of you wanted to learn English in order to get a better job. That is a good reason, and although it worked for some of you, it is not the reason you kept going. You kept going because you started enjoying being understood. That is a different thing, and it is much harder to lose. A warning, however. In three weeks you will stop practising. Not because you decide to, but because nothing will remind you. So decide now what will remind you. Some of you have agreed to meet on Fridays, and I would recommend keeping that, even when only two people come. Others plan to read for ten minutes a night, which sounds too small to matter and is in fact the only size that survives. Finally, avoid waiting to feel ready before you speak. Nobody feels ready. You did not feel ready in week one either, and yet here you are, understanding every word I am saying.
