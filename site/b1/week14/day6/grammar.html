📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Gerunds and infinitives + linkers (although / however) — B1 review

المصدر الاسمي والمصدر + أدوات الربط (although / however) — مراجعة B1

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Verb + -ing vs verb + to; and link ideas with although, however, because of, so that; consolidate all B1.

الفعل + -ing مقابل الفعل + to؛ وربط الأفكار بـ although وhowever وbecause of وso that؛ ومراجعة كل B1.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

verb + -ing (enjoy, finish, keep, avoid, mind) · verb + to + verb (want, need, decide, hope, plan) · although + clause (one sentence) · However, + sentence (two sentences)

[I enjoy reading] // [I want to read] // [Although it rained, we went] // [It rained. However, we went.]

⚡ enjoy/finish/keep/avoid → -ing · want/need/decide/hope → to + verb · although inside one sentence · However, between two sentences.

enjoy/finish/keep/avoid → -ing · want/need/decide/hope → to + فعل · although داخل جملة · However, بين جملتين.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Choose '-ing' after verbs like enjoy, finish, keep, avoid, mind, suggest; choose 'to + verb' after want, need, decide, hope, plan, promise, learn. Use 'although' to join a contrast INSIDE one sentence, and 'However,' to link two SEPARATE sentences.

استخدم '-ing' بعد أفعال مثل enjoy, finish, keep, avoid؛ و'to + فعل' بعد want, need, decide, hope, plan. 'although' تربط تضادًا داخل جملة واحدة، و'However,' تربط جملتين منفصلتين.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic uses أن + المضارع or المصدر for both patterns, so learners say 'I enjoy to read' or 'I want reading'. With linkers they double them ('Although ... but ...') copying مع أن ... لكن، and they forget the comma after 'however'.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

I enjoy reading before bed.

أستمتع بالقراءة قبل النوم

[enjoy + -ing]

I want to read this book.

أريد قراءة هذا الكتاب

[want + to + verb]

He stopped smoking last year.

أقلع عن التدخين العام الماضي

[stop + -ing] (quit)

Although it rained, we went out.

رغم أنها أمطرت، خرجنا

Although + [clause], [clause]

It was expensive. However, I bought it.

كان غاليًا. لكنني اشتريته

[sentence]. However, [sentence].

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ I enjoy to read.

✅ I enjoy reading.

'enjoy' is always followed by the -ing form.

❌ Although it rained, but we went out.

✅ Although it rained, we went out.

Use 'although' OR 'but', never both in one sentence.

❌ It was expensive, however I bought it.

✅ It was expensive. However, I bought it.

'However' starts a new sentence and takes a comma after it.

✍️ Practice / تمرين

Fill in the blank. Completing all of them marks this exercise done. / املا الفراغ. لما تخلص كلهم التمرين يتحسب.

I've finished ___ (read) that book.

She decided ___ (leave) early.

Keep ___ (try) and you'll improve.

___ he studied hard, he didn't pass.

The hotel was full. ___, we found a room nearby.

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

Feelings and finishing take -ing; wanting and planning take 'to'. Although joins, However separates.

🎙️ Use it when you speak / استخدمها وانت بتتكلم

In accent week 14 you practised clear '-ing' /ŋ/, weak 'to' /tə/, voiced 'although' /ð/, and the comma pause after 'however'. Say: 'I want tə go' and 'However, // I bought it.' This completes B1 — you are ready for B2.
