What I Learned From Failing

اللي اتعلمته من الفشل

تأمّل شخصي عن التقدم والفشل في تعلّم الإنجليزي.

📄 Read / اقرا

When I decided to learn English properly, I expected to enjoy it.

In fact, I spent the first six months avoiding it.

I kept making the same mistake: I wanted to speak perfectly, so I said nothing.

Although I understood the grammar, I avoided every conversation.

However, saying nothing is not safe — it is just slow.

The change came because of a small decision.

I managed to agree with a colleague that we would speak English for ten minutes every morning, despite how bad it felt.

In order to keep going, we made one rule: mistakes were allowed, silence was not.

After three months I noticed that I had stopped translating in my head.

My confidence grew, although my grammar was still far from perfect.

My advice is simple.

Don't hope to be ready; practise so that you become ready.

The goal is not to avoid mistakes.

The goal is to make them early, in front of someone kind.

of someone kind.

Practise so that you become ready, don't wait to be ready
