📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Past continuous + past simple (interrupted actions)

الماضي المستمر + الماضي البسيط (فعل يقطع فعلاً)

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Background action in progress interrupted by a completed action; when vs while.

حدث مستمر في الخلفية قاطعه حدث منتهٍ؛ when مقابل while.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

was/were + -ing (background/longer action) · past simple (interruption) · when / while

[I was cooking] + [when] + [the phone rang]

⚡ Long background action = was/were + -ing. Short interruption = past simple. while + long, when + short.

الفعل الطويل الخلفي = was/were + -ing. المقاطعة القصيرة = ماضٍ بسيط. while + الطويل، when + القصير.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Past continuous (was/were + -ing) for a longer background action in progress; past simple for a shorter action that interrupts it. Use 'while' before the long action and 'when' before the short one.

الماضي المستمر (was/were + -ing) لفعل طويل مستمر في الخلفية؛ الماضي البسيط لفعل قصير يقطعه. 'while' قبل الطويل و'when' قبل القصير.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic often uses 'كان + يفعل' but learners forget the was/were auxiliary in English ('I cooking') and mix up when/while, or use past simple for both actions so the sequence is unclear.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

I was cooking when the phone rang.

كنت أطبخ عندما رنّ الهاتف

[was cooking] + when + [rang]

While she was studying, the lights went out.

بينما كانت تذاكر، انطفأت الأنوار

While + [was studying], [went out]

They were playing football when it started to rain.

كانوا يلعبون كرة عندما بدأت تمطر

[were playing] + when + [started]

What were you doing at 8 pm?

ماذا كنت تفعل الساعة الثامنة مساءً؟

[were + doing]?

He was driving while she was sleeping.

كان يقود بينما كانت نائمة

[was driving] + while + [was sleeping]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ I cooking when he called.

✅ I was cooking when he called.

Continuous needs was/were + -ing.

❌ When I was walking, I was seeing an accident.

✅ While I was walking, I saw an accident.

The short interrupting action is past simple, not continuous.

❌ While the phone rang, I cooked.

✅ While I was cooking, the phone rang.

'while' goes with the longer -ing action.

✍️ Practice / تمرين

Fill in the blank. Completing all of them marks this exercise done. / املا الفراغ. لما تخلص كلهم التمرين يتحسب.

I ___ (watch) TV when you called.

While they ___ (drive), it started to snow.

She ___ (cook) when the guests arrived.

What ___ you ___ (do) at midnight?

The lights went out while we ___ (eat).

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

Picture the scene (-ing) → then something happens (past simple).

🎙️ Use it when you speak / استخدمها وانت بتتكلم

In accent week 2 you practised -ing narration and weak was/were. Say: 'I wəz cooking when the phone rang' — light 'was', clear '-ing', then the sharp past-simple 'rang'.
