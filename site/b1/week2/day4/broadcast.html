🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Why is he telling this story?

بيحكي القصة دي ليه؟

To complain about the building's fire alarm
To explain why he now saves his work constantly
To describe the fire drill they practised last year

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What was he doing when the alarm went off?

كان بيعمل إيه لما الإنذار رنّ؟

Finishing the monthly report
Making toast
Waiting in the car park

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What caused the alarm?

إيه اللي سبّب الإنذار؟

A real fire on the third floor
Someone making toast
A practice drill

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What did he actually lose?

إيه اللي ضاع منه فعلًا؟

His laptop
His coffee
About forty minutes of work

🔒 Answer the main-point question above to unlock the detail questions. / جاوب على سؤال الفكرة الأساسية فوق عشان تفتح أسئلة التفاصيل.

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Colleague
So, you asked what happened on Tuesday. It was actually quite funny, although it did not feel funny at the time. I was sitting at my desk, finishing the monthly report, when the fire alarm went off. Everybody stood up, and we all walked down to the car park, the way we practised last year. While we were waiting outside, I realised that I was still holding my coffee, but I had left my laptop upstairs, unlocked, with the report open on the screen. I was thinking about running back up when the building manager told us it was a false alarm. Someone was making toast on the third floor. Anyway, when I got back to my desk, my laptop had gone to sleep and the report had not saved. I lost about forty minutes of work. So now I press save every five minutes, and I do not make toast at work.
