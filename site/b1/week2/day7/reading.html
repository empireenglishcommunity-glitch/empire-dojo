The Night the Lights Went Out

الليلة اللي النور فيها قطع

حكاية عن ليلة قطع فيها النور والعيلة اتجمعت.

📄 Read / اقرا

It was a normal Thursday evening.

My mother was cooking, my father was reading the newspaper, and I was waiting for a message from my friend.

Suddenly, at nine o'clock, all the lights went out.

At that moment nobody moved.

Then my father laughed and said, 'Well, we still have candles.'

While my mother was looking for them, I noticed something strange: the whole street was dark, not only our building.

We sat together in the kitchen with two candles.

My father started telling stories about his childhood, and my mother remembered the day she arrived in this city.

I realised that we had not talked like that for months.

The lights came back at midnight.

Everybody stood up and went to their rooms, and the evening ended.

But I still think about that night — the night we lost the electricity and found each other again.
