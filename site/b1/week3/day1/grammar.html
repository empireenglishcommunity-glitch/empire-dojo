Future forms: will / going to / present continuous

صيغ المستقبل: will / going to / المضارع المستمر

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Choose between predictions/decisions (will), plans/intentions (going to) and fixed arrangements (present continuous).

الاختيار بين التوقعات/القرارات (will) والخطط (going to) والترتيبات المؤكدة (present continuous).

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

will + verb (decision now/prediction) · be going to + verb (plan/evidence) · am/is/are + -ing (arrangement)

[I'll help] // [I'm going to study] // [I'm meeting Sara at 6]

⚡ Decide now/predict → will. Plan or evidence → going to. Fixed arrangement (time+place) → present continuous.

قرار الآن/توقّع → will. خطة أو دليل → going to. ترتيب ثابت (وقت+مكان) → مضارع مستمر.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

will: instant decisions and predictions ('I'll get it', 'It'll rain'). going to: plans and predictions from evidence ('I'm going to study medicine', 'Look — it's going to rain'). Present continuous: fixed arrangements with a time/place ('I'm meeting Sara at six').

will: قرار لحظي وتوقّع. going to: خطة وتوقّع من دليل. المضارع المستمر: ترتيب ثابت بوقت/مكان.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic marks the future mainly with سـ/سوف, so learners use 'will' for everything and rarely use 'going to' for plans or the present continuous for arrangements. They also say 'I will meeting' or 'I going to'.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

The phone's ringing — I'll get it.

الهاتف يرنّ — سأردّ أنا

[will + verb] (instant decision)

I'm going to study medicine.

سأدرس الطب (خطة)

[be going to + verb] (plan)

Look at those clouds — it's going to rain.

انظر للغيوم — ستمطر (دليل)

[going to] (evidence)

I'm meeting Sara at six tonight.

سأقابل سارة السادسة الليلة (ترتيب)

[am + -ing] (arrangement)

I think our team will win.

أعتقد أن فريقنا سيفوز (توقّع)

[will] (prediction)

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ I will study medicine (a long-decided plan).

✅ I'm going to study medicine.

A plan you already decided → going to, not a spur-of-the-moment will.

❌ I will meet Sara at six (fixed arrangement).

✅ I'm meeting Sara at six.

Fixed arrangement with a time → present continuous.

❌ Look, it will rain now.

✅ Look, it's going to rain.

Prediction from present evidence → going to.

Just decided → 'll. Already planned → going to. In my diary → -ing.
