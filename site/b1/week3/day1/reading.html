A Change of Plan

تغيير في الخطة

إيميل بيغيّر معاد اجتماع ويوضّح الجدول الجديد.

📄 Read / اقرا

Dear team, I am writing to change the arrangements for next week.

We were going to hold the meeting on Tuesday, but two colleagues are travelling that day, so I have decided to postpone it.

The new schedule is: the meeting is taking place on Thursday at ten o'clock in Room 4.

It will last ninety minutes.

I am going to send the documents on Wednesday morning, so please read them before you arrive.

If you cannot come on Thursday, tell me before Monday and I will arrange a short call instead.

I expect that we will finish the main decisions in one meeting, but if we need more time, we will organise a second meeting in two weeks.

Please note the deadline for the report has not changed: it is still the end of the month.

I promise I will not cancel anything again!

hing again! Best wishes, Hanan
