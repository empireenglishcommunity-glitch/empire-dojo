📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Manager
Before you go, here is next week, so nobody is surprised. On Monday the auditors are coming at nine. That is arranged, it is in the calendar, and they are staying until Wednesday. While they are here, please do not move any files out of the shared folder. On Tuesday I am meeting the two new suppliers, so I will not be at my desk in the morning. If you need me, send a message and I will answer between meetings. Wednesday is the big one: we are presenting the quarter figures at two o'clock, and I am going to ask Yusuf to open, because he built the numbers. Thursday will probably be quiet, so that is a good day to finish anything the auditors ask for. And on Friday the office is closing at one, because the lift is being repaired in the afternoon. One last thing. I am not going to send a reminder about the timesheets again. They are due Thursday. Please just do them.
