What is the speaker's main purpose?

إيه هدف المتكلم الأساسي؟

To explain what will happen if students break the rules
To describe how the exam will be marked
To tell students what to revise tonight

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What happens if a student arrives forty minutes late?

إيه اللي يحصل لو الطالب جه متأخر أربعين دقيقة؟

They get extra time at the end
They will not be allowed into the hall
They must sit at the front

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What is the rule about phones?

إيه القاعدة بخصوص الموبايل؟

Any sound ends the exam, with no warning
One warning is given first
Phones must be switched off but may stay in bags

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What should a student do if they feel unwell?

الطالب يعمل إيه لو حس إنه تعبان؟

Leave the hall quietly
Raise their hand and wait
Take the paper to the front

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Examiner
Right, listen carefully, because these rules decide whether you sit the exam or not. If you arrive more than thirty minutes late, you will not be allowed into the hall. If you arrive before that, you can come in, but you will not get extra time. As soon as you sit down, put your identity card on the desk, face up. If an invigilator cannot see your card, they will stop and ask you, and that wastes your time, not theirs. Phones: if your phone makes any sound, even inside your bag, the exam is over for you. There is no warning and there is no second chance. When you finish, stay in your seat until an invigilator collects your paper. If you stand up and walk to the front, your paper may be counted as incomplete. Finally, if you feel unwell during the exam, raise your hand and wait. Do not leave the hall on your own, because once you go through that door you cannot come back in.
