If I Had the Chance

لو كانت عندي الفرصة

رسالة عن أحلام وأمنيات لو الظروف اتغيرت.

📄 Read / اقرا

Dear Mona, You asked me a difficult question: what would I do if I had the chance to change my life?

I have been thinking about it all week.

If I were a millionaire, I don't think I would stop working.

I would probably invest some of the money in a small school, because I would like to give other people the opportunity that I did not have.

I would donate a part of it too, but I wouldn't tell anybody.

If I could travel anywhere tomorrow, I would choose Japan.

I wish I spoke Japanese!

I imagine walking in a quiet street there in spring.

The honest answer, though, is that I would change very little.

If I quit my job and spent every day travelling, I think I would miss my students.

Sometimes we dream about a different life, and then we discover that we would choose the same one again.

What about you?

What would you do?

at would you do? Layla
