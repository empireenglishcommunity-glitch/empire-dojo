What Would You Do? — A Radio Phone-In

👂 Before you listen / قبل ما تسمع

A radio phone-in with two callers. They reach the same decision for very different reasons. Listen for what they AGREE on.

برنامج مداخلات في الراديو مع متصلين. الاتنين وصلوا لنفس القرار بأسباب مختلفة. ركّز على إيه اللي متفقين عليه.

🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

What do the two callers agree about?

إيه اللي المتصلين الاتنين متفقين عليه؟

That they would take the bag to the police
That everyone who keeps the money is a bad person
That they would keep the money if the rent was due

❓ Question / سؤال 1

Why does Hisham say he would return it?

Because a stranger once returned his father's wallet
Because he is afraid of the police
Because he has never needed money

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What does Dina admit?

دينا اعترفت بإيه؟

That she would keep the money
That she would think about it first
That she has kept money before

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What does Dina say would help?

دينا قالت إيه اللي هيساعد؟

Stronger punishments
Talking about money more openly
More police on the streets

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Host
Good afternoon. Today's question is simple. If you found a bag with a lot of money in it, what would you do? The lines are open. First caller, you are on.
Hisham
Hello. I would take it straight to the police. I know some people would keep it, but I would not sleep. If it were my bag, I would want somebody to do the same for me. My father lost his wallet in a taxi twenty years ago, and a stranger returned it with everything inside. If that had not happened, I think I would be a different person today.
Dina
I would take it to the police too, but I want to be honest: I would think about it first. If I had no money for the rent that month, it would be much harder. I do not think people who keep it are all bad. I think they are frightened. If we talked about money more openly, fewer people would be in that situation.
Host
Two answers, and neither of them is as simple as it sounds. More after the news.
