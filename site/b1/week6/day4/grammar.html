Modals of deduction (must / might / could / can't)

أفعال الاستنتاج (must / might / could / can't)

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Degrees of certainty about the present: must (sure), might/may/could (possible), can't (impossible).

درجات اليقين عن الحاضر: must (متأكد)، might/may/could (ممكن)، can't (مستحيل).

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

must + verb (sure it's true) · might / may / could + verb (possible) · can't + verb (sure it's false)

[He must be tired] // [She might be at work] // [That can't be right]

⚡ Sure yes → must. Maybe → might/may/could. Sure no → can't. Always + bare verb (no 'to').

متأكد نعم → must. ربما → might/may/could. متأكد لا → can't. دائمًا + فعل مجرد بدون to.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

To say how CERTAIN you are about a present situation, based on evidence. must = I'm sure it's true. might/may/could = it's possible. can't = I'm sure it's not true. All are followed by the bare infinitive (no 'to').

للتعبير عن درجة اليقين في موقف حاضر بناءً على دليل. must = متأكد أنه صحيح. might/may/could = ممكن. can't = متأكد أنه غير صحيح. كلها + الفعل المجرد بدون to.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic expresses certainty with separate words (لا بد أن، ربما، مستحيل), so learners add 'to' after the modal ('must to be') or use 'mustn't' for impossibility instead of 'can't'.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

He's been working all day — he must be tired.

يعمل طوال اليوم — لا بد أنه متعب

[must + be] (certain true)

She might be at work now.

ربما تكون في العمل الآن

[might + be] (possible)

That can't be right — check it again.

مستحيل أن يكون هذا صحيحًا

[can't + be] (certain false)

The keys could be in the car.

قد تكون المفاتيح في السيارة

[could + be] (possible)

They must know each other well.

لا بد أنهما يعرفان بعضهما جيدًا

[must + know]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ He must to be tired.

✅ He must be tired.

Modals take the bare infinitive — no 'to'.

❌ That mustn't be right.

✅ That can't be right.

For 'I'm sure it's NOT true' we use can't, not mustn't.

❌ She maybe is at work.

✅ She might be at work.

'maybe' is an adverb; the modal pattern is might + verb.

✍️ Practice / تمرين

Fill in the blank. Completing all of them marks this exercise done. / املا الفراغ. لما تخلص كلهم التمرين يتحسب.

The lights are off. They ___ (be) asleep. (I'm sure)

I'm not certain, but she ___ (be) in the library. (possible)

That ___ (be) his brother — he's an only child.

He speaks perfect French. He ___ (have) lived in Paris.

It ___ (rain) later, so take an umbrella. (possible)

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

must = 100% yes · might = 50% · can't = 100% no.
