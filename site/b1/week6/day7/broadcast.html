The Empty House

البيت الفاضي

👂 Before you listen / قبل ما تسمع

A television documentary about an empty house. The reporter is not certain of anything. Listen for the one thing she says CANNOT be true.

برنامج وثائقي عن بيت فاضي. المراسلة مش متأكدة من حاجة. ركّز على الحاجة الوحيدة اللي قالت إنها مستحيلة.

🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

What is the programme's main point about the house?

إيه الفكرة الأساسية في البرنامج عن البيت؟

It has been completely abandoned for ten years
Somebody is clearly looking after it, but nobody knows who
The neighbours know the owner and will not say

❓ Question / سؤال 1

Why does the reporter say the house cannot be abandoned?

ليه المراسلة قالت إن البيت مش ممكن يكون متروك؟

The garden is cut regularly
The taxes are paid every year
A family sleeps there in the evening

❓ Question / سؤال 2

How often do the neighbours say the car comes?

الجيران قالوا العربية بتيجي كل قد إيه؟

Twice a year
Every evening
Twice a month

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What does the reporter conclude about the woman?

المراسلة وصلت لإيه بخصوص الست؟

She is definitely the owner
She might be the owner, a lawyer, or someone asked to check
She is one of the neighbours

🔒 Answer the main-point question above to unlock the detail questions. / جاوب على سؤال الفكرة الأساسية فوق عشان تفتح أسئلة التفاصيل.

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Reporter
Every city has a house like this one. It stands at the end of a quiet street, the windows are covered, and the garden has not been cut for at least ten years. Nobody in the neighbourhood can tell us who owns it. It must belong to somebody: the taxes are paid every year, on time, from a bank in another city. So the owner might be living abroad, or the house could be part of an old family argument that no lawyer has been able to finish. What is certain is that it cannot be abandoned, because abandoned houses do not pay their taxes. Neighbours say a car comes twice a year, always in the evening. A woman gets out, stays for about an hour, and leaves. She might be the owner. She may be a lawyer. She could simply be someone who was asked, a long time ago, to check that the roof is still there. We knocked on the door for three days. Nobody answered, and yet somebody, somewhere, is still paying.
