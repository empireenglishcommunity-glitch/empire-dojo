🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

How did the guest's business begin?

شغل الضيف بدأ إزاي؟

He trained as a bicycle mechanic at college
He planned it carefully while working in the office
A neighbour asked him to fix her bicycle

❓ Question / سؤال 1

How long had he worked in an office?

قعد قد إيه بيشتغل في مكتب؟

Six years
Nine years
Eighteen months

❓ Question / سؤال 2

Which part of the work does he enjoy most now?

أنهي جزء من الشغل بيستمتع بيه أكتر دلوقتي؟

Working late until midnight
Teaching the Saturday class
Repairing his own bicycle

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What does he say about bicycles?

قال إيه عن العجل؟

They are complicated machines
They have barely changed and want to be understood
They should be treated with respect

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Host
You have been repairing bicycles in the same shop for nine years. How did it start?
Guest
By accident. I had been working in an office for six years, and I hated it. I had been fixing my own bike since I was a boy, and one evening a neighbour asked me to look at hers. Then her brother came. Then his friends. I have been doing it ever since. For the first two years I was still in the office, so I was repairing bikes until midnight and going to work tired. I have been teaching a class on Saturdays for about eighteen months now, and that is the part I enjoy most. People arrive thinking a bicycle is complicated. It is not. It has been more or less the same machine for a hundred years, and it wants to be understood, not respected.
Host
And the office? Do you ever miss it?
Guest
I have not thought about the office for years. That is the answer.
