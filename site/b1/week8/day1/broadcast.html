What is the main point of this programme?

The street was better in every way in the past

What does Mrs Aziza say she does NOT miss?

The noise of the street
Selling vegetables in the cold
Sitting outside with the neighbours

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Presenter
In nineteen eighty-five, this street had four bakeries. Today it has one, and it opens at six because the man who runs it still cannot sleep past five. People here will tell you that things used to be better, and then, if you sit with them long enough, they will tell you something more honest. Mrs Aziza used to sell vegetables from a table outside her door. She would start at four in the morning, and in winter her hands would crack. She does not miss that. What she misses is the noise: the children who used to play in the road because there were almost no cars, and the neighbours who would sit outside until midnight in summer because nobody had air conditioning. Her grandson has a good job and a flat with cold air and a lock on the door, and she is proud of him. But he does not know the names of the people upstairs. She used to know everybody in three streets. That is the thing nobody planned to lose.
