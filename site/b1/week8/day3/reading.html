Letters From My Grandmother's Village

رسائل من قرية جدتي

ذكريات جدتي عن الماضي ومقارنتها بالحاضر.

📄 Read / اقرا

Dear Amina, You asked me what life used to be like in the village when I was young.

Back then, everything was slower and, in some ways, simpler.

We used to wake up before the sun.

My mother would bake bread every morning, and the smell would fill the whole house.

We didn't use to have a fridge, so we bought food on the same day we ate it.

In the evening the neighbours would sit outside together, and the children would play in the street until somebody called us in.

I won't pretend it was all beautiful.

We used to walk four kilometres to school, and in winter that walk was hard.

There was no hospital nearby.

Nowadays my grandchildren have everything I dreamed of, and I am glad.

But I do miss one thing: back then we knew every family in the street.

These days people live closer together and know each other less.

ch other less. Your grandmother
