City or Village: Which Life Is Better?

مدينة ولا قرية: أنهي حياة أحسن؟

مقال بيقارن المدينة والقرية وبيوصل لخلاصة.

📄 Read / اقرا

Every year thousands of young people move from villages to cities.

Are they right?

On one hand, the city offers far more opportunities.

Salaries are usually higher, and public transport is much more convenient.

A young engineer in a city has slightly better chances than an equally talented one in a village.

On the other hand, the advantages are not as large as they seem.

City rent is not nearly as affordable as village rent, so a bigger salary does not always mean more money at the end of the month.

The city is also far more crowded and a bit less healthy, and it is certainly not as peaceful.

Some people argue that the two ways of life are becoming similar to each other, because the internet has brought city work to the village.

That is true for a few jobs, but not for most.

So what is the conclusion?

Neither place is better in general.

The city is better for starting a career; the village is better for raising a family.

The right question is not 'which is better?'

but 'better for what?'

'better for what?'
