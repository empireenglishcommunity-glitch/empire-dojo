📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Modals of past deduction (must/might/can't have + participle)

أفعال الاستنتاج في الماضي (must/might/can't have + التصريف الثالث)

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Make deductions about the past: must have (certain), might/could have (possible), can't have (impossible).

استنتاجات عن الماضي: must have (متأكد)، might/could have (ممكن)، can't have (مستحيل).

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

must have + participle (sure it happened) · might/may/could have + participle (possible) · can't/couldn't have + participle (impossible)

[He must have forgotten] // [She might have missed it] // [It can't have been him]

⚡ must have (sure) · might/may/could have (maybe) · can't/couldn't have (impossible). Always + past participle. Never 'mustn't have' for impossibility.

must have (متأكد) · might/could have (ربما) · can't have (مستحيل). دائمًا + التصريف الثالث. ولا تستخدم mustn't have للاستحالة.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

To make deductions about the PAST based on present evidence. must have = I'm sure it happened. might/may/could have = it possibly happened. can't/couldn't have = it's impossible. All take 'have + past participle'.

للاستنتاج عن الماضي بناءً على دليل حاضر. must have متأكد، might/may/could have ممكن، can't/couldn't have مستحيل. كلها + have + التصريف الثالث.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Learners confuse 'mustn't have' with "can't have" — in English 'mustn't' means prohibition, never impossibility. They also write 'must of' because that is exactly how /mʌstəv/ sounds, and they mix this up with the regret meaning of 'should have'.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

He must have forgotten the meeting.

لا بد أنه نسي الاجتماع

[must have + participle] (certain)

She might have missed the train.

ربما فوّتت القطار

[might have + participle] (possible)

It can't have been him; he was abroad.

مستحيل أن يكون هو؛ كان بالخارج

[can't have been] (impossible)

They couldn't have finished already.

مستحيل أن يكونوا انتهوا بالفعل

[couldn't have + participle]

The lights are off, so they must have gone out.

الأنوار مطفأة، فلا بد أنهم خرجوا

evidence + so + [must have]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ It mustn't have been him.

✅ It can't have been him.

For impossibility use "can't have"; 'mustn't' means prohibition.

❌ He must of forgotten.

✅ He must have forgotten. / He must've forgotten.

'must've' is 'must have'; 'must of' is never correct in writing.

❌ He must forgot the meeting.

✅ He must have forgotten the meeting.

Past deduction needs 'have + past participle' after the modal.

✍️ Practice / تمرين

Fill in the blank. Completing all of them marks this exercise done. / املا الفراغ. لما تخلص كلهم التمرين يتحسب.

He ___ (must / forget) the meeting.

She ___ (might / miss) the train.

It ___ (can't / be) him; he was abroad.

They ___ (couldn't / finish) already.

The lights are off, so they ___ (must / go) out.

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

Sure → must've · Maybe → might've · Impossible → can't have (NOT mustn't have).

🎙️ Use it when you speak / استخدمها وانت بتتكلم

In accent week 10 you drilled /mʌstəv/, /maɪtəv/ and /kɑːntəv/ with three different confidence tones. Your certainty must be audible — the same words said flat lose the meaning.
