💬 This Week's Pattern / نمط الأسبوع

Relative clauses: non-defining and reduced

جُمل الوصل: غير المحدِّدة والمختصرة

noun, who/which + clause, (non-defining, commas) · noun + who/that + clause (defining, no commas) · noun + -ing / -ed (reduced) · ..., which + comment · preposition + whom/which

📍 Non-defining clauses add EXTRA information, take commas, and cannot use 'that'. Defining clauses identify which one and take no commas. Reduced relatives drop 'who is'/'that was' and leave just -ing (active) or -ed (passive). 'which' can also comment on a whole clause.

💡 "My brother, who lives in Dubai, is visiting us."
