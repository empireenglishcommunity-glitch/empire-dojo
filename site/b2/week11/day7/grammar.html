Relative clauses: non-defining and reduced

جُمل الوصل: غير المحدِّدة والمختصرة

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Add extra (non-essential) information with non-defining clauses (commas); and reduce relatives to participles.

إضافة معلومات إضافية بجُمَل الوصل غير التعريفية (بفواصل)؛ واختصار جُمَل الوصل إلى مشتقّات.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

noun, who/which + clause, (non-defining, commas) · noun + who/that + clause (defining, no commas) · noun + -ing / -ed (reduced) · ..., which + comment · preposition + whom/which

[My brother, who lives in Dubai, is visiting] // [the man standing there]

⚡ Extra info → commas + who/which (never 'that'). Essential info → no commas. Reduced → drop 'who is', keep -ing (active) or -ed (passive). Comment on a clause → 'which'.

معلومة إضافية → فواصل + who/which (لا that). معلومة أساسية → بدون فواصل. المختصرة → احذف who is وأبقِ -ing أو -ed.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Non-defining clauses add EXTRA information, take commas, and cannot use 'that'. Defining clauses identify which one and take no commas. Reduced relatives drop 'who is'/'that was' and leave just -ing (active) or -ed (passive). 'which' can also comment on a whole clause.

غير المحدِّدة تضيف معلومة إضافية بفواصل ولا تستخدم that. والمحدِّدة تحدّد أيّهم بدون فواصل. والمختصرة تحذف who is وتترك -ing أو -ed. و'which' يمكنها التعليق على الجملة كلها.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic relative clauses repeat the object pronoun (الرجل الذي رأيته), producing 'the man who I saw him'. Arabic also has no comma-based distinction between essential and extra information, so learners add or omit commas randomly, which changes the meaning in English.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

My brother, who lives in Dubai, is visiting us.

أخي، الذي يسكن في دبي، سيزورنا (إضافة)

[noun], who ..., (non-defining)

The man who lives next door is a doctor.

الرجل الذي يسكن بالجوار طبيب (تحديد)

[noun] + who ... (defining)

The man standing over there is my uncle.

الرجل الواقف هناك عمّي (مختصرة)

[noun] + -ing (reduced active)

He arrived late, which annoyed everyone.

وصل متأخرًا، وهذا أزعج الجميع

..., which + [comment on whole clause]

The person to whom I spoke was helpful.

الشخص الذي تحدثت إليه كان مفيدًا (رسمي)

[preposition + whom] (formal)

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ The man who I saw him was tall.

✅ The man who I saw was tall.

Don't repeat the object pronoun after the relative pronoun.

❌ My brother, that lives in Dubai, is visiting.

✅ My brother, who lives in Dubai, is visiting.

'that' cannot be used in non-defining clauses.

❌ He arrived late, that annoyed everyone.

✅ He arrived late, which annoyed everyone.

Use 'which' to comment on a whole clause, never 'that'.

✍️ Practice / تمرين

Fill in the blank. Completing all of them marks this exercise done. / املا الفراغ. لما تخلص كلهم التمرين يتحسب.

My brother, ___ lives in Dubai, is visiting.

The man ___ lives next door is a doctor.

He arrived late, ___ annoyed everyone.

The man ___ (stand) over there is my uncle. (reduced)

The letter ___ (send) yesterday hasn't arrived. (reduced)

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

Commas = extra, no commas = essential. 'that' only lives in defining clauses.

🎙️ Use it when you speak / استخدمها وانت بتتكلم

In accent week 11 you drilled the parenthetical pitch drop. Non-defining clauses must be SPOKEN as a parenthesis — pause, drop, pause — or your listener hears a defining clause and a different meaning.
