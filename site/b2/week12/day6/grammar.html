Gerunds and infinitives: advanced patterns and meaning change

المصدر الاسمي والمصدر: أنماط متقدمة وتغيّر المعنى

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Master verb patterns and verbs where -ing vs to changes meaning (remember, stop, try, regret, go on).

إتقان أنماط الأفعال والأفعال اللي يتغيّر معناها بين -ing و to.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

remember/forget/stop/go on/try + -ing vs to (meaning changes) · to have + participle (perfect infinitive) · to be + participle (passive infinitive) · not to / not -ing

[stop smoking] = quit // [stop to smoke] = pause in order to smoke

⚡ -ing looks BACK (memory, ongoing, quitting). 'to' looks FORWARD (duty, purpose, attempt). 'to have done' = earlier. 'to be done' = passive.

-ing تنظر للخلف (ذكرى، استمرار، إقلاع). 'to' تنظر للأمام (واجب، غرض، محاولة). 'to have done' أقدم. 'to be done' مجهول.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

With some verbs the choice CHANGES THE MEANING: remember/forget + -ing = a memory; + to = a duty. stop + -ing = quit; + to = pause in order to. go on + -ing = continue; + to = move to a new topic. try + -ing = experiment; + to = attempt. Also: 'to have done' for an earlier action, 'to be done' for passive.

مع بعض الأفعال يغيّر الاختيار المعنى: remember + -ing ذكرى و+ to واجب؛ stop + -ing يقلع و+ to يتوقف ليفعل؛ go on + -ing يستمر و+ to ينتقل.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

At A2/B1 learners memorise which verbs take which form. At B2 that strategy breaks down, because BOTH forms are grammatical and the choice carries the meaning. Learners produce perfectly formed sentences that say the opposite of what they intended.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

He stopped smoking last year.

أقلع عن التدخين العام الماضي

[stop + -ing] = quit

We stopped to rest halfway.

توقفنا لنرتاح في المنتصف

[stop + to] = pause in order to

I remember locking the door.

أتذكر أنني أغلقت الباب (ذكرى)

[remember + -ing] = memory

Remember to lock the door.

تذكّر أن تغلق الباب (واجب)

[remember + to] = duty

He seems to have forgotten our meeting.

يبدو أنه نسي اجتماعنا

[to have + participle] (earlier action)

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ He stopped to smoke last year. (meaning he quit)

✅ He stopped smoking last year.

'stop to smoke' means he paused in order to smoke — the opposite meaning.

❌ Remember locking the door tonight. (as an instruction)

✅ Remember to lock the door tonight.

A future duty takes 'to'; '-ing' refers to a past memory.

❌ He seems to forgot our meeting.

✅ He seems to have forgotten our meeting.

For an action earlier than the main verb, use the perfect infinitive.

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها
