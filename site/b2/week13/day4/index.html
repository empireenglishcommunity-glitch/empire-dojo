💬 This Week's Pattern / نمط الأسبوع

Determiners, articles and quantifiers (advanced)

المحدِّدات والأدوات وكلمات الكمية (متقدّم)

a/an (first mention) · the (known/specific) · zero article (general plural & uncountable) · most/most of the · few vs a few · little vs a little · either/neither/both

📍 Zero article for general plurals and uncountables ('Life is hard', 'I like music'). 'the' when the noun is specific or already known. 'most people' (general) but 'most of the people here' (specific group). 'few' = not many (negative); 'a few' = some (positive). Same for 'little' and 'a little'.

💡 "Life is hard, but people are kind."
