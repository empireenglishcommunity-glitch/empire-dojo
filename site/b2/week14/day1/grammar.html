📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Linking and discourse: contrast, concession, cause and result

الربط والخطاب: التضاد والتنازل والسبب والنتيجة

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Connect ideas formally: despite/in spite of, although/even though, however/nevertheless, therefore/as a result.

ربط الأفكار بشكل رسمي: despite/in spite of، although/even though، however/nevertheless، therefore.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

whereas / while + clause (contrast) · although / even though + clause · despite / in spite of + noun or -ing · however / nevertheless + new sentence · consequently / therefore / as a result

[Despite the rain], we went out // [It rained.] However, [we went out.]

⚡ although/whereas + CLAUSE · despite/in spite of + NOUN or -ING · however/nevertheless/therefore + NEW SENTENCE + comma. Never double up 'although ... but'.

although/whereas + جملة · despite/in spite of + اسم أو -ing · however/therefore + جملة جديدة وفاصلة. ولا تجمع 'although ... but'.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Choose by what FOLLOWS: 'although/even though/whereas' take a full clause; 'despite/in spite of' take a noun or -ing; 'however/nevertheless/therefore/consequently' start a NEW sentence and take a comma. Use them to structure an argument, which is heavily rewarded in speaking and writing exams.

اختر حسب ما يليها: although/whereas + جملة كاملة؛ despite/in spite of + اسم أو -ing؛ however/therefore + جملة جديدة وفاصلة. تُستخدم لبناء الحجة.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic connects clauses continuously with و/ف/لكن, so learners chain everything with 'but' and 'so'. They also produce 'despite it rained' (Arabic رغم أنّ takes a clause) and double up linkers as 'Although... but...', copying مع أن... لكن.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

I like tea, whereas she prefers coffee.

أحب الشاي، بينما تفضّل هي القهوة

[clause], whereas + [clause]

Although it rained, we went out.

رغم أنها أمطرت، خرجنا

Although + [clause], [clause]

Despite the rain, we went out.

رغم المطر، خرجنا

Despite + [noun]

It was expensive. Nevertheless, I bought it.

كان غاليًا. مع ذلك، اشتريته

[sentence]. Nevertheless, [sentence].

Sales fell. Consequently, we closed two shops.

انخفضت المبيعات. وبالتالي أغلقنا متجرين

[sentence]. Consequently, [sentence].

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ Despite it rained, we went out.

✅ Despite the rain, we went out. / Although it rained, we went out.

'despite' takes a noun or -ing; a clause needs 'although'.

❌ Although it was expensive, but I bought it.

✅ Although it was expensive, I bought it.

Use 'although' OR 'but', never both.

❌ It was expensive, however I bought it.

✅ It was expensive. However, I bought it.

'however' starts a new sentence and takes a comma after it.

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

Check what comes NEXT: clause → although · noun → despite · new sentence → however.

🎙️ Use it when you speak / استخدمها وانت بتتكلم

In accent week 14 you drilled linker stress and the pitch RESET. Fall, pause, say the linker, then restart higher — that reset is what makes your argument structure audible to an examiner.
