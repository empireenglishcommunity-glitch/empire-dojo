🎯 This week you will be able to / بعد الأسبوع ده تقدر

  • يقدر يطوّر حجّة بشكل منهجي، ويدّي أسبابًا مؤيّدة أو معارضة لوجهة نظر.
    Can develop an argument systematically, giving reasons in support of or against a point of view.
  • يقدر يكتب مقالًا أو تقريرًا يطوّر حجّة، ويدّي أسبابًا وأمثلة ذات صلة.
    Can write an essay or report that develops an argument, giving reasons and relevant examples.
  • يقدر يدفع العمل للأمام بدعوة الآخرين للمشاركة، وقول رأيه، والتفاوض للوصول لحلّ وسط.
    Can help along the progress of the work by inviting others to join in, saying what they think, and negotiating a compromise.

CEFR-aligned goals for this week. / أهداف الأسبوع حسب معيار CEFR.

💬 This Week's Pattern / نمط الأسبوع

Linking and discourse: contrast, concession, cause and result

الربط والخطاب: التضاد والتنازل والسبب والنتيجة

whereas / while + clause (contrast) · although / even though + clause · despite / in spite of + noun or -ing · however / nevertheless + new sentence · consequently / therefore / as a result

📍 Choose by what FOLLOWS: 'although/even though/whereas' take a full clause; 'despite/in spite of' take a noun or -ing; 'however/nevertheless/therefore/consequently' start a NEW sentence and take a comma. Use them to structure an argument, which is heavily rewarded in speaking and writing exams.

💡 "I like tea, whereas she prefers coffee."
