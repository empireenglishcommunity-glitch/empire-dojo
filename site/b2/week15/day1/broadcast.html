🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

What is revealed in this scene?

إيه اللي اتكشف في المشهد؟

That the audit found nothing wrong
That Karim reported the company himself, and did not tell Samira
That Nour was the one who contacted the auditors

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What made Samira suspicious?

إيه اللي خلّى سميرة تشكّ؟

He was unusually calm and stopped tidying for three weeks
He alphabetised everything in the office
He was absent for three weeks

❓ Question / سؤال 2

Why does Karim say he did not tell Samira?

ليه كريم قال إنه مقالش لسميرة؟

She would have come with him, and one of them needed to still be there
He did not trust her to keep it quiet
She was away at the time

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What does Karim say about Monday?

كريم قال إيه عن يوم الاتنين؟

He will resign on Monday
Nothing happens on Monday; it is March he cannot discuss
The audit result is published on Monday

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What does Karim conclude from the fact that nobody moved him?

كريم استنتج إيه من إن محدش نقله من مكانه؟

That the company does not consider him important
That the company has not yet noticed anything
That Hana protected him

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Samira
It wasn't the audit that worried me. It was how calm you were about the audit.
Karim
I'm calm about a lot of things.
Samira
Not about this. What you do when something's wrong is you tidy. You alphabetise. For three weeks you didn't tidy anything.
Nour
Can I just—
Samira
No. Karim. It was you who called them.
Karim
It was me who called them.
Nour
Wait, you—
Karim
What I couldn't do was watch it happen twice. That's all it was. It wasn't loyalty to the company and it certainly wasn't courage. I did it in a car park at eleven at night and I was sick afterwards.
Samira
Why didn't you tell me?
Karim
Because what you'd have done is come with me. And then it would have been the two of us in that car park, and one of us needed to still be here in six months.
Samira
That wasn't yours to decide.
Karim
No. It's the second time this year someone has told me that.
Nour
It was Hana, wasn't it. The first time.
Karim
It was Hana.
Nour
Does she know you did it?
Karim
What Hana knows is that somebody did it, and that whoever it was waited eight months longer than she did. She hasn't asked me. I think that's deliberate.
Samira
It was her they moved to the Alexandria office, wasn't it.
Karim
It was. And what I keep coming back to is that nobody moved me anywhere. Which tells you something about how much they think I matter, and it isn't flattering.
Samira
Right. So what happens on Monday?
Karim
On Monday, nothing happens. It's what happens in March that I can't tell you about.
