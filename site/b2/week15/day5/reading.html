What Matters Is the Emphasis

اللي يهم هو التوكيد

مقال عن إن ترتيب الجملة بيحدّد اللي القارئ يفتكره.

📄 Read / اقرا

It is not the facts that persuade people; it is the order they arrive in.

What most writers underestimate is how much emphasis decides.

Consider a sentence carrying three pieces of information: the delay, the cause and the remedy.

Put the remedy last and the reader remembers a problem.

Put it first and they remember a plan.

The facts are identical; what changes is which one the sentence points at.

That is precisely why 'the reason we cancelled was the weather' lands so differently from 'the weather cancelled it'.

What matters particularly is the end of a sentence.

English gives its final position enormous weight, so a crucial detail buried in the middle is, in fact, hidden.

Writers who are so worried about being unclear that they explain everything twice often bury the essential point in the second explanation.

The thing is, emphasis cannot be added afterwards with bold type.

It is built by structure.

If you want a reader to leave with one idea, the honest test is simple: read the last six words of each paragraph and ask whether they are the six you would choose.

u would choose.

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What happens if the remedy comes last?

The reader remembers a problem
The facts change
The reader remembers a plan

❓ Question / سؤال 3

Why is a detail in the middle of a sentence hidden?

ليه التفصيلة في وسط الجملة مخفية؟

Readers skip the middle
Middles are usually longer
English gives the final position most weight
