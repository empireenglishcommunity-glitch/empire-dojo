🎙️ Say This / قول ده

"Let me set the record straight, because what happened wasn't what people are saying. It was Karim who suggested the change, not me. I did raise concerns at the time — several people heard me. What I actually said was that the timing was risky, whereas the idea itself was fine. The meeting was so rushed that nobody wrote anything down. Consequently, it's now my word against his. What frustrates me most is the assumption that I stayed silent. I didn't stay silent; I was ignored. It was in that meeting that the mistake was made, and it was the rush that caused it. I do understand why people are confused. Nevertheless, what matters now isn't blame — it's fixing the problem before Friday."
