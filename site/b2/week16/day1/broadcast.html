🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

What is the point of this scene?

إيه الفكرة من المشهد ده؟

The product was withdrawn for genuine strategic reasons
The same event is told in two registers, and only the informal one is honest
The interviewer forgot to ask about safety

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What reason does Ms Farid give on the record?

السيدة فريد قالت إيه في الكلام المسجّل؟

That the product no longer aligned with strategic priorities
That nobody had actually checked the product before it went to market
That there was a genuine safety concern

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What does she say actually happened, off the record?

قالت إن اللي حصل فعلًا إيه، بعيد عن التسجيل؟

Everyone assumed somebody else had checked, and nobody had
The product had been known to be unsafe right from the very start
A competitor copied the design

❓ Question / سؤال 3

Why will she not give the honest answer on camera?

ليه مش هتقول الإجابة الصادقة على الكاميرا؟

Because it is not actually true
Because she will not be the person who says 'nobody had checked' publicly
Because her assistant advised her against it

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What does she say about the formal line she gave?

قالت إيه عن الجملة الرسمية اللي قالتها؟

She expects it to be forgotten within a week
She knows it may be used against her later and has accepted that cost
She believes it is the more accurate of the two answers

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Interviewer
Ms Farid, thank you for making time. Could you outline for us the rationale behind the decision to withdraw the product?
Ms Farid
Certainly. Following a routine review, we concluded that the product no longer aligned with our long-term strategic priorities, and we took the decision to discontinue it in an orderly manner.
Interviewer
And the reports of a safety concern?
Ms Farid
I'm not in a position to comment on speculation.
Interviewer
Thank you. Right, that's us. We're done. Ahmed, you can put that down.
Ms Farid
Is that thing off?
Interviewer
It's off.
Ms Farid
Right, look. Between us, it fell apart. Not the product, the whole thing. We'd been putting off the review for a year and a half because nobody wanted to be the one who brought it up, and then someone finally did, and it turned out we'd all been quietly assuming somebody else had checked. Nobody had.
Interviewer
That's a much better answer.
Ms Farid
It's a much worse answer, which is exactly why you got the other one. I'm not going to be the person who says 'nobody had checked' on camera.
Interviewer
You realise the strategic-priorities line is what everyone will run.
Ms Farid
Of course they'll run it. It's clean, it's short, and it doesn't require anybody to ring me back. And in eighteen months, when somebody digs it up and puts it next to whatever comes out of the review, I'll be the one explaining why I said it. I know exactly what I've just bought and what it cost.
Interviewer
Off the record, then. Will you pull the next one earlier?
Ms Farid
We'll see. Ask me again in a year and I'll give you a sentence you can use.
