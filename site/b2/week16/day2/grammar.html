🧩 The Pattern / النمط

separable: verb + object + particle (pronoun MUST go in the middle) · inseparable: verb + particle + object · phrasal (informal) vs Latinate (formal) equivalents

[turn it off] ✅ // [turn off it] ❌ // [put off] = informal, [postpone] = formal

⚡ Pronoun ALWAYS goes between verb and particle: 'turn it off'. Inseparable verbs never split. Phrasal = conversation, Latinate = formal.

الضمير دائمًا بين الفعل وحرف الجر: 'turn it off'. وغير القابل للفصل لا ينفصل. المركّب للمحادثة والرسمي للمقام الرسمي.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Separable phrasal verbs allow 'turn off the light' or 'turn the light off', but with a PRONOUN the particle must follow it: 'turn it off'. Inseparable verbs never split: 'look after him'. Choose phrasal verbs for conversation and their one-word Latinate equivalents (postpone, discover, investigate) for formal contexts.

الأفعال القابلة للفصل تسمح بالترتيبين، لكن مع الضمير يأتي حرف الجر بعده: 'turn it off'. وغير القابلة للفصل لا تنفصل. واختر المركّب للمحادثة والرسمي للمقامات الرسمية.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic verbs don't split, so 'turn off it' feels natural. Learners also treat phrasal verbs as informal-only and avoid them in speech (sounding stiff), or use them in formal writing (sounding too casual). Register control is the final B2 skill.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

Could you turn it off, please?

هل يمكنك إطفاؤه؟ (الضمير في المنتصف)

[verb + pronoun + particle] ✅

Turn off the light. / Turn the light off.

أطفئ النور (كلا الترتيبين صحيح)

[separable, both orders OK with a noun]

I'll look after him.

سأعتني به (غير قابل للفصل)

[inseparable: verb + particle + object]

We had to put off the meeting. / The meeting was postponed.

أجّلنا الاجتماع (غير رسمي / رسمي)

[phrasal] vs [Latinate]

She handed out the handouts.

وزّعت النشرات

[verb 'hand OUT'] vs [noun 'HANDout']

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ Could you turn off it?

✅ Could you turn it off?

With a pronoun the particle must come after it.

❌ I'll look him after.

✅ I'll look after him.

'look after' is inseparable and never splits.

❌ The committee will look into the matter. (in a formal report)

✅ The committee will investigate the matter.

Formal writing prefers the one-word Latinate equivalent.

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

Pronoun in the middle. Casual → phrasal · Formal → one word.

🎙️ Use it when you speak / استخدمها وانت بتتكلم

In accent week 16 you drilled particle stress and register rhythm. Say 'put it OFF' (fast, casual) against 'postPONE it' (measured, formal). This completes B2 — you now control not just accuracy but appropriacy.
