The Letter He Had Not Sent

الجواب اللي مابعتوش

مقطع أدبي فيه معنى ضمني عن أب وابنه.

📄 Read / اقرا

By the time Adel arrived, the house had already been emptied.

Somebody had swept the floor earlier and stacked the chairs, and the room smelled of a cleaning product his mother would never have bought.

He had expected to feel something dramatic.

Instead he noticed small things: a nail where a photograph had previously hung, a curtain that had faded on one side only.

He had prepared a speech on the plane and now he could not remember why.

He had planned this visit for years and had decided, before he left, exactly what he would say.

In the drawer he found an envelope with his own name on it.

His father had written the address twice, as though he had been unsure of it, and had never posted it.

Adel put it in his pocket.

He had promised himself he would read it just as soon as he arrived, and he did not.

Only afterwards, at the airport, did he realise what he had been avoiding.

The driver had asked whether he had come for a holiday.

'Something like that,' Adel said, and looked out at a city that had changed less than he had.

The envelope stayed in his jacket for eleven months.

He told people he had forgotten where he put it, which was, in a way, the truth.

a way, the truth.

He seemed unsure of it
He wanted two copies
