📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Narrative tenses together (past simple, continuous, perfect)

أزمنة السرد مجتمعة (البسيط، المستمر، التام)

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

past simple (main events) + past continuous (background scene) + past perfect (earlier backstory) + when / while / by the time

[I was walking] (scene) + [when I realised] (event) + [I'd forgotten my phone] (backstory)

⚡ Scene → was/were + -ing. Events → past simple. Earlier than the story → had + participle.

المشهد → was/were + -ing. الأحداث → ماضٍ بسيط. ما قبل القصة → had + التصريف الثالث.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

In real storytelling all three work together: past continuous sets the scene, past simple carries the events forward, past perfect fills in what happened earlier. Choosing the right layer is what makes a narrative clear.

في الحكي الحقيقي تعمل الثلاثة معًا: المستمر يهيّئ المشهد، والبسيط يحمل الأحداث، والتام يوضّح ما حدث قبلها. اختيار الطبقة الصحيحة يجعل السرد واضحًا.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic narrative relies heavily on the past simple plus connectives, so learners tell whole stories in one tense. The result is grammatically acceptable but the listener cannot tell what happened first, what was ongoing, and what is background.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

I was walking home when I realised I'd forgotten my phone.

كنت أسير إلى البيت عندما أدركت أنني نسيت هاتفي

[was + -ing] + when + [past simple] + [had + participle]

The sun was shining and the birds were singing.

كانت الشمس مشرقة والطيور تغرّد (مشهد)

[was/were + -ing] (scene)

She opened the door and looked outside.

فتحت الباب ونظرت للخارج (أحداث)

[past simple] + [past simple]

When we arrived, they had already closed.

عندما وصلنا، كانوا قد أغلقوا

when + [past simple], [had + participle]

He'd been driving for hours, so he was exhausted.

كان يقود لساعات، فكان منهكًا

[had been + -ing], so [past simple]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ I walked home when I realised I forgot my phone.

✅ I was walking home when I realised I'd forgotten my phone.

Use the continuous for the ongoing scene and the past perfect for the earlier event.

❌ While I was opening the door and I was looking outside...

✅ I opened the door and looked outside.

Sequential completed events take the past simple, not the continuous.

❌ When we arrived, they already closed.

✅ When we arrived, they had already closed.

Something completed before your arrival needs the past perfect.
