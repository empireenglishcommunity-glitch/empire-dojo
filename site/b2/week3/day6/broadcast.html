🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

What are they really arguing about?

هما بيتخانقوا على إيه فعلًا؟

Whether the photograph should be thrown away
Whether he misled her, and whether she went looking for it
Which drawer the photograph belongs in

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What had Tarek actually said he had burnt?

طارق قال إنه حرق إيه بالظبط؟

Everything
The letters
The photograph

❓ Question / سؤال 2

Why does the photograph upset Mona?

ليه الصورة بتضايق منى؟

It reminds her of the morning after, not of the beach
It is badly torn and damaged from being carried around for years
She does not appear in it

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What does Mona concede at the end?

منى اعترفت بإيه في الآخر؟

That she was the one who actually burnt the letters herself
That she was the one who went looking for it
That she never asked him anything

❓ Question / سؤال 4

How does Tarek explain the difference between what he meant and what he said?

طارق فسّر الفرق بين اللي قصده واللي قاله إزاي؟

He meant he would not keep a shrine, but said he had burnt everything because it was shorter
He meant that he had already burnt the photograph too
He says now that he meant nothing in particular

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Mona
You kept it.
Tarek
Of course I kept it. Why would I throw it away?
Mona
Because you'd told me you had. That's why. In the car, on the way back from the hospital. You said you'd burnt everything.
Tarek
I said I'd burnt the letters.
Mona
Oh. The letters.
Tarek
They were different. You know they were different. I'd been carrying those letters around for two years, and every time I read them I was worse afterwards. The photograph is just, it's the four of us on a beach. Nobody is angry in it.
Mona
That's exactly what's wrong with it. Sorry. That came out—
Tarek
No. Go on.
Mona
It's just that when I look at it, I don't see the four of us on a beach. I see the morning after. I see you standing in the kitchen not telling me. And I'd been asking you for a week. So when you keep it, and you put it in a drawer where I'd obviously find it—
Tarek
I didn't put it anywhere. It's been in that drawer since we moved. Mona. It's been in that drawer since we moved.
Mona
Do you remember what you actually said to me in that car?
Tarek
I remember what I meant.
Mona
That's not the same thing, and you know it isn't.
Tarek
No. It isn't. What I meant was, I'm not going to be the person who keeps a shrine. What I said was, I've burnt everything. Because the second one was shorter, and you were crying, and I wanted the conversation to be over.
Mona
It was over. For eight months it was completely over.
Tarek
I know.
Mona
Fine. Then I'm the one who went looking.
