Future perfect and future continuous

المستقبل التام والمستقبل المستمر

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

will have + participle (completed before a future point) · will be + -ing (in progress at a future point) · by + time · this time next year

[By Friday] [I'll have finished] // [At nine] [I'll be working]

⚡ Completed by a deadline → will have + participle (use 'by'). In progress at a moment → will be + -ing (use 'at/this time').

مكتمل بحلول موعد → will have + التصريف الثالث (مع by). مستمر في لحظة → will be + -ing (مع at/this time).

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Future perfect for something COMPLETED before a future deadline — almost always with 'by' ('by Friday', 'by the time you arrive'). Future continuous for something IN PROGRESS at a future moment ('at nine tomorrow', 'this time next week').

المستقبل التام لشيء مكتمل قبل موعد مستقبلي، وغالبًا مع 'by'. والمستقبل المستمر لشيء مستمر في لحظة مستقبلية.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic rarely marks these distinctions explicitly, so learners use the simple future for everything. The forms also compress heavily in speech ('I'll have' → /aɪləv/), so learners who have only read them don't recognise them when heard.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

By Friday I'll have finished the report.

بحلول الجمعة سأكون أنهيت التقرير

By + time, [will have + participle]

At nine tomorrow I'll be working.

التاسعة غدًا سأكون أعمل

[will be + -ing]

This time next week we'll be flying to Turkey.

هذا الوقت الأسبوع القادم سنكون مسافرين لتركيا

[will be + -ing] + future time

By the time you arrive, we'll have eaten.

بحلول وصولك سنكون قد أكلنا

By the time + [present], [will have + participle]

By next year she'll have graduated.

بحلول العام القادم ستكون تخرّجت

By + time, [will have + participle]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ By Friday I will finish the report.

✅ By Friday I'll have finished the report.

'by + deadline' needs the future perfect for completion.

❌ By the time you will arrive, we'll have eaten.

✅ By the time you arrive, we'll have eaten.

After 'by the time' use the present simple, never 'will'.

❌ At nine tomorrow I'll work.

✅ At nine tomorrow I'll be working.

In progress at a specific future moment → future continuous.

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها
