🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

What does Layla finally get Sami to accept?

ليلى خلّت سامي يعترف بإيه في الآخر؟

That speaking up in April would certainly have worked
That they did not try, rather than that trying would have failed
That Hana was the one responsible

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What does Sami say would have happened if they had spoken up in April?

سامي قال إيه اللي كان هيحصل لو اتكلموا في أبريل؟

The project would have been cancelled
They would have been replaced, and Hana would have signed the same thing
The board would have opened an investigation

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What had Sami said in the car park?

سامي قال إيه في الجراج؟

That the numbers were too clean
That they should go to the board
That Layla should check the file

❓ Question / سؤال 3

Which distinction does Sami insist on?

سامي متمسّك بأنهي فرق؟

Between what they actually knew back in April and what they know now
Between 'wouldn't have mattered' and 'wouldn't have worked'
Between the board and Hana

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What does Layla say 'let's see what the second quarter looks like' was really for?

ليلى قالت إن جملة 'نشوف الربع التاني' كانت لإيه فعلًا؟

Gathering all the evidence they would eventually need
Avoiding knowing for another three months
Giving Hana time to review the file

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Layla
If we'd said something in April, none of this would have happened.
Sami
That's not true, and you know it isn't. Because if we'd said something in April, they would have moved us both off the project and given it to Hana, and Hana would have signed exactly the same thing. It wouldn't have changed the outcome. It would have changed who was standing here.
Layla
So your position is that it wouldn't have mattered.
Sami
My position is that it wouldn't have worked. Those aren't the same thing.
Layla
Go on, then.
Sami
Look. If I'd known in April what I know now, I'd have gone to the board directly. I would have. But in April all I had was a feeling that the numbers were too clean.
Layla
You told me they were too clean. In the car park. You said nobody's costs come in that tidy.
Sami
I did say that.
Layla
And I said we should check. And you said, let's see what the second quarter looks like.
Sami
Which was a reasonable thing to say in April.
Layla
It was a reasonable thing to say if you wanted to not know for another three months. That's what it was for. If either of us had genuinely thought the numbers were fine, we wouldn't have needed to wait for the second quarter to find out.
Sami
That's a hard way to put it.
Layla
It's the accurate way to put it. So don't tell me it wouldn't have worked. Tell me we didn't try.
Sami
All right. We didn't try. That doesn't make it better.
Layla
No. But it makes it ours.
