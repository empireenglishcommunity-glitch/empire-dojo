🔊 Target Sounds / الأصوات المستهدفة: regret intonation (falling, weighted)

📝 Minimal Pairs / أزواج التمييز

I would've called ↘ (regret) / I would've called ↗ (question)
falling + slow (regret) / flat + fast (neutral)
I should've known ↘ / I should've known? ↗
what a shame ↘ / what a shame? ↗
if only I'd known ↘ / if I'd known ...

🎯 Practice Words / كلمات للتمرين

I would've called • I should've known • if only I'd known • what a shame • I wish I had • it's a pity • I regret that • I could've done more

🎙️ Say This / قول ده

"I would've called you straight away if I'd known the truth. Looking back, I should've known better than to trust him. If only I'd listened to your advice at the time, none of this would've happened."
