Circumstances and Character

الظروف والشخصية

مقال بيناقش هل الظروف ولا الشخصية اللي تحدّد النتايج.

📄 Read / اقرا

If I were a more patient person, I would have handled that meeting better.

Perhaps.

But if my circumstances had been different, I might be a more patient person now — and that is the harder thought.

We like explanations based on character.

He is disorganised; she is confident.

Such descriptions feel permanent, which is convenient, because permanent things require no action.

Yet if you had been born into a different position, with different priorities and responsibilities, you would probably describe your own character differently today.

This does not remove responsibility.

A person who is qualified and wealthy still chooses how to behave.

The point is narrower: when we explain an outcome entirely by character, we usually stop looking, and the circumstances that influenced it survive untouched to affect the next person.

A practical test is to ask what would have to change for this behaviour to become unnecessary.

If the honest answer is 'nothing — he is simply like that', character may be the explanation.

If the answer names a deadline, a workload or a lack of training, then we were never describing a character at all.

What does the writer say does NOT follow?

That character can change

What survives when we explain everything by character?

The person's confidence
The original deadline
The circumstances that influenced it
