📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

wish / if only (regrets and wishes)

wish / if only (الأماني والأسف)

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Wishes/regrets about the present (wish + past) and past (wish + past perfect); wish + would for complaints.

الأمنيات والندم عن الحاضر (wish + ماضٍ) والماضي (wish + past perfect)؛ wish + would للشكوى.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

wish + past simple (present wish) · wish + had + participle (past regret) · wish + would (annoyance) · wish + could (ability) · if only (emphatic)

[I wish I knew] (now) // [I wish I'd known] (then) // [I wish he'd stop] (annoyance)

⚡ Present wish → wish + past. Past regret → wish + had + participle. Annoyance → wish + would. Realistic future → use 'hope', not 'wish'.

أمنية حاضرة → wish + ماضٍ. أسف ماضٍ → wish + had + التصريف. تضايق → wish + would. مستقبل واقعي → استخدم hope.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

'wish + past simple' for a present situation you want to be different. 'wish + past perfect' for regret about the past. 'wish + would' to complain about someone else's repeated behaviour. 'if only' is the emphatic version of all three. Use 'were' for all persons after wish.

'wish + ماضٍ بسيط' لوضع حاضر تريده مختلفًا. 'wish + ماضٍ تام' للأسف على الماضي. 'wish + would' للشكوى من سلوك متكرر. 'if only' الصيغة التوكيدية.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic يا ليت covers all of these with one structure, so learners don't shift the tense back. They also say 'I wish I would have known' by blending the wish and third-conditional patterns, and use 'wish + will' for the future.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

I wish I knew the answer.

أتمنى لو كنت أعرف الجواب (الآن)

[wish + past simple] (present wish)

I wish I'd studied harder at school.

أتمنى لو درست بجد في المدرسة (أسف)

[wish + had + participle] (past regret)

I wish he'd stop making that noise.

أتمنى لو يتوقف عن ذلك الضجيج (تضايق)

[wish + would + verb] (annoyance)

I wish I were taller.

أتمنى لو كنت أطول

[wish + were] (all persons)

If only I'd listened to you!

يا ليتني سمعت كلامك!

[If only + had + participle] (emphatic)

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ I wish I would have known.

✅ I wish I'd known.

Past regret after 'wish' uses the past perfect, not 'would have'.

❌ I wish I will pass the exam.

✅ I hope I'll pass the exam.

'wish' is not used for realistic future hopes — use 'hope'.

❌ I wish I can come.

✅ I wish I could come.

After 'wish' the modal shifts back: can → could.

✍️ Practice / تمرين

Fill in the blank. Completing all of them marks this exercise done. / املا الفراغ. لما تخلص كلهم التمرين يتحسب.

I wish I ___ (know) the answer.

I wish I ___ (study) harder at school.

I wish he ___ (stop) making that noise.

I wish I ___ (be) taller.

I ___ (hope) I pass tomorrow. (realistic future)

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

Step the tense back one level. 'hope' for the real future, 'wish' for the unreal.

🎙️ Use it when you speak / استخدمها وانت بتتكلم

In accent week 7 you drilled /aɪwɪʃaɪd/ and regret prosody. Say 'I wish I'd STUDied harder ↘' with a breath first and a falling tone — the grammar alone won't sound like regret.
