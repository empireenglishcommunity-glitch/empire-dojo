🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

Which mistake does the father actually admit to?

الأب اعترف بأنهي غلطة فعلًا؟

Letting Amir find out about the diagnosis before Hana did
Taking away Hana's right to decide for herself
Waiting until Tuesday for the operation

❓ Question / سؤال 1

How did Amir find out?

أمير عرف إزاي؟

The father told Amir about it directly, before he told Hana
He works at the hospital and found out
Hana told him from Dubai

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What reason does the father give for not telling her?

إيه السبب اللي الأب قاله لعدم إخبارها؟

She would have lost the contract and sat watching him have tests
He simply forgot to mention it
The doctors advised him not to

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What does he ask Hana to do?

طلب من هنا تعمل إيه؟

Leave and come back on Tuesday
Ask him the medical question she has been holding
Telephone her mother immediately

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Hana
How long?
Father
Since March.
Hana
March. Since March, and you told Amir.
Father
I wish you wouldn't look at me like that. I didn't tell Amir. Amir works at the hospital. Amir found out, which is not the same thing.
Hana
If only you'd said something. One sentence. Hana, I've had some tests.
Father
And then what? You'd have come back from Dubai, you'd have lost the contract, and you'd have sat in this flat for six months watching me have tests. I wish I could tell you I'd have handled it better than you would have. I wouldn't.
Hana
That was my decision to make.
Father
Yes. It was. That's the part I got wrong. I keep wishing I'd been the sort of father you could have told bad news to. Then maybe I'd have been the sort you could hear it from.
Hana
Baba—
Father
Don't. Not yet. Ask me the medical question. You've been holding it since you walked in.
Hana
What did they say on Thursday?
Father
They said it's operable. They said Tuesday. I wish it were sooner. And before you say it: yes, I have already told your mother, and no, I did not tell her first. She asked me the same question you did, in the same voice, standing in the same place. You are more like her than either of you would like.
