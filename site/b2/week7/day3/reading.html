The Complaint That Worked

الشكوى اللي نجحت

مقال عن إزاي تشتكي بطريقة تجيب نتيجة.

📄 Read / اقرا

I wish more people knew how to complain.

Not to complain more — most of us do enough of that — but to complain in a way that changes something.

The frustrating complaint is the one that describes a feeling.

'This service is disappointing' tells the reader that you are annoyed, which they already assumed, and gives them nothing to act on.

If only we were taught the alternative at school: name the event, name the effect, name the remedy.

Compare two messages about the same problem.

The first says the delivery service is irritating and unprofessional.

The second says a parcel due on the ninth arrived on the nineteenth, that the recipient took a day off work to receive it, and that a refund of the delivery fee would settle the matter.

Only one of those can be answered.

I wish organisations would say this openly: we cannot fix a mood.

Behind almost every complaint there is a specific event somebody could have prevented, and a specific remedy somebody can afford.

The complaint that works is the one that has already done that thinking for the reader.

or the reader.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What is wrong with describing only a feeling?

إيه الغلط في وصف الإحساس بس؟

It gives the reader nothing to act on
It is usually exaggerated
It is impolite

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What three things does the writer say to name?

الكاتب بيقول تسمّي تلات حاجات إيه؟

The person, the date and the cost
The event, the effect and the remedy
The feeling, the service and the delay

❓ Question / سؤال 3

Why can only the second message be answered?

ليه الرسالة التانية بس ممكن تُجاب؟

It mentions the company by name
It is longer and more polite
It names a specific event and a specific remedy

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What does the writer say organisations cannot fix?

إيه اللي المؤسسات مش قادرة تصلّحه؟

A delivery date
A refund
A mood
