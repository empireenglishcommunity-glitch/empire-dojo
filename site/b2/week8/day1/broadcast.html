🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

What do both guests agree the coverage has got wrong?

إيه اللي الضيفين متفقين إن التغطية غلطت فيه؟

That she flatly denied every single one of the allegations put to her in the interview
That the third question, in which she made a commitment, has been ignored
That the interviewer treated her unfairly

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What did she say when asked whether she had seen the file?

قالت إيه لما اتسألت لو كانت شافت الملف؟

That she had definitely never seen the file at any point
That she could not recall
That she would resign

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What does Karim say she could have done but did not?

كريم قال إنها كان ممكن تعمل إيه وملمتش؟

Refused to deal in hypotheticals
Denied that the payments existed
Walked out of the studio

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What did she ask the interviewer?

هي سألت المحاور إيه؟

Whether he had the file
Whether he would resign
Who had approved the payments

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What question does Rania point out nobody asked?

رانيا نبّهت إن محدش سأل أنهي سؤال؟

Whether the department had approved the payments
Whether the payments had actually been made
Whether she had seen the file

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Host
Last night's interview has been described as a collapse, a trap and, by one newspaper this morning, as the most honest four minutes in a decade. Rania, you were in the room. What was she actually asked?
Rania
She was asked three things, and the order matters. She was first asked whether the department had approved the payments. She said it had not. She was then asked whether she personally had seen the file. She said she couldn't recall. And she was finally asked, and this is the question everyone has forgotten, whether she would resign if it turned out that she had. She said she would.
Host
Karim, why has that third question disappeared from the coverage?
Karim
Because it doesn't fit either story. Those who want her gone are reporting that she denied everything, which she didn't; she made a commitment. Those defending her are reporting that she was ambushed, which she wasn't; she was asked a hypothetical and she chose to answer it. She could have said 'I won't deal in hypotheticals'. Ministers say that eleven times a day. She didn't.
Rania
I would add one thing. The word 'approved' was doing a lot of work in that first answer. She was asked whether the department had approved the payments, and she said it had not. Nobody asked her whether the payments had been made. Those are different questions, and she answered the one she was given, precisely and, as far as I can tell, truthfully.
Karim
Which is what makes the interview interesting rather than damaging. She was not caught out. She was unusually careful, and then, in the last thirty seconds, she was not.
Rania
She also asked the interviewer a question, which nobody has reported. She asked him whether he had the file.
Host
And?
Rania
He said he did.
