Reported speech: questions, commands and reporting verbs

الكلام المنقول: الأسئلة والأوامر وأفعال النقل

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Report questions, orders and requests; use reporting verbs (advise, warn, offer, suggest, admit).

نقل الأسئلة والأوامر والطلبات؛ واستخدام أفعال النقل.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

asked + if/whether + statement order · asked + wh- + statement order · told/asked + person + to + verb · reporting verb + that / + -ing / + preposition

"Where is it?" → [He asked where it was] // "Wait!" → [She told me to wait]

⚡ Reported questions → statement order, no inversion. Commands → told/asked + person + to + verb. Choose a reporting verb that carries the attitude, and learn its pattern.

الأسئلة المنقولة → ترتيب خبري بدون قلب. الأوامر → told/asked + شخص + to + فعل. واختر فعل نقل يحمل الموقف وتعلّم نمطه.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

Reported questions use statement word order with no auxiliary inversion and no question mark. Reported commands and requests use 'told/asked + person + to + verb'. At B2, choose a reporting verb that carries the speaker's attitude: admitted, denied, insisted, suggested, warned, refused.

الأسئلة المنقولة بترتيب الجملة الخبرية بدون قلب ولا علامة استفهام. والأوامر بـ told/asked + شخص + to + فعل. وفي B2 اختر فعل نقل يحمل موقف المتحدث.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic keeps question word order in reported speech, so 'he asked where is the bank' feels correct. Learners also default to 'said that' for everything, losing the nuance, and each reporting verb takes a different pattern (suggested + -ing, insisted on + -ing, refused + to) which must be learnt individually.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

He asked where the station was.

سأل أين كانت المحطة

[asked + wh- + statement order]

She asked whether I was ready.

سألت إن كنت مستعدًا

[asked + whether + statement order]

She told me to wait outside.

قالت لي أن أنتظر بالخارج

[told + person + to + verb]

He admitted that he'd been wrong.

اعترف بأنه كان مخطئًا

[admitted + that + clause]

They suggested meeting on Friday.

اقترحوا الاجتماع يوم الجمعة

[suggested + -ing]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ He asked where was the station.

✅ He asked where the station was.

Reported questions use statement word order — no inversion.

❌ She suggested me to come.

✅ She suggested that I come. / She suggested coming.

'suggest' never takes 'person + to'; use 'that + clause' or '-ing'.

❌ He said me to wait.

✅ He told me to wait.

'say' takes no person object; 'tell' does.
