The Triumph Narrative

حكاية الانتصار

تقرير عن عيادة: الكاتب مش مقتنع بحكاية 'الإصرار'، وبيلمّح إن المشكلة كانت إدارية.

📄 Read / اقرا

Rarely does a training programme survive its first year unchanged, and seldom does one survive it twice.

What follows is not a triumph narrative, although it has been sold as one.

The clinic opened with fourteen staff and a daunting brief: to treat, within eighteen months, a population that had never had access to physiotherapy.

No sooner had the doors opened than the first setback arrived.

The equipment, delayed at the port, did not clear customs for eleven weeks.

Not only did the staff have no machines; they had no protocol for working without them.

What they did next is described, in the funder's report, as remarkable resilience.

It was certainly perseverance.

Whether it was good practice is a separate question, and one the report under no circumstances raises.

Staff improvised, using furniture and their own body weight, and they persisted for nearly three months.

Two of them developed the injuries they had been hired to treat.

Hardly anyone reading the published account would guess that.

The word "adversity" appears four times.

The word "injury" does not appear at all.

The relentless emphasis on grit obscures a simpler finding: the obstacle was administrative, it was foreseeable, and it had nothing to do with anybody's character.

There is a version of this story in which fourteen people overcame adversity against all expectations.

There is another in which they endured an avoidable failure of logistics and were then congratulated for enduring it.

Both are accurate.

Only one raises money.

The clinic now treats two hundred patients a week.

It also keeps a customs officer on retainer, which is what actually solved the problem, and which nobody has called extraordinary.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What is the writer's attitude to the funder's report?

إيه موقف الكاتب من تقرير الجهة الممولة؟

Broadly supportive, with minor reservations
Critical — it praises character to avoid naming an administrative failure
Neutral; the writer only summarises it

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What does the writer imply actually solved the problem?

الكاتب بيلمّح إن إيه اللي حلّ المشكلة فعلًا؟

The staff's perseverance
The eventual arrival of the equipment
Keeping a customs officer on retainer

❓ Question / سؤال 3

Why does the writer point out that the word 'injury' never appears?

ليه الكاتب نبّه إن كلمة 'إصابة' مش موجودة خالص؟

To suggest the report omits the cost of the improvisation it praises
To show the report was written carelessly and without the funder ever reading the clinical notes
To blame the staff for being injured

❓ Question / سؤال 4

'Both are accurate. Only one raises money.' What is being implied?

'الاتنين صح. واحد بس هو اللي بيجمع فلوس.' إيه المعنى الضمني؟

The fundraising version is dishonest and ought never to have been published by the funder
Both versions should have been published
Accuracy is not what determined which version was told
