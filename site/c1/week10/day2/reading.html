The Extent to Which

إلى أي مدى

نص فني متخصص عن تحديد المعايير: الرقم الواحد اللي بيلخّص كل حاجة بيخفي أكتر مما يوضح.

📄 Read / اقرا

The index, most of whom who cite it have never examined, combines nine indicators, many of which measure the same underlying quantity.

This note delineates what it can and cannot support.

Begin with the parameters.

Each indicator is normalised to a scale of one to ten, a step by means of which incommensurable quantities are made addable, and at which point most of the interpretive damage is done.

Air quality, a continuous variable with a well-characterised threshold above which harm is documented, is thereby rendered comparable to institutional trust, for which no threshold has ever been specified.

The way in which the two are then averaged implies a spectrum of equivalent magnitude.

There is none.

Two consequences follow, as a result of which the index is routinely misapplied.

First, movement is uninterpretable at the margin: a city may rise four places on changes in three indicators, none of which is individually significant, in which case the aggregate is precise without being accurate.

Second, the criterion by which cities are grouped into bands is a quantile, not a substantive cut, whereby a difference of 0.

2 becomes a difference of category.

The index does one thing well, and it is worth being explicit about it.

Where the degree to which two cities differ is large — a point at which the composite comfortably exceeds its own error — the ranking is robust and has been validated against nuanced local data on four occasions.

Used comprehensively, it is a screening instrument.

Used to quantify improvement year on year, it characterises noise.

The distinction is not subtle, and the elaborate methodological appendix, which does specify it, is not what gets read.

ot what gets read.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What does the writer say the index is genuinely good for?

المؤشر كويس في إيه فعلًا؟

Comparing cities that differ by a wide margin
Measuring year-on-year improvement
Setting policy thresholds

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What is the problem with normalising to a one-to-ten scale?

إيه مشكلة التوحيد على مقياس من واحد لعشرة؟

It loses decimal precision
It favours large cities
It makes incommensurable quantities addable and implies equivalent magnitude

❓ Question / سؤال 3

Why is movement at the margin uninterpretable?

ليه الحركة عند الحدود مش قابلة للتفسير؟

The data are collected annually
A city can move several places on changes none of which is individually significant
Cities are grouped alphabetically

What does the writer note about the methodological appendix?

It does specify the limitation, and is not what gets read
It contradicts the index
It has never been written
