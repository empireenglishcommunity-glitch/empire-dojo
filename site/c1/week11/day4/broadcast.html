🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

What had they not actually agreed?

إيه اللي مكانوش متفقين عليه فعلًا؟

Whether to run a pilot at all
Who would write the minutes
What counted as the pilot — one district or two

❓ Question / سؤال 1

Why had nobody noticed?

ليه محدش لاحظ؟

They had all been nodding at the same word meaning different things
Two of them were absent
The minutes were wrong

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What changes Tarek's mind?

إيه اللي غيّر رأي طارق؟

That Hala disagreed with him
That Rania outranks him
That it is a district — four thousand people and a budget line

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What does Hala say happened before?

هالة قالت إيه اللي حصل قبل كده؟

They found out late and it cost them a quarter
They ran the pilot twice
Nobody minuted the meeting

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Rania
Pilot first, then rollout. Yes?
Tarek
Yes.
Hala
Same.
Rania
Good. So that's the two northern districts done by March and if they're fine, the rest in the summer.
Tarek
Sorry — the two?
Rania
The pilot. The two.
Tarek
I had the pilot as one. The other one's in the rollout, so it doesn't wait.
Hala
Neither did I. One.
Rania
You did? Both of you?
Hala
Well — I assumed. Nobody said otherwise.
Rania
Nobody said anything, that's the trouble. We've all been nodding at a word.
Tarek
Does it matter that much? One or two, it's the same programme.
Rania
It's a district. It's four thousand people and a budget line. So yes.
Tarek
Fair enough. Point taken.
Hala
Can I say — I'd rather we sorted it now than found out in April. We've done that before and it cost us a quarter.
Rania
Precisely. So: one or two, out loud, and somebody writes it down. Tarek?
Tarek
Two. If it's four thousand people, two.
Hala
Two. And I'll minute it this time.
Rania
And while we're here — the summer date. Is that all of the rest, or the rest minus the two we've just been arguing about?
Tarek
...See, now I don't know.
Hala
Nor do I, and I wrote the timeline.
Rania
Right. So we have two words doing this to us. 'Pilot' and 'the rest'.
Tarek
Shall we just list the districts? Names and dates, one page, no nouns that can mean two things.
Hala
Yes. And circulate it before anybody says the word 'rollout' again.
Rania
Agreed. And Tarek — no offence meant — if it lands and it doesn't match what you think you agreed, say so that day. Not in April.
Tarek
Fair. That one's on me last time, I'll own it.
Hala
Then we're done. One page, names and dates, by Friday.
