Ellipsis and substitution

الحذف والاستبدال

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Avoid repetition efficiently with ellipsis and substitute forms: so/neither do I, one/ones, do so, if not.

تجنّب التكرار بكفاءة عبر الحذف والاستبدال: so/neither do I، one/ones، do so.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

So + auxiliary + subject (So do I) · Neither/Nor + auxiliary + subject · one/ones for nouns · do so / do the same for verb phrases · omission of repeated words (I'd like to, but I can't)

[So] + [do] + [I] · I'd like to come, but I can't [come]

⚡ So/Neither + auxiliary + subject (invert!). One negative only. one/ones for nouns, do so for verb phrases.

So/Neither + المساعد + الفاعل (اقلب!). ونفي واحد فقط. وone/ones للأسماء، وdo so للعبارات الفعلية.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

To avoid repetition, which English strongly prefers. 'So do I' agrees positively; 'Neither do I' agrees negatively; 'one/ones' replaces a repeated noun; 'do so' replaces a whole verb phrase in formal contexts; and repeated words are simply deleted after an auxiliary ('I can't'). Using ellipsis well is one of the clearest markers of natural, fluent English.

لتجنّب التكرار الذي تكرهه الإنجليزية. 'So do I' للموافقة المثبتة، و'Neither do I' للمنفية، و'one/ones' لاستبدال الاسم، و'do so' لعبارة فعلية كاملة في السياق الرسمي.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic tolerates repetition comfortably and marks agreement with pronoun-plus-particle constructions (وأنا أيضًا), so there is no pressure to compress and no inversion involved. Learners therefore repeat the full verb, which sounds laboured, or they produce 'So I do' without inverting. The negative pattern causes a further error: because 'neither' already carries the negative, adding another one ('Neither I don't') reverses the meaning.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

I love this city. — So do I.

أحبّ هذه المدينة. — وأنا أيضًا

[So] + [auxiliary] + [subject]

I don't agree. — Neither do I.

لا أوافق. — ولا أنا

[Neither] + [auxiliary] + [subject]

I'll take the cheaper ones, thanks.

سآخذ الأرخص، شكرًا

[determiner + adjective + ones]

She was asked to explain and did so willingly.

طُلب منها أن تشرح ففعلت ذلك برضا

[did so] replacing a verb phrase

I'd like to come, but I can't.

أودّ أن آتي، لكن لا أستطيع

auxiliary + [deleted verb phrase]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ So I do.

✅ So do I.

After 'so' and 'neither' the auxiliary comes before the subject.

❌ Neither I don't agree.

✅ Neither do I.

'Neither' already carries the negative, so a second one reverses the meaning.

❌ I'll take the cheaper one's.

✅ I'll take the cheaper ones.

The plural of the substitute is 'ones' with no apostrophe.

❌ She was asked to explain and did so it.

✅ She was asked to explain and did so.

'Do so' already replaces the whole verb phrase, so no object is added.
