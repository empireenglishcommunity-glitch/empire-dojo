The Former and the Latter

الأول والتاني

نص عن الحذف في اللغة: الاختصار بيوفّر كلمات لكن بيحمّل القارئ عبء.

📄 Read / اقرا

Two conventions govern professional prose, and neither is a rule.

The first is that repetition should be avoided; the second, that meaning should never depend on the reader's memory.

The former is taught; the latter is not, and where they conflict the elliptical option usually wins.

Consider a paragraph that introduces two funding streams and then discusses "the latter" three sentences later.

Such a construction is economical, and it is correct.

Whether it is readable depends on something the writer cannot observe: whether the reader, at that point, still holds both items in the same order.

Many do.

Some do not, and if not, the sentence is not merely harder — it silently reverses.

Substitution carries the same risk in a milder form.

"Do so" is admirably concise, and likewise "if so" and "the ones I mentioned"; nobody would omit them on principle.

But each substitutes a short form for an antecedent that must be retrieved, and retrieval is where a tired reader fails.

Vice versa, prose that refuses all substitution reads as though addressed to a machine, and is genuinely worse.

The practical test is not stylistic.

Read the sentence in isolation.

If it remains unambiguous, the ellipsis was efficient.

If it does not, you have not been concise; you have transferred work, and implicitly asserted that the reader would rather do it than read six more words.

Most would not.

Redundancy, then, is not always waste.

A phrase repeated once, at the point where a reader is likely to have lost it, costs a line and buys accuracy.

Writers trained to streamline everything omit it, and are surprised to be misread by people who were, in fact, reading carefully.

act, reading carefully.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

Which of the two conventions does the writer say goes untaught?

أنهي قاعدة من الاتنين مش بتتعلّم؟

That repetition should always be avoided, which the writer calls the more important of the two
That prose should be formal
That meaning should not depend on the reader's memory

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What is the specific danger of 'the latter' used three sentences later?

إيه الخطر في استخدام 'الأخير' بعد تلات جمل؟

It is grammatically incorrect
If the reader has lost the order, the sentence silently reverses
It is too formal for professional prose, and most editors now forbid the construction outright

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What is the writer's practical test for ellipsis?

إيه الاختبار العملي للحذف؟

Count the words saved
Read the sentence in isolation and check it is still unambiguous
Ask a colleague to read it

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What does the writer conclude about redundancy?

إيه خلاصة الكاتب عن التكرار؟

It should always be removed
It is a sign of careless writing
