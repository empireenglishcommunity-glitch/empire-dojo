Emphasis and intensification

التوكيد والتكثيف

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Add emphasis with auxiliary 'do', and intensifiers: far too, nowhere near, by far, all the more, utterly.

التوكيد بـ 'do' المساعدة، والمكثّفات: far too، nowhere near، by far، utterly.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

emphatic do/does/did + base verb · far too + adjective · nowhere near + adjective · by far the + superlative · all the more + adjective · utterly/absolutely/completely + extreme adjective

[I] [do] [understand] · [by far] [the best] · [nowhere near] [ready]

⚡ Emphatic do/does/did + BASE verb, and stress it. far too = excess. nowhere near = big shortfall. utterly/absolutely only with extreme adjectives.

do/does/did التوكيدية + المصدر، وانبرها. وfar too للإفراط. وnowhere near لنقص كبير. وutterly/absolutely مع الصفات المتطرّفة فقط.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

To insist, contradict or strengthen a claim. Emphatic 'do' adds insistence to a positive sentence, usually against an unspoken doubt ('I do understand'). 'far too' marks excess, 'nowhere near' marks a large shortfall, 'by far' strengthens a superlative, and 'all the more' means 'even more, because of that'. Use these to sound firm without sounding aggressive.

للإصرار أو النقض أو تقوية الادّعاء. 'do' التوكيدية تضيف إصرارًا لجملة مثبتة، و'far too' للإفراط، و'nowhere near' لنقص كبير، و'by far' لتقوية التفضيل، و'all the more' لمزيد بسبب ذلك.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic intensifies with adverbs and repetition (جدًا / إطلاقًا) and has no auxiliary whose only job is to carry emphasis, so emphatic 'do' has nothing to map onto. Learners omit it and lose the insistence, or include it without stressing it, which makes the sentence merely odd. The intensifiers add a collocation problem: 'utterly' and 'absolutely' pair only with extreme adjectives, so 'utterly good' is not English even though it is grammatical.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

I do understand your concern.

أنا أفهم قلقك فعلًا

subject + [do] + base verb

The original quote was far too expensive.

كان العرض الأصلي مكلفًا جدًا

[far too] + adjective

We're nowhere near ready for the launch.

لسنا مستعدّين إطلاقًا للإطلاق

[nowhere near] + adjective

It was by far the best presentation of the day.

كان أفضل عرض في اليوم بفارق كبير

[by far] + the + superlative

Given her age, that is all the more impressive.

بالنظر إلى عمرها، هذا أكثر إثارة للإعجاب

[all the more] + adjective

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ I do understood your concern.

✅ I do understand your concern.

Emphatic 'do' carries the tense, so the main verb stays in its base form.

❌ She does cares about the result.

✅ She does care about the result.

'Does' already marks the third person, so the verb takes no extra -s.

❌ The plan is utterly good.

✅ The plan is absolutely excellent.

'Utterly' and 'absolutely' pair with extreme adjectives, not mild ones.

❌ We are nowhere near of ready.

✅ We are nowhere near ready.

'Nowhere near' is followed directly by the adjective, with no preposition.

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

'Do' adds no meaning — it exists purely to be stressed.

🎙️ Use it when you speak / استخدمها وانت بتتكلم

In accent week 12 you drilled emphatic 'do' as the loudest word in its clause and one heavy beat for corrections. Included but unstressed, emphatic 'do' has no purpose at all.
