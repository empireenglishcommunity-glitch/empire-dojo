💬 This Week's Pattern / نمط الأسبوع

Emphasis and intensification

التوكيد والتكثيف

emphatic do/does/did + base verb · far too + adjective · nowhere near + adjective · by far the + superlative · all the more + adjective · utterly/absolutely/completely + extreme adjective

📍 To insist, contradict or strengthen a claim. Emphatic 'do' adds insistence to a positive sentence, usually against an unspoken doubt ('I do understand'). 'far too' marks excess, 'nowhere near' marks a large shortfall, 'by far' strengthens a superlative, and 'all the more' means 'even more, because of that'. Use these to sound firm without sounding aggressive.

💡 "I do understand your concern."
