Future in the past and complex time reference

المستقبل في الماضي والإشارة الزمنية المركّبة

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Refer to a future seen from the past and layered time: was going to, would later, was to become, by which time.

الإشارة لمستقبل من منظور الماضي وزمن متعدّد الطبقات: was going to، would later، by which time.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

was/were going to + verb · would later/eventually + verb · was/were to + verb (destiny or arrangement) · was/were to have + participle (unfulfilled) · was about to + verb · on the point of + -ing · by which time

[I was going to call], [but it got late]

⚡ was going to + base (usually didn't happen). would (not will) inside past narrative. was to have + third form = failed plan. on the point of + -ing.

was going to + المصدر (لم يحدث عادةً). وwould لا will داخل السرد الماضي. وwas to have + التصريف الثالث لخطة فشلت. وon the point of + -ing.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

To describe a future seen from a point already in the past — essential for storytelling, biography and explanation. 'was going to' marks an intention that usually did not happen. 'would later' narrates what followed. 'was to' expresses destiny or a formal arrangement, and 'was to have' marks a plan that failed. 'was about to' means something was seconds away.

لوصف مستقبل من منظور نقطة ماضية — أساسي للحكاية والسيرة والتفسير. 'was going to' نيّة لم تتحقّق غالبًا، و'would later' تسرد ما تلا، و'was to' قدر أو ترتيب، و'was to have' خطة فشلت.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic narrative marks sequence through context and particles rather than through a dedicated future-in-the-past form, so learners default to a simple past and lose the crucial implication that the intention was never fulfilled. Saying 'I called you but it was late' instead of 'I was going to call you but it got late' changes the facts entirely. A second problem is using 'will' inside a past narrative, where 'would' is required.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

I was going to call you, but it got late.

كنت سأتصل بك، لكن الوقت تأخّر

[was going to + base verb], but …

He would later become the company's first director.

كان سيصبح لاحقًا أول مدير للشركة

[would + later + base verb]

The meeting was to begin at nine sharp.

كان من المقرّر أن يبدأ الاجتماع في التاسعة

[was to + base verb]

The report was to have been finished by Friday.

كان من المفترض أن ينتهي التقرير بحلول الجمعة

[was to have been + participle]

I was about to leave when the phone rang.

كنت على وشك المغادرة عندما رنّ الهاتف

[was about to + base verb] + when …

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ I was going to called you.

✅ I was going to call you.

'Going to' is always followed by the base form of the verb.

❌ He will later become the director.

✅ He would later become the director.

Inside a past narrative the future is expressed with 'would', not 'will'.

❌ She was on the point of resign.

✅ She was on the point of resigning.

'On the point of' is followed by an -ing form.

❌ The delivery was to arrive on Monday, but it didn't.

✅ The delivery was to have arrived on Monday.

For a plan that clearly failed, 'was to have + participle' states it directly.
