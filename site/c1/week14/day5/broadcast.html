🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

What risk does she identify that the contract ignores?

أنهي مخاطرة حدّدتها والعقد متجاهلها؟

That the permits will be refused
That her steel price will rise while she waits for permits
That the milestone penalty will be enforced

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What does 'I'm eating the difference' mean?

إيه معنى 'أنا اللي هدفع الفرق'؟

She absorbs the extra cost herself
She will cancel the contract
She will pass it to the supplier

❓ Question / سؤال 2

Why can she not simply walk away later?

ليه مش تقدر تمشي بعدين؟

She will have poured foundations by then
The contract forbids it
Her supplier would sue her

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What does she settle for instead of a price clause?

قبلت بإيه بدل بند الأسعار؟

A larger advance payment
A review trigger if permit delay passes nine months
Removal of the milestone penalty

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Dalia
So on paper it's fine. Twelve months, and if the permits drag, the clock stops. I've no problem with that.
Fouad
Good. It's fairly standard, to be honest. Most people sign it as is.
Dalia
Most people aren't carrying the concrete. Here's my thing. Permits drag — fine, we're covered. Milestone slips — that's your call, you can waive it or come after me, and at least I can see that coming.
Fouad
We've never enforced it. Not once.
Dalia
I believe you. It's still your call, though, isn't it. Anyway that's not what's keeping me up. What's keeping me up isn't in here at all.
Fouad
Go on.
Dalia
Steel. If the permits take fourteen months and my supplier's quote lapses at six, I'm eating the difference. There's nothing in this about price.
Fouad
That's — well, that's between you and your supplier, strictly speaking.
Dalia
Strictly speaking, yes. Which is a polite way of saying it lands on me. And I can't hedge it, I'm too small to hedge it, and I can't walk away in month thirteen because I'll have poured foundations.
Fouad
I hear you. I'm not sure I can get an escalation clause past the committee.
Dalia
Then let's do this. Put in writing that if the permit delay runs past nine months we sit down again. That's not money. That's a conversation.
Fouad
A review trigger. That I can probably do.
Dalia
Then we're close. Because as it stands, the only person in this room taking a risk nobody has priced is me.
Fouad
I'll take the review trigger to them on Thursday. I think it goes through.
Dalia
And if it doesn't?
Fouad
Then you'd be signing it as it stands, and I'd understand if you didn't sign it.
Dalia
Good. I'd rather hear that than be told it's standard one more time.
