On Condition That

بشرط أن

نص عن صياغة العقود: الشرط اللي مش مكتوب بيبقى واجب على الطرف الأضعف.

📄 Read / اقرا

A grant agreement is a machine for allocating uncertainty, and it works only insofar as every condition in it is attached to somebody who can satisfy it.

Provided that this holds, the document is a genuine allocation of risk.

Where it does not, the risk has not disappeared; it has simply been left where it fell.

Consider a clause of a familiar type.

Funds are released on condition that construction begins within twelve months, unless delay is attributable to regulatory approval, in which case the period is extended.

Supposing the approval takes fourteen months — as it did — the extension is triggered and nobody is at fault.

So far the machine works.

Now consider what the agreement did not stipulate.

Assuming, as both parties evidently did, that the contractor's price would hold for those fourteen months, no provision was made for the eventuality that it would not.

But for that omission, the escalation would have been a shared contingency.

As long as it went unmentioned, it remained wholly with the recipient, who had no means of hedging it and no standing to renegotiate.

This is the general form of the problem.

A condition that is written is a condition that has been priced by somebody.

A prerequisite that is merely presupposed is subject to no such discipline, and it will hinge on whichever party is least able to walk away.

Given that drafting time is finite, the practical counsel is narrow: for every assumption on which the schedule depends, ask who bears it if it fails.

Were the answer to be "the weaker party, by default", the clause has not been drafted.

It has been declined, and the reciprocal obligation everyone believes they signed does not exist.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What does the writer say a grant agreement is for?

اتفاقية التمويل موجودة ليه حسب الكاتب؟

Recording the parties' intentions
Protecting the funder
Allocating uncertainty to whoever can satisfy each condition

❓ Question / سؤال 2

Which risk was left entirely with the recipient?

أنهي مخاطرة وقعت بالكامل على المستفيد؟

The contractor's price not holding
Regulatory approval taking fourteen months
Construction beginning late

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What is the difference between a written and a presupposed condition?

إيه الفرق بين شرط مكتوب وشرط مفترض؟

A written one is legally binding, a presupposed one is moral
A written one has been priced by somebody; a presupposed one has not
There is no practical difference

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What test does the writer recommend when drafting?

إيه الاختبار اللي بينصح بيه عند الصياغة؟

Count the conditions
Ask who bears each assumption if it fails
Have a lawyer review every clause
