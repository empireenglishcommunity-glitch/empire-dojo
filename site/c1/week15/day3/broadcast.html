🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

What are the idioms being used to avoid?

التعبيرات المجازية بتستخدم لتجنّب إيه؟

Naming the individual colleague who would be held responsible for the outcome
Admitting the rule is clear and applying it would be costly
Explaining a genuinely ambiguous regulation

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What distinction does Hala draw?

إيه الفرق اللي هالة وضّحته؟

Between a grey area and a review date
Between parking and dropping a decision
Between the answer being inconvenient and the question being hard

❓ Question / سؤال 2

Why does the wording matter administratively?

ليه الصياغة مهمة إداريًا؟

The minutes are published externally
'Grey area' generates no follow-up; a decision gets a review date
Fouad drafts the minutes himself

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What does she say is not defensible?

قالت إيه اللي مش ممكن الدفاع عنه؟

Parking a decision for six months
A phrase that means whatever the next person needs it to mean
Dropping the matter altogether, as long as the reason for doing so is minuted

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Hala
So we're calling it a grey area and moving on. That's where we landed.
Fouad
It is a grey area. I don't think anybody's pretending otherwise.
Hala
Is it, though. Because a grey area is when the rule doesn't tell you what to do, and the rule here is crystal clear. It's just expensive.
Fouad
Well, quite. And if we get into it we'll open a right can of worms, and then we're into three years of it.
Hala
That I'll grant you. But notice what you just did. You said the answer would be inconvenient, not that the question was hard. Those are different animals.
Fouad
You're splitting hairs.
Hala
I'm reading the minutes. Because 'grey area' goes in the file as unresolved-ish and generates nothing, whereas 'we decided not to look' gets a review date. One of those comes back and the other doesn't, and we both know which is which.
Fouad
You'd have us write 'we decided not to look'? That's a hostage to fortune.
Hala
It's the truth, though, isn't it. And I'd rather hand a hostage to fortune than kick this down the road for somebody who wasn't in the room.
Fouad
...
Hala
Look. I'm not trying to reopen it today. I'm saying pick your words on purpose. If we're parking it, say parked, and put a date on it. If we're dropping it, say dropped, and say why. Either's defensible. What isn't is a phrase that means whatever the next person needs it to mean.
Fouad
Parked. Six months. I'll draft it.
Hala
Thank you. And put your name on the draft, not the committee's.
Fouad
Why does that matter?
Hala
Because in six months somebody will ask who parked it, and 'the committee' isn't a person you can put that question to.
Fouad
...That's a fair point, annoyingly. Fine. My name on it.
