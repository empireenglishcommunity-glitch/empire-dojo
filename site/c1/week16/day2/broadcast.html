🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

What changes when the recorder stops?

إيه اللي يتغيّر لما التسجيل يقف؟

She contradicts what she said on the record
She gives the same event in a register that names what actually happened
She refuses to discuss it further

❓ Question / سؤال 1

Why does she call the honest answer 'much worse'?

ليه سمّت الإجابة الصادقة 'أسوأ بكتير'؟

Because it is untrue and she would be lying to the public if she ever said it
Because it is longer
Worse for her, which is why the formal line was given instead

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What does she say about the strategic-priorities line?

قالت إيه عن جملة 'الأولويات الاستراتيجية'؟

She expects it to be forgotten
She knows it may be used against her later and accepts that cost
She believes it is the accurate explanation

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What does 'nobody fancied being the one to bring it up' mean?

إيه معنى 'محدش كان نفسه يكون هو اللي يفتح الموضوع'؟

Nobody wanted to be the person who raised it
Nobody on the team actually understood the safety issue that was involved
Nobody was asked

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Interviewer
Ms Farid, could you outline for us the rationale behind the decision to withdraw the product?
Ms Farid
Certainly. Following a routine review, we concluded that the product no longer aligned with our long-term strategic priorities, and we took the decision to discontinue it in an orderly manner.
Interviewer
And the reports of a safety concern?
Ms Farid
I'm not in a position to comment on speculation.
Interviewer
Thank you. Right, that's us — Ahmed, you can put that down.
Ms Farid
Is that thing off?
Interviewer
It's off.
Ms Farid
Right. Look — between us, the whole thing fell apart. Not the product, the process. We'd been putting the review off for a year and a half because nobody fancied being the one to bring it up, and then someone finally did, and it turned out we'd all been quietly assuming somebody else had checked. Nobody had.
Interviewer
That's a much better answer.
Ms Farid
It's a much worse answer, which is exactly why you got the other one. I'm not going to be the woman who says 'nobody had checked' on camera.
Interviewer
You know the strategic-priorities line is what everyone will run.
Ms Farid
Of course they'll run it. It's clean, it's short, nobody has to ring me back. And in eighteen months when somebody digs it up and puts it next to the review, I'll be the one explaining why I said it. I know exactly what I've bought and what it cost.
Interviewer
Will you pull the next one earlier?
Ms Farid
We'll see. Ask me in a year and I'll give you a sentence you can use.
Interviewer
One more, off the record. Do you regret the line?
Ms Farid
No. I regret that it's the one that works. Those are different regrets, and only one of them is mine to fix.
Interviewer
I might use that.
Ms Farid
You won't. It's got a subordinate clause in it.
