💬 This Week's Pattern / نمط الأسبوع

Register and style shifting

تغيير الأسلوب ومستوى الرسمية

Formal: full forms, nominalisation, passive, Latinate verbs (require, obtain, commence) · Neutral: standard clauses · Casual: contractions, phrasal verbs (need, get, start), ellipsis · Signposting for presentations

📍 To match how you speak or write to the situation. Formal register uses full forms, nominalisation, the passive and Latinate verbs; casual register uses contractions, phrasal verbs and ellipsis. Presenting is a third register with explicit signposting. At C1 the expectation is not that you know one style, but that you can move deliberately between them and stay consistent within each.
