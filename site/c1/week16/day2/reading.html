We Regret to Inform You

يأسفنا أن نبلغكم

مقارنة بين نسختين من نفس الخبر: الرسمية بتخفي الفاعل، وغير الرسمية بتقوله.

📄 Read / اقرا

Two documents describe the same decision.

The first is the letter that was sent.

The second is the email a manager wrote to her own team an hour earlier, which was later disclosed.

Reading them together is the most efficient lesson in register I know.

The letter commences: "Please be advised that, with respect to your application, we regret to inform you that the panel has been unable to proceed on this occasion."

It requires eighteen words to say no.

It attributes the decision to a panel, which is courteous and also true in a limited sense, and it uses "unable", which implies constraint where a choice was in fact made.

Nothing in it is false.

Its formality is not decoration; it is doing the work of obscuring agency.

The email reads: "We're not going ahead.

It's not the proposal, it's that we've got loads of these and only two slots, and honestly the other lot know the sector better.

Tell folks it was competitive, which it was."

That is blunt, colloquial, and considerably more informative.

It supplies the actual criterion, the comparison, and a suggested public formulation, and it would be indefensible to send to an applicant.

The interesting question is not which is honest.

Both are.

The letter conceals who decided and why; the email conceals nothing and would humiliate the reader.

Register is not a scale from evasive to plain.

It is a choice about which information to purchase and what to pay for it.

What a professional writer should be able to do is name that price.

To paraphrase the letter accurately, one has to obtain something it declines to state — and knowing that a euphemism is present is a different skill from resenting it.

skill from resenting it.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What does the writer say the letter's formality is doing?

الرسمية في الخطاب بتعمل إيه حسب الكاتب؟

Showing respect for the applicant
Obscuring who made the decision and why
Making the letter shorter and more efficient than the manager's blunt internal email

❓ Question / سؤال 2

Why does the writer object to the word 'unable'?

ليه الكاتب معترض على كلمة 'لم يتمكن'؟

It is too informal for a decision letter of this kind and quietly undermines the panel
It implies constraint where a choice was made
It is factually false

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What does the writer say about which document is honest?

قال إيه عن أي مستند هو الصادق؟

The email is honest and the letter is not
Neither is honest
Both are honest; they conceal different things

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What skill does the writer say a professional should have?

إيه المهارة اللي المفروض تكون عند المحترف؟

Naming the price of a register choice, and noticing a euphemism is present
Always writing plainly
Refusing to use formal language
