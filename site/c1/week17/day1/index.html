💬 This Week's Pattern / نمط الأسبوع

Complex noun phrases and precision

العبارات الاسمية المركّبة والدقّة

Pre-modification: determiner + adjectives + compound modifiers + head noun · Post-modification: head noun + participle/prepositional/relative phrase · hyphenated compounds: a three-year contract, an export-driven economy

📍 To pack precise information into a single phrase so it can function as one subject or object. Modifiers before the noun are compact ('a low-cost housing project'); information after the noun carries longer detail ('the decision taken by the committee in March'). This is the dominant style of reports, headlines and academic prose, where economy and precision matter more than simplicity.

💡 "It is a three-year government-funded training programme."
