Complex noun phrases and precision

العبارات الاسمية المركّبة والدقّة

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Pack meaning into pre- and post-modified noun phrases for precise, economical expression.

تكثيف المعنى في عبارات اسمية مركّبة (تعديل قبلي وبعدي) لتعبير دقيق واقتصادي.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

Pre-modification: determiner + adjectives + compound modifiers + head noun · Post-modification: head noun + participle/prepositional/relative phrase · hyphenated compounds: a three-year contract, an export-driven economy

[a three-year government-funded] [training programme] [aimed at graduates]

⚡ Number-plus-noun modifiers stay SINGULAR (a five-year contract). Hyphenate compounds before a noun, not after a verb. Post-modify for longer detail.

معدّلات العدد + الاسم تبقى مفردة (a five-year contract). واستخدم الشرطة قبل الاسم لا بعد الفعل. والتعديل البعدي للتفصيل الأطول.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

To pack precise information into a single phrase so it can function as one subject or object. Modifiers before the noun are compact ('a low-cost housing project'); information after the noun carries longer detail ('the decision taken by the committee in March'). This is the dominant style of reports, headlines and academic prose, where economy and precision matter more than simplicity.

لتكثيف معلومة دقيقة في عبارة واحدة تعمل كفاعل أو مفعول. المعدّلات قبل الاسم مختصرة، والمعلومات بعده تحمل تفصيلًا أطول. الأسلوب السائد في التقارير والعناوين والنثر الأكاديمي.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic builds these relationships with إضافة chains and with adjectives that follow the noun and agree with it, so stacking several modifiers BEFORE the head noun is structurally unfamiliar and the order feels arbitrary. Two errors are very common: pluralising a number-plus-noun modifier ('a five-years contract'), since the noun is plural in meaning, and omitting the hyphen that signals the words are working as a single modifier.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

It is a three-year government-funded training programme.

هو برنامج تدريبي حكومي التمويل لمدة ثلاث سنوات

[determiner + compound modifiers + head noun]

We faced a short-term cash-flow problem.

واجهنا مشكلة سيولة قصيرة الأجل

[hyphenated compounds + head noun]

The decision taken by the committee was final.

كان القرار الذي اتخذته اللجنة نهائيًا

[head noun + participle phrase]

She is a well-known writer in the region.

هي كاتبة معروفة في المنطقة

[compound modifier before noun]

Her earlier work is well known outside academia.

عملها السابق معروف جيدًا خارج الأوساط الأكاديمية

[compound after verb, no hyphen]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ They signed a five-years contract.

✅ They signed a five-year contract.

A number-plus-noun modifier stays singular before the head noun.

❌ It was a two-hours meeting.

✅ It was a two-hour meeting.

Same rule: the modifier is singular even though the meaning is plural.

❌ She is a well known writer.

✅ She is a well-known writer.

Before a noun the compound takes a hyphen; after the verb it does not.

❌ Her work is well-known outside academia.

✅ Her work is well known outside academia.

After the verb the hyphen is dropped, since the words are no longer modifying a following noun.
