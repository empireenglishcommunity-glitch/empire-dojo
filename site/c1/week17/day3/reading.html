A Growing Body of Evidence

حجم متزايد من الأدلة

نص متخصص: العبارات الاسمية المكثّفة بتكدّس معلومات وبتخفي مين اللي بيدّعي إيه.

📄 Read / اقرا

Consider the phrase "the well-documented long-term impact of export-driven, labour-intensive growth on deeply rooted regional inequality".

Twelve words, one head noun, and at least four claims that are not being defended.

Dense premodification is the characteristic register of the policy literature, and its economy of expression is real.

To condense that phrase into clauses would take three sentences, and a reader who works in the field will encapsulate it correctly without effort.

Precision of a kind is achieved: the writer has specified which impact, of which growth, on which inequality.

What is lost is the distinction between what is established and what is asserted.

"Well-documented" is a modifier, so it is not a proposition and cannot be challenged as one.

A significant proportion of the increasingly complex apparatus of this genre works the same way.

A widely held belief becomes an adjective; a contested causal mechanism becomes a compound premodifier; and "state-of-the-art" or "purpose-built" or "cutting-edge" arrive as description rather than evaluation, having been evaluated by nobody in particular.

The effect compounds.

Because the head noun is late, the reader assembles the qualifications first and meets the subject already framed.

The extent of the damage is difficult to quantify — which is itself a sentence of this type, and one I would not be able to substantiate if pressed.

There is no remedy in shorter sentences, streamlined prose being perfectly capable of the same manoeuvre.

The remedy is a reading habit: unpack the noun phrase into the claims it contains, and ask of each which is evidence and which is furniture.

In this literature the underlying causes of disagreement are usually sitting inside a modifier, where they cannot be cited.

y cannot be cited.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What does the writer say is lost in dense noun phrases?

إيه اللي بيضيع في العبارات الاسمية المكثّفة؟

Precision about which impact is meant
The distinction between what is established and what is asserted
Economy of expression

❓ Question / سؤال 2

Why can 'well-documented' not be challenged?

ليه مش ممكن تعترض على 'موثّق جيدًا'؟

It is a modifier, not a proposition
It is always true, because a well-documented claim has by definition been verified
It appears in the appendix

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What does the writer admit about his own sentence on quantifying damage?

الكاتب اعترف بإيه عن جملته عن قياس حجم الضرر؟

It is an example of the same construction, which he could not substantiate
It contradicts his argument
It was added by an editor

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What remedy does the writer propose?

إيه العلاج اللي اقترحه؟

Writing shorter sentences, since streamlined prose cannot perform the same manoeuvre
Avoiding compound adjectives
Unpacking each noun phrase and asking which parts are evidence
