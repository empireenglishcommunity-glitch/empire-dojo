📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Fluency, coherence and discourse strategies (C1 review)

الطلاقة والتماسك واستراتيجيات الخطاب (مراجعة C1)

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Sustain coherent, well-organised extended discourse; paraphrase, self-correct smoothly, and hold the floor; consolidate all C1.

الحفاظ على خطاب مطوّل متماسك ومنظّم؛ إعادة الصياغة والتصحيح الذاتي بسلاسة؛ ومراجعة كل C1.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

Repair: or rather, I mean, what I should say is, to put it another way · Floor-holding: that's a good question, it depends on what you mean by · Structuring: first, then, the key point is this, to summarise · plus all C1 structures from weeks 1-17

[The report was finished in May] — [or rather], [submitted in May]

⚡ Repair inside the sentence with 'or rather' and keep your pace. Hold the floor with a full phrase and a LEVEL tone. Signpost so the listener always knows where you are.

صحّح داخل الجملة بـ 'or rather' وحافظ على سرعتك. واحفظ دورك بعبارة كاملة ونبرة مستوية. وأشِر لموقعك دائمًا.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

To sustain long, coherent turns and manage them like an advanced speaker. Repair phrases let you correct yourself mid-sentence without collapsing; floor-holding phrases keep your turn while you form a thought; structuring phrases tell the listener where you are. This week also consolidates every C1 structure from weeks 1 to 17 into extended, connected discourse.

للحفاظ على أدوار كلام طويلة ومتماسكة وإدارتها كمتحدّث متقدّم. عبارات التصحيح تتيح تصحيح نفسك دون انهيار، وعبارات الحفاظ على الدور تمنحك وقتًا للتفكير، وعبارات التنظيم تُخبر المستمع بموقعك. ومراجعة لكل تراكيب C1.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Learners at this level usually control the individual structures but treat every hesitation as a failure, so they restart sentences, apologise, or fall silent — and silence with a falling tone hands the turn to someone else. Arabic conversation has its own repair and floor-holding formulas, but they do not transfer, so learners are left with 'erm' repeated, which sounds far less competent than their actual English.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

The report was finished in May — or rather, submitted in May.

انتهى التقرير في مايو، أو بالأحرى قُدّم في مايو

clause + [or rather] + correction

It's cheaper. I mean, more cost-effective overall.

إنه أرخص. أعني أكثر جدوى اقتصاديًا بشكل عام

clause + [I mean] + rephrasing

That's a good question — let me think about that.

سؤال جيد — دعني أفكّر فيه

[floor-holding phrase]

It depends, really, on what you mean by success.

الأمر يعتمد فعلًا على ما تعنيه بالنجاح

[it depends on] + [what you mean by]

To summarise, we have three realistic options.

خلاصة القول، لدينا ثلاثة خيارات واقعية

[structuring phrase], + clause

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ Sorry, sorry, let me start again from the beginning.

✅ The report was finished in May — or rather, submitted in May.

Advanced speakers repair inside the sentence and keep going rather than restarting.

❌ Erm... erm... erm...

✅ That's a good question — let me think about that for a moment.

A full phrase holds your turn and sounds competent; repeated fillers do not.

❌ I don't know. (falling tone, then silence)

✅ It depends, really, on what you mean by that.

A falling tone plus silence hands your turn away; a level tone keeps it.

❌ To summarise, and also I want to add another new point.

✅ To summarise, we have three options.

A summary signpost must actually summarise; adding new material confuses the structure.
