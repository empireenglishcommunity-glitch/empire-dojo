💬 This Week's Pattern / نمط الأسبوع

Inversion in conditionals (Had I / Were I / Should you)

القلب في جُمَل الشرط (Had I / Were I / Should you)

Had + subject + participle, … · Were + subject + to + verb, … · Should + subject + verb, … · Had it not been for … · Were it not for …

📍 To express a condition formally without using 'if'. Remove 'if' and move the auxiliary in front of the subject: 'If I had known' becomes 'Had I known'. 'Had' replaces third-conditional if-clauses, 'Were' replaces second-conditional ones, and 'Should' expresses a polite possibility ('Should you need anything'). This is standard in professional writing, contracts, presentations and formal speech.

💡 "Had I known, I would have acted differently."
