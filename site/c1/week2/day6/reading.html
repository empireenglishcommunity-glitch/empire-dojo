The Contingency Nobody Costed

الاحتمال اللي محدش حسبه

تحليل لقرار بناء: كل الاحتمالات كانت متوقّعة إلا واحد، والنتايج كانت بعيدة المدى.

📄 Read / اقرا

Were it not for a single omitted line in the 2019 tender, the eastern water network would probably have been finished on schedule.

That is a strong claim, and it is worth setting out the reasoning before accepting it.

The tender priced every contingency the engineers could anticipate: ground conditions, currency movement, a labour shortage, and the possibility that the main supplier would withdraw.

Had it not been for the last of these, the project would have carried no reserve at all.

What the document did not price was access.

It was assumed, on the assumption that the municipality would grant a right of way, that machinery could reach the western trench from the ring road.

Granted that this looked reasonable in 2019, the implication should have been obvious: the right of way was not a certainty but a prerequisite, and a prerequisite outside your control is a hypothetical, not a provision.

Should the municipality have refused — and it did refuse, twice — the only remaining route added nine kilometres to every delivery.

The outcome was far-reaching.

Provided the reader accepts that a schedule is a chain of dependencies, the nine kilometres were not a delay of nine kilometres but of four months, because the trench could not be opened before the rains, and otherwise the pumping stations could not be commissioned in sequence.

None of this was unforeseeable.

Two engineers said so in writing, and their memorandum survives.

What is regrettable is not that the circumstance arose.

It is that it was described, in every subsequent review, as inevitable — a word which quietly converts a decision into weather.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What single factor does the writer identify as the root cause?

إيه العامل الواحد اللي الكاتب حدّده كسبب أساسي؟

Access to the western trench being assumed, not secured
Currency movement
The main supplier withdrawing, a risk the tender had failed to anticipate or price at all

❓ Question / سؤال 2

Why does the writer call the right of way a prerequisite rather than a provision?

ليه الكاتب سمّى حق المرور شرطًا مسبقًا وليس بندًا احتياطيًا؟

Because it was outside the project's control
Because it was cheap to obtain
Because the engineers had priced it into the tender as a formal contingency reserve

❓ Question / سؤال 3

How did nine extra kilometres become a four-month delay?

إزاي تسعة كيلومترات زيادة بقت تأخير أربع شهور؟

The machinery broke down repeatedly
The municipality refused a third time
The trench missed the window before the rains, so the stations could not be commissioned in sequence

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What does the writer object to in the later reviews?

الكاتب معترض على إيه في المراجعات اللاحقة؟

That they called the outcome inevitable, turning a decision into something like weather
That they blamed the two engineers
That they were never published
