🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

What is she actually accusing him of?

هي بتتهمه بإيه فعلًا؟

Keeping a photograph he had no right to keep
Throwing away the letters without telling her
Saying something he knew was not what he meant, and letting it stand

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What does 'that's a bit rich' mean here?

إيه معنى 'دي زيادة شوية' هنا؟

That her accusation is unfair coming from her
That he agrees with her
That the photograph is valuable

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What does she mean by "'I know' has been doing a lot of heavy lifting"?

إيه معنى إن 'أنا عارف' شايلة حمل كبير؟

He uses it to end conversations instead of answering
He says it too quietly
He has admitted everything already

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What does he admit at the end?

اعترف بإيه في الآخر؟

He knew at the time that what he said was not what he meant
He never binned the letters
He put the photograph in the drawer deliberately

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Mona
You kept it. Don't — don't give me the face. You told me you'd binned the lot.
Tarek
I said I'd binned the letters. Which I did, and it nearly did me in, if we're keeping score.
Mona
Oh, we're keeping score now, are we. Because from where I'm sitting you've been sitting on this for eight months and letting me think otherwise.
Tarek
That's a bit rich. It's been in the drawer since we moved. I haven't been sitting on anything, it's been sitting there, which is not the same and you know it isn't.
Mona
Fine. Splitting hairs. Go on then.
Tarek
The letters were doing my head in. Every time I read them I came out worse. The photo's four of us on a beach. Nobody's kicking off in it. That's the whole point of it.
Mona
That is exactly what's wrong with it. Sorry. That came out — no, actually, I'll stand by that. I don't see a beach. I see the morning after, and you in the kitchen saying nothing while I'd been asking you all week.
Tarek
I know.
Mona
Do you? Because 'I know' has been doing a lot of heavy lifting in this house.
Tarek
What do you want me to say? I fudged it. In the car, coming back, you were in bits and I took the short version because the long one was going to take an hour.
Mona
Right. So the short version was a lie you could live with.
Tarek
The short version was 'I've binned everything' and what I meant was 'I'm not going to be the bloke who keeps a shrine'. Those aren't the same and I knew it at the time.
Mona
Thank you. That's all I was after.
