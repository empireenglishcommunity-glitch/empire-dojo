Advanced cleft sentences and fronting

الجُمَل المشقوقة المتقدّمة والتقديم

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Rearrange for emphasis and information flow: cleft (It is… that; What/All/The thing… is) and fronting of objects/complements.

إعادة الترتيب للتوكيد وتدفّق المعلومة: الجُمَل المشقوقة وتقديم المفعول/المكمّل.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

It + be + focus + that/who … · What + clause + be + focus · All (that) + clause + be + focus · The reason/thing/problem + be + focus · Fronted object/complement + subject + verb

[It was] [the price] [that worried me] · [What I need] [is] [time]

⚡ It + be + focus + THAT/WHO. What/All + clause + IS (singular). Fronted element first, then normal order.

It + be + العنصر + THAT/WHO. وWhat/All + جملة + IS بالمفرد. والعنصر المُقدَّم أولًا ثم الترتيب العادي.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

To control which part of your sentence the listener treats as most important. An 'it' cleft spotlights one element ('It was the price that worried me'). A 'what' cleft builds suspense before the key idea ('What I need is time'). Fronting moves an object or complement to the front for contrast ('That much I can accept'). These are the main tools for managing emphasis and information flow in argument and clarification.

للتحكّم في الجزء الذي يعتبره المستمع الأهم. الجملة المشقوقة بـ 'it' تُسلّط الضوء على عنصر واحد، وبـ 'what' تبني تشويقًا، والتقديم ينقل المفعول للمقابلة. أدوات أساسية لإدارة التوكيد وتدفّق المعلومة.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic marks focus mainly by moving words and by adding emphatic particles, and it has no direct equivalent of the English 'it … that' frame, so learners often skip the frame and simply stress a word instead. The commonest structural errors are omitting 'that' or 'who' in an it-cleft, and using a plural verb after a 'what' cleft ('What I need are time'), since 'what' feels plural when the following noun is plural.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

It was the price that worried me, not the design.

السعر هو ما أقلقني، لا التصميم

[It was] + focus + [that] + clause

It was Sarah who first raised the issue.

سارة هي أول من أثار المسألة

[It was] + person + [who] + clause

What I need is more time, not more advice.

ما أحتاجه هو وقت أكثر، لا نصائح أكثر

[What + clause] + [is] + focus

All I want is a straight answer.

كل ما أريده جواب صريح

[All + clause] + [is] + focus

That much I can accept, but no more.

هذا القدر أقبله، لا أكثر

[Fronted object] + subject + verb

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ It was the price worried me.

✅ It was the price that worried me.

An it-cleft needs 'that' or 'who' to link the focus to the rest of the clause.

❌ What I need are more time.

✅ What I need is more time.

The verb after a 'what' cleft agrees with 'what', which is singular.

❌ What I need it is time.

✅ What I need is time.

Do not add a second subject pronoun; the 'what' clause is already the subject.

❌ It was because of the price that I worried about it.

✅ It was the price that worried me.

Keep the cleft tight. Repeating the idea with 'because of' and 'about it' undoes the focusing effect.
