🔊 Target Sounds / الأصوات المستهدفة: negative and 'being' openers: 'Not having…' / 'Being aware…'

📝 Minimal Pairs / أزواج التمييز

Being aware of the risk… / Because I was aware…
/ˈbiːɪŋ/ (light) / BEING (heavy)
Not knowing what to say… / I did not know what to say, so…

🎯 Practice Words / كلمات للتمرين

🎙️ Say This / قول ده

"Not having received a reply after four full days, I called the office again. Being fully aware of the risk involved, we proceeded very carefully with the trial. Not knowing quite what to say in the moment, he simply nodded and looked away."
