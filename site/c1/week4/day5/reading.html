Having Reviewed the Evidence

بعد مراجعة الأدلة

ملخص تنفيذي متخصص: خلاصة مراجعة لبرنامج تدريب، بأسلوب مكثّف ورسمي.

📄 Read / اقرا

Having considered the submissions of all four departments, and taking into account the pilot data gathered between March and September, the review reaches three conclusions.

First, and being aware that this contradicts the interim report, the programme does not reduce processing time.

Measured across 1,400 cases, the mean fell by under two per cent, a figure well within normal variation.

Faced with this, the department has argued that the benefit is qualitative.

That may be so, but it was not what was funded.

Second, the programme does streamline handover.

Cases transferred between officers now carry a single concise summary rather than the previous file note, and errors at handover fell by thirty-one per cent.

Given that handover errors were the original justification for the funding, this is the finding that should have led the interim report, and did not.

Third, and in light of the above, the underlying design assumption requires revision.

The programme was intended to save time by improving quality, thereby recovering its cost within a year.

Compared with that projection, it has improved quality without saving time, and is thus better understood as a risk-reduction measure.

Assessed on that basis it performs well; assessed on the basis it was approved on, it does not.

Accordingly, and notwithstanding the department's preference for a further pilot, this review recommends that the programme be continued, with a view to re-approval under a corrected business case, and that the interim report be formally withdrawn.

Having said that, no reader should conclude that the programme failed.

It succeeded at something nobody asked it to do, which is a governance problem, not a design one.

not a design one.
