📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Advanced and impersonal passive (it is thought that …)

المبني للمجهول المتقدّم وغير الشخصي (it is thought that …)

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Impersonal reporting passives and passive infinitives: It is believed that…; He is thought to have left; is said to be.

المبني للمجهول غير الشخصي وأفعال النقل.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

It + is/was + reporting verb + that + clause · Subject + is said/thought/known + to + verb · Subject + is thought + to have + participle · Long chains: is being + participle, has been + participle, will have been + participle

[It is believed] [that the practice began earlier] · [He is thought] [to have left]

⚡ It is + reporting verb + THAT + clause. Or Subject + is said/thought + TO + verb. Add TO HAVE + third form for the past.

It is + فعل النقل + THAT + جملة. أو الفاعل + is said/thought + TO + فعل. وأضف TO HAVE + التصريف الثالث للماضي.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

To report information without naming its source, which is the default voice of academic writing, news and formal reports. 'It is believed that …' introduces a whole clause. 'He is said to be …' moves the subject to the front. Add 'have' for a past event: 'He is thought to have left.' Use it when the source is unknown, unimportant, or deliberately unstated.

لنقل معلومة دون تسمية مصدرها، وهو الأسلوب الافتراضي للكتابة الأكاديمية والأخبار والتقارير الرسمية. و'have' تُضاف لحدث ماضٍ. يُستخدم عندما يكون المصدر مجهولًا أو غير مهم.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic reports impersonally with active forms (يُقال إنّ / يُعتقد أنّ) that need no chain of auxiliaries, so English chains such as 'is thought to have been' feel top-heavy and are often shortened incorrectly. Two errors recur: omitting 'to' in the personal pattern ('He is said be rich'), and using a base verb instead of a participle after 'have' ('is thought to have leave'). Learners also mix the two frames into 'It is thought him to have left'.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

It is believed that the practice began much earlier.

يُعتقد أن هذه الممارسة بدأت في وقت أبكر

[It is + reporting verb] + [that + clause]

The building is said to be four hundred years old.

يُقال إن المبنى عمره أربعمئة سنة

[Subject] + [is said] + [to be] + complement

He is thought to have left the country in 1938.

يُعتقد أنه غادر البلاد عام ١٩٣٨

[Subject] + [is thought] + [to have + participle]

The proposal is being considered at the highest level.

يُدرَس المقترح على أعلى مستوى

[is being + participle]

The work will have been completed by then.

سيكون العمل قد أُنجز بحلول ذلك الوقت

[will have been + participle]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ He is said be very wealthy.

✅ He is said to be very wealthy.

The personal reporting passive always keeps 'to' before the verb.

❌ He is thought to have leave early.

✅ He is thought to have left early.

After 'to have' the verb takes its third form.

❌ It is thought him to have left.

✅ It is thought that he left. / He is thought to have left.

Choose one frame: 'It is thought that + clause' or 'Subject + is thought to + verb'.

❌ It is believed the practice began earlier.

✅ It is believed that the practice began earlier.

In formal writing keep 'that' after the reporting verb for clarity.

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

No source needed — the reporting verb carries the claim.
