It Is Widely Believed

من المعتقد على نطاق واسع

نص مكتوب بصيغة المجهول: الكاتب بيوضح إن الصيغة دي بتخفي مين اللي قال الكلام.

📄 Read / اقرا

It is widely believed that the reservoir project reduced regional water stress by a third.

It is also claimed, in the same paragraph of the same briefing, that the figure is provisional.

Both statements are unattributed, and the difference between them is the subject of this note.

Consider what the impersonal construction does.

When it is reported that consumption fell, the reader is not told who reported it, how, or whether anyone disagreed.

The claim is thought to be credible because it is presented as though already settled.

It is estimated that the reduction was 31 per cent — but estimated by whom, and against which baseline?

The 2011 figure is generally used; it is also known to have been collected during a drought, which makes any later year look like an improvement.

The authorities are said to have commissioned an independent review.

They are also reported, by two of the three engineers involved, to have declined to publish it.

Note that both sentences are equally passive and equally unverifiable, and that only one of them is likely to be repeated.

None of this is an allegation of bad faith.

The construction is standard in official writing, and it is often used simply to avoid a clumsy attribution.

But it has a systematic effect: it converts contested findings into consensus.

Where a named author must defend a hypothesis, "it is argued" needs no defence at all, because nobody is arguing.

The reader's practical test is short.

If a claim cannot be traced to somebody who could be wrong, it has not yet been substantiated — however widely it is believed, and however often it is allegedly disputed by people the text will not name.

text will not name.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What is the writer's main point about the impersonal passive?

إيه فكرة الكاتب الأساسية عن صيغة المجهول؟

It is grammatically incorrect in official writing
It converts contested findings into apparent consensus by removing who is responsible
It is always used to deceive

❓ Question / سؤال 2

Why does the writer question the 31 per cent figure?

ليه الكاتب بيشكّك في رقم 31 في المية؟

The baseline year was collected during a drought
The reservoir was never built
Consumption actually rose

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What does the writer say about the two passive sentences concerning the review?

قال إيه عن الجملتين المبنيتين للمجهول عن المراجعة؟

Both are equally unverifiable, but only one will be repeated
The first is true and the second false
Both should be deleted

❓ Question / سؤال 4

Does the writer accuse the authorities of dishonesty?

هل الكاتب بيتهم الجهات بعدم الأمانة؟

Yes, explicitly
The writer does not mention the authorities
No — the objection is to a systematic effect, not to bad faith
