Nominalisation for formal register

التحويل إلى الاسمية للأسلوب الرسمي

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

verb → noun: rise/increase, fail → failure, implement → implementation, develop → development, able → ability · the + noun + of/in + noun · upon completion of …

[Prices rose] → [the rise in prices] → [The rise in prices caused complaints]

⚡ Turn the action into a noun and make it the subject. Watch the ending (-tion, -ment, -ity) and the preposition (rise IN, failure OF).

حوّل الفعل إلى اسم واجعله الفاعل. وانتبه للنهاية (-tion, -ment, -ity) وحرف الجر (rise IN، failure OF).

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

To make writing formal, compact and impersonal by turning actions and qualities into noun phrases. 'Prices rose sharply, so people complained' becomes 'The sharp rise in prices led to complaints'. This is the dominant style of academic writing, reports and official documents, because it lets you package a whole event as the subject of a new sentence.

لجعل الكتابة رسمية ومكثّفة وغير شخصية بتحويل الأفعال والصفات إلى عبارات اسمية. الأسلوب السائد في الكتابة الأكاديمية والتقارير والوثائق الرسمية.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic derives nouns through internal root patterns (مصادر), so learners expect a predictable transformation and are surprised by the number of English endings (-tion, -ment, -ity, -al, -ance) and by the fact that some nouns are identical to the verb but differ in stress ('an INcrease' versus 'to inCREASE'). The typical result is either the wrong ending or a heavily nominalised sentence in conversation, where it sounds unnaturally stiff.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

The sharp rise in prices led to widespread complaints.

أدّى الارتفاع الحاد في الأسعار إلى شكاوى واسعة

[the + noun + in + noun] + verb + object

The failure of the system caused serious delays.

تسبّب فشل النظام في تأخيرات خطيرة

[the + noun + of + noun] + verb

Implementation of the recommendation was postponed.

تم تأجيل تنفيذ التوصية

[Nominalised subject] + passive verb

Her ability to explain complex ideas is rare.

قدرتها على شرح الأفكار المعقّدة نادرة

[noun + to + verb] + be + complement

Upon completion of the work, a full review is recommended.

عند إنجاز العمل، يُنصح بمراجعة كاملة

[Upon + noun + of + noun], + clause

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ The implement of the policy took two years.

✅ The implementation of the policy took two years.

The noun requires its own ending; 'implement' is the verb.

❌ The develop of the site was delayed.

✅ The development of the site was delayed.

Use the noun form 'development', not the bare verb.

❌ The rise of prices caused complaints.

✅ The rise in prices caused complaints.

'Rise' takes 'in' when describing amounts or levels.

❌ Her able to explain things is rare.

✅ Her ability to explain things is rare.

A possessive needs a noun, so the adjective must become 'ability'.
