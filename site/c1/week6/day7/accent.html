🎙️ Say This / قول ده

"This report examines the increase in demand recorded over the last three years and its implications for management. The introduction of the online system in the first year produced a marked improvement in efficiency, but the implementation was uneven across regions. Our assessment is that the absence of proper training explains most of the variation. The complexity of the new interface, combined with a reduction in support staff, reduced productivity in several offices. There is a clear possibility that further development will be required. Consideration should therefore be given to a revised training programme, and the availability of local support must be treated as a requirement rather than an improvement. Upon completion of that work, a full evaluation of the system's reliability is recommended. The commitment of senior management to this process will determine its success."
