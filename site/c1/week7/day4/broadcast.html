🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

Does she get what she asked for?

خدت اللي طلبته؟

No — he avoids answering to the end
She withdraws the question
Yes — he finally states plainly that it is her choice

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What does 'bite my hand off' mean?

إيه معنى 'يخطفها من إيدي'؟

Accept eagerly
Refuse angrily
Ask for more time

❓ Question / سؤال 2

Why does she say vagueness costs her and not him?

ليه بتقول إن الغموض بيكلّفها هي مش هو؟

He gets to be vague; for her it decides whether she goes home
She is paid less than him
He does not attend her reviews

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What is 'the whole ask'?

إيه 'كل المطلوب'؟

Give her more leeway
Remove the comment from her review
Say 'must' when it is required and 'up to you' when it is not

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Nour
Can I just check something. When you said I might as well finish it today — was that a 'do it today'?
Sami
It was a — I mean, you'd know better than me how long it takes.
Nour
Right, but I don't know whether I'm in trouble on Monday if it's not done. That's the bit I'm after.
Sami
You're not in trouble. Nobody's talking about trouble. I'd have thought you'd want the leeway, honestly, most people bite my hand off for that.
Nour
Leeway's great when I know it's leeway. When I can't tell, I do the cautious thing and stay till eight, and then you say I needn't have, and I can't work out if that's a thank you or a ticking off.
Sami
It's a thank you.
Nour
Last time it wasn't. Last time you said it and then it came up in my review.
Sami
Did it? That wasn't — that's not how I meant it.
Nour
I'm not having a go. I'm asking you to say the word 'must' when you mean must, and 'up to you' when it's up to me. That's it. That's the whole ask.
Sami
That feels a bit heavy-handed for what's basically a friendly working relationship.
Nour
It feels heavy-handed to you because you're the one who gets to be vague. For me it's the difference between going home and not.
Sami
...
Nour
So. Today, or up to me?
Sami
Up to you. Genuinely. Put it in writing if you like.
Nour
And you'll say it like that? Not 'if you get a chance'?
Sami
Not 'if you get a chance'. I hear it. I do it without noticing, that's the honest answer, and I'd rather you pulled me up on it.
Nour
That's all I needed. Thanks.
Sami
No — thank you. I'd rather be told than have you sitting here till eight, guessing what I meant.
