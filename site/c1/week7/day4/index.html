💬 This Week's Pattern / نمط الأسبوع

Advanced modals and semi-modals

الأفعال الناقصة المتقدّمة وشبه الناقصة

needn't have + participle · ought (not) to (have) · be bound/likely/unlikely to · may well · might as well · would rather (+ past for others) · was/were to have + participle

📍 To express fine degrees of necessity, probability, advice and preference. 'needn't have done' says something was done unnecessarily. 'ought to' gives advice slightly more formally than 'should'. 'be bound to' means near-certainty, 'be likely to' probability. 'may well' means quite possibly; 'might as well' means there is no better option. 'would rather' expresses preference.

💡 "You needn't have waited — I had already finished."
