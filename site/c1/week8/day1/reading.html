Arguably, the Safest Word

'يمكن القول' — أأمن كلمة

نص عن التحفّظ في اللغة: الكاتب بيهاجم استخدام الحيطة كغطاء، والموقف مش معلن.

📄 Read / اقرا

The report is, by and large, a model of caution.

It concludes that the scheme "appears to have delivered benefits to some extent", that costs were "relatively contained", and that the approach is "arguably suitable for wider adoption, subject to further work".

Each of those is defensible.

Together they are something else.

Hedging has a legitimate function.

Where evidence is genuinely tentative, "the data tend to suggest" is more honest than a flat assertion, and a good analyst would be right to qualify a finding that is only apparent.

On the whole, our professional literature would be improved by rather more of this and rather less confidence.

That is not what is happening here.

Notice which claims are hedged and which are not.

The benefits, which are contested, appear with every available qualifier.

The costs, which are documented and substantial, are described as contained without reservation.

Ostensibly the register is uniformly cautious; in practice the caution is applied asymmetrically, and it is applied to precisely the findings that would be inconvenient if stated plainly.

One might presume this is accidental.

It is conceivable.

Somewhat against that reading, however, the executive summary drops the qualifiers altogether, and it is the summary that will be quoted.

The caveat, then, is not really addressed to the reader.

It is addressed to the future — to whoever, up to a point reasonably, might later ask why the scheme was recommended.

Broadly speaking, that is what provisional language is for in documents of this kind.

It is not there to describe uncertainty.

It is there to be produced afterwards, in principle as evidence of care, and in practice as a receipt.

practice as a receipt.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What is the writer's real objection to the report?

إيه اعتراض الكاتب الحقيقي على التقرير؟

It is too confident about its findings
It hedges everything equally, which is unhelpful
Its caution is applied asymmetrically, only to inconvenient findings

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What does the writer concede about hedging in general?

الكاتب بيقرّ بإيه عن اللغة المتحفّظة عمومًا؟

It is always evasive
It is legitimate where evidence is genuinely tentative
It belongs only in executive summaries

❓ Question / سؤال 3

Why does the executive summary matter to the argument?

ليه الملخص التنفيذي مهم في الحجة؟

It is the most cautious section
It was written by someone else
It drops the qualifiers, and it is the part that will be quoted

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What does the writer say provisional language is really for here?

الكاتب بيقول اللغة المتحفّظة موجودة ليه فعلًا هنا؟

To be produced later as evidence of care
To describe genuine uncertainty
To make the report shorter
