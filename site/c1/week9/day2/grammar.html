Advanced discourse markers and cohesion

أدوات الربط المتقدّمة والتماسك

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Signal complex relationships formally: hence, thereby, conversely, notwithstanding, in that, insofar as.

الإشارة للعلاقات المعقّدة رسميًا: hence، thereby، conversely، notwithstanding، insofar as.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

however, nevertheless, nonetheless (contrast) · hence, thus, thereby (result) · conversely (opposite) · notwithstanding (despite) · in that, insofar as (respect/extent) · moreover, furthermore (addition)

[The results], [however], [were disappointing]

⚡ however/nevertheless need a semicolon or full stop, plus commas. thereby + -ing. notwithstanding + noun. in that/insofar as join clauses with no commas.

however/nevertheless تحتاج فاصلة منقوطة أو نقطة مع فواصل. وthereby + -ing. وnotwithstanding + اسم. وin that/insofar as تربط الجُمَل بلا فواصل.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

To signal precise logical relationships in formal writing and argument. 'however/nevertheless' mark contrast, 'hence/thus/thereby' mark result, 'conversely' introduces the mirror case, 'notwithstanding' concedes, and 'in that/insofar as' specify a respect or extent. These connectors are the skeleton of an academic paragraph, letting a reader follow an argument without rereading.

للإشارة إلى علاقات منطقية دقيقة في الكتابة الرسمية. هذه الأدوات هي هيكل الفقرة الأكاديمية، وتتيح للقارئ متابعة الحجّة دون إعادة القراءة.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic connectors usually attach directly to the clause (لكنّ / ومع ذلك / لذلك) and are not bracketed by commas, so learners either omit English punctuation or, more often, treat 'however' as a coordinating conjunction and join two independent clauses with only a comma. That produces a comma splice, which in formal writing is a visible error. Learners also mix up 'thereby' (followed by an -ing form) with 'therefore' (followed by a clause).

💡 Examples / أمثلة

The results, however, were disappointing.

لكن النتائج كانت مخيّبة

subject, [however], + verb

The evidence is thin; nevertheless, it is suggestive.

الدليل ضعيف؛ ومع ذلك فهو مُلفت

clause; [nevertheless], + clause

Demand fell sharply; hence the price cut.

انخفض الطلب بحدّة؛ ومن هنا خفض السعر

clause; [hence] + noun phrase

We automated the process, thereby reducing errors.

قمنا بأتمتة العملية، وبذلك قلّلنا الأخطاء

clause, [thereby] + -ing

The case is unusual in that no warning was given.

الحالة غير معتادة من حيث أنه لم يُعطَ أي تحذير

clause + [in that] + clause

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ The plan was good, however it failed.

✅ The plan was good; however, it failed.

'However' is not a conjunction, so it cannot join two clauses with only a comma. Use a semicolon or full stop.

❌ We automated it, thereby we reduced errors.

✅ We automated it, thereby reducing errors.

'Thereby' is followed by an -ing form, not by a new clause.

❌ Nevertheless of the delays, we finished.

✅ Notwithstanding the delays, we finished.

'Nevertheless' cannot take an object; 'notwithstanding' means 'despite' and can.

❌ It is unusual, in that, no warning was given.

✅ It is unusual in that no warning was given.

'In that' joins clauses directly and takes no commas around it.

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

'However' is an adverb, not a joiner — it needs stronger punctuation.

🎙️ Use it when you speak / استخدمها وانت بتتكلم

In accent week 9 you drilled the pause-dip-pause bracketing of 'however' and the final stress on 'nevertheless'. Those written commas are audible boundaries, and skipping them makes formal speech hard to follow.
