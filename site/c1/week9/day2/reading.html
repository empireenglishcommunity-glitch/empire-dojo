The Case and Its Joints

الحجة ومفاصلها

نص متخصص عن بناء الحجج: الحجة بتفشل عند المفصل، مش عند الدليل.

📄 Read / اقرا

An argument fails at its joints far more often than at its premises.

This is worth stating plainly, insofar as most training in critical reading concentrates on evidence, whereas the errors that survive peer review are almost invariably errors of connection.

Consider a structure common in policy writing.

A premise is established; evidence is presented; a conclusion follows, introduced by "thus" or "hence" or, more ambitiously, "accordingly".

The evidence may be impeccable.

Nevertheless the conjunction is doing work that has not been earned, in that it asserts a relation the data do not contain.

Correlation is reported and causation is inferred, whereby a marker becomes a mechanism without anybody claiming that it is one.

Conversely, a genuinely cohesive argument can be recognised by a simple test.

Remove every discourse marker and read what remains.

If the sequence still compels, the markers were describing a structure that was there.

If it collapses into a list, they were supplying one that was not.

Coherence, in other words, is a property of the reasoning; cohesion is a property of the prose, and the second is regularly mistaken for the first.

Notwithstanding this, moreover is not a crime.

Furthermore, the practice of signposting is what allows a reader to follow a long chain at all, and papers stripped of it are measurably harder to use.

The rationale for attending to joints is therefore practical.

Evidence can be checked by anyone with the data.

A connective cannot, which is why it is where a weak case is most safely concealed, and why the reviewer who concedes the evidence and interrogates the conjunction is the one who tends to refute something.

to refute something.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

Where does the writer say arguments most often fail?

فين بتفشل الحجج أكتر حسب الكاتب؟

At their joints — the connections
In their evidence, which is where peer review most reliably catches them
In their conclusions

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What is the test for a genuinely cohesive argument?

إيه اختبار الحجة المترابطة فعلًا؟

Count the discourse markers
Check the evidence against the raw data
Remove every marker and see whether the sequence still compels

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What distinction does the writer draw between coherence and cohesion?

إيه الفرق بين الترابط المنطقي والترابط اللغوي؟

They are the same thing
Coherence belongs to the reasoning, cohesion to the prose
Cohesion is more important

❓ Question / سؤال 4

Why is a connective the safest place to conceal a weak case?

ليه أداة الربط أأمن مكان لإخفاء حجة ضعيفة؟

Readers routinely skip over connectives, so no reviewer ever stops to examine them
It cannot be checked against data the way evidence can
Editors always delete them
