So Be It

فليكن

نص رسمي مكثّف عن صيغة الإلزام: الفرق بين 'يوصي بأن' و'يشترط أن' هو الفرق بين الرجاء والإلزام.

📄 Read / اقرا

It is essential that a drafter know the difference between a wish and a provision, and it is imperative that the reader be able to tell which he has been handed.

Be that as it may, the mandative subjunctive has become so formulaic that it now conceals precisely the distinction it once marked.

Suffice it to say that 'the committee recommend that the scheme proceed' and 'the committee require that the scheme proceed' are, in the syntax, identical.

Both take the bare subjunctive; both would be indistinguishable were the verbs exchanged.

Yet one is advisable and the other binding, and the whole weight of the sentence rests on a lexical choice the form does nothing to mark.

Far be it from me to propose that we ordain a new grammar.

The remedy is duller: that drafters stipulate, in a decree's opening provision, which of their verbs carry obligation and which do not.

Consider what happens when they do not.

A reader encountering 'it is vital that the record be retained' cannot know whether retention is a duty or a counsel of prudence, and lest that seem a small thing, it is the difference between a breach and a preference.

God forbid the question ever reaches a tribunal, where the solemn decorum of the construction will be read as evidence of intent — the form is grave, therefore the obligation was meant to bind.

That inference is unwarranted, and it is made daily.

Long live the subjunctive, by all means; it is one of the few genuinely economical things left in formal English.

But henceforth, come what may, let the verb do the binding and not the mood.

Otherwise the most solemn sentence in the document will also be its least determinate, which is a peculiar way to write a rule.

to write a rule.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What is the writer's central complaint about the mandative subjunctive?

إيه شكوى الكاتب الأساسية من صيغة الإلزام؟

It is archaic and should be abandoned
Its form no longer marks the distinction between advice and obligation
It is too economical for legal drafting

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What remedy does he propose?

إيه الحل اللي بيقترحه؟

That drafters state which of their verbs carry obligation
That the subjunctive be replaced by modal verbs
That tribunals ignore the construction

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What unwarranted inference does he say is made daily?

إيه الاستنتاج غير المبرَّر اللي بيقول إنه بيحصل كل يوم؟

That a grave form implies a binding obligation
That recommendations are never enforceable
That drafters deliberately intend the ambiguity in order to deceive the reader

❓ Question / سؤال 4

How would he describe a document's most solemn sentence?

إزاي بيوصف أكثر جملة مهيبة في المستند؟

Its clearest and most binding statement of duty, which a tribunal will always uphold
The one a tribunal will disregard
Also its least determinate, which he finds peculiar
