The Word You Wanted

الكلمة اللي كنت عايزها

نص عن الفروق الدقيقة بين المترادفات: التمييز بينها مش تنطّع، هو الفرق بين خبر صح وغلط.

📄 Read / اقرا

A misnomer is not a small thing.

To refute a claim is to disprove it; to rebut is to answer it; to contest is to declare it open; to dispute is to argue with it.

Only the first asserts that the matter is closed, and it is the one used when none of the others will do the rhetorical work.

The same collapse afflicts quantity.

Substantial denotes size, considerable denotes enough size to matter, and significant — outside statistics — has been worn down to mean roughly 'quite big', which is a pity, because inside statistics it means something precise and the two now share a page.

A reader encountering 'a significant reduction' cannot always tell which is meant, and the felicitous choice would be to say which.

Terminate, conclude, cease.

The first is transitive and administrative, the second implies completion, the third describes stopping without implying that an end was reached.

A contract that has ceased has not necessarily concluded, and an employee whose post is terminated has not concluded anything at all.

These are not shades a careful reader can ignore; they are the difference between a description and a claim.

Consider the connotative cluster around silence.

Keen, avid and eager differ in warmth; reticent, taciturn and laconic differ in what they attribute.

Reticent is a reluctance to disclose, and it is about a topic.

Taciturn is a disposition, and it is about a person.

Laconic is a style, and it is a compliment.

To call a witness taciturn where you mean reticent is to convert a choice into a character.

None of this is pedantry, and the test is easy: if substituting the near-synonym changes what a reader would then be entitled to infer, the words were never interchangeable and the precise one was doing work.

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What is the difference between reticent and taciturn?

إيه الفرق بين 'محتفظ بمعلومة' و'قليل الكلام بطبعه'؟

Reticent is a compliment; taciturn is not
Reticent is about a topic; taciturn is about a person's disposition
They differ only in formality, not in what they attribute to the speaker

❓ Question / سؤال 3

Why does he say 'significant' is a pity?

ليه بيقول إن كلمة 'significant' مؤسفة؟

It is overused in journalism
It has no equivalent in Arabic
It has a precise statistical meaning and a vague general one on the same page

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What is his test for whether two near-synonyms are interchangeable?

إيه اختباره لمعرفة إن المترادفين قابلين للتبديل؟

Whether substituting one changes what a reader is entitled to infer
Whether a dictionary lists them together as synonyms, which he calls the decisive test
Whether the register is the same
