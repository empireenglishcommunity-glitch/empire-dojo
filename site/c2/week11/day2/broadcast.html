📄 Transcript / النص

Message one — Hala
Hi, it's Hala, it's about ten past nine on the Tuesday. Look, ignore my last one, I'd got it completely the wrong way round. The valuation didn't come in low, the valuation came in fine — it's the survey that's flagged the retaining wall at the back. So the number in the email is right, the reason is wrong. I've asked Marwan to send you the survey itself rather than his summary of it, because his summary says 'movement noted' and the actual survey says 'no evidence of recent movement', and those are quite different sentences to be making a decision on. Ring me.
Message two — Marwan
It's Marwan, Tuesday about half eleven. Hala's told me off, entirely fairly. I've sent the full survey. Page fourteen is the wall. What it says is the crack is historic, it's been pointed at some stage, and the surveyor's recommendation is monitoring rather than works. So the eight thousand in my summary was my own estimate for rebuilding it, which I shouldn't have put in a document without labelling it as mine. There's no eight thousand anywhere in the survey. Sorry. Genuinely.
Message three — Hala
Me again, Tuesday, just gone two. Right, so the seller has come back and they'll do monitoring at their cost for twelve months, in writing, and they want an answer by Friday because there's a second party. I have no way of telling you whether the second party is real. In my experience it's real about half the time. What I can tell you is that the monitoring offer is unusual and it's worth more than the eight thousand you were braced for, because it transfers the uncertainty to them. Friday, then. And don't ring Marwan about the valuation, he's had enough.
Message four — Nadia
It's your sister. It's Wednesday morning. I've had all three of them forwarded to me and I want to say one thing and then I'll leave you alone. Everyone in those messages is talking about the wall. Nobody is talking about the fact that you told me in January you didn't want to live that far from Baba, and the wall is the thing you've found to be worried about instead. That may be completely unfair. If it is, ignore me and buy the house, and I'll help you paint it. But if I'm right, you've got until Friday and a retaining wall is a much easier conversation than the real one, so I thought somebody should say the real one out loud.
