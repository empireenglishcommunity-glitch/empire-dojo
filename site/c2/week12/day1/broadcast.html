I've Known Omar Since University

👂 Before you listen / قبل ما تسمع

A best man's speech. Warm on the surface. Underneath it there is both a reproach and an apology, and neither is stated.

خطاب صاحب العريس. ودود من برّه. جوّاه عتاب واعتذار، والاتنين مش مقولين صريح.

📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Best man
Right. Phones down, I'll be quick, and I've been asked by three separate people not to mention Prague, so I won't mention Prague. I've known Omar since university, which is nineteen years, and in that time I have watched him become extremely good at one specific thing, which is being the person everybody rings. You all know this. You've all rung him. Some of you rang him this week about the seating. He is the man who turns up with a van. He turned up with a van for me in 2016 and he has never once mentioned it since, which is the part I'd draw your attention to, because most people would have got two years out of that story. And I want to say something to Leila, who I met four years ago and who I liked immediately, and who I'll admit I was slightly wary of for about six months, entirely on Omar's behalf and entirely unnecessarily. Leila, you are the first person I have seen ring him and ask him nothing. You ask him how he is. And you wait. It's an extraordinary thing to watch, if you've known him as long as I have, because he doesn't know what to do with the pause and he's learning. Now. There's a version of this speech where I list his flaws for laughs, and I've written it, and it's funnier than this one. But I'd rather use my ninety seconds on this: Omar, you spent most of your thirties being useful to people, and a few of us let you, and I let you more than most. I've thought about that a fair bit this year. So my toast isn't to the happy couple, if you'll forgive me, because you'll get plenty of those. It's to the next nineteen years being the ones where somebody turns up with a van for you. And Leila already has. To Omar and Leila.
