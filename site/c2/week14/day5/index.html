💬 This Week's Pattern / نمط الأسبوع

Irony, understatement and litotes

السخرية والتهوين والتقليل بالنفي

Litotes: not + negative adjective (not bad, not unlike, not exactly cheap, no small achievement) · Understatement: diminishing phrase for a large event (a bit of a setback) · Ironic praise (a brave choice, an interesting approach) · Deadpan: flat comment on extreme content

📍 To convey strong meaning while appearing to say little, which is a defining feature of educated British English in particular. Litotes praises or criticises through negation. Understatement lets the listener supply the real scale. Ironic praise criticises without an explicit attack. All three soften the surface while sharpening the content.

💡 "That was not bad at all, actually."
