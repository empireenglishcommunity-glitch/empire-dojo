💬 This Week's Pattern / نمط الأسبوع

Rhetoric and persuasion

البلاغة والإقناع

Tricolon: three parallel items, third heaviest · Parallelism: repeated structure across clauses · Antithesis: two balanced opposed halves · Climax: escalating series · Anaphora: repeated opening words

📍 In speeches, arguments, closing statements and any writing meant to move rather than merely inform. A tricolon gives a claim a sense of completeness. Parallelism makes repetition deliberate. Antithesis sharpens a contrast into an argument. A climax builds pressure toward a conclusion. Used sparingly, these convince; used constantly, they exhaust.
