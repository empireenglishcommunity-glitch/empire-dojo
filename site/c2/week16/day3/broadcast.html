Announcer A
Good morning. This is a platform alteration. The zero nine forty-two service to Alexandria, calling at Tanta, Damanhur and Alexandria, will now depart from platform four. Platform four. This is due to a points failure at the north end of the station. Passengers already on platform two are asked to make their way over the bridge, not through the underpass, as the underpass is closed for cleaning.
Announcer B
We are sorry to announce that the zero nine fifty-eight to Port Said has been cancelled. This is due to a shortage of train crew. Passengers for Port Said may travel on the ten twenty-six, which will call additionally at Ismailia, or may use the replacement road service departing from stand C outside the main entrance. Tickets will be accepted on either. We are sorry for the disruption this causes.
Announcer A
This is a further alteration to the zero nine forty-two to Alexandria. This service will now depart from platform two. Platform two, as originally advertised. Passengers who have moved to platform four are asked to return over the bridge. The service is now expected to depart at ten oh nine and will no longer call at Damanhur. Passengers for Damanhur should travel to Tanta and change.
Announcer B
Would passenger Nour Al-Sayed, travelling to Aswan, please make herself known to a member of staff at the information point on the concourse. Passenger Nour Al-Sayed, to the information point, please.
Announcer A
The ten twenty-six to Port Said, calling additionally at Ismailia, is now ready for boarding at platform one. This service is expected to be extremely busy and will be formed of six coaches rather than the advertised nine. Reservations on this service are not valid. Please move right down inside the train and do not leave luggage in the vestibules.
Announcer B
A reminder that the underpass remains closed. Step-free access between platforms is available via the accessible route at the south end of the station only, and staff are on hand at the information point to assist. Passengers requiring step-free access to platform two should allow an additional ten minutes.
Announcer A
Platform two for the delayed ten oh nine to Alexandria. Tanta and Alexandria only. This is the final call for this service. Platform two.
