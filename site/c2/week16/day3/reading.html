We Came, We Saw, We Circulated a Summary

جينا، شوفنا، وزّعنا ملخّص

نص بيحلّل خطبة حماسية وبيستخدم نفس أدواتها البلاغية عليها.

📄 Read / اقرا

Three things were promised.

Three things were announced.

Three things, naturally, will be reviewed in the autumn.

That is a tricolon, and I have used it against a speech that used four.

The address on Tuesday was eloquent, impassioned and, by the measure that matters to whoever commissioned it, effective: the room stood.

It was also, considered as an argument, almost entirely composed of form.

Count the devices.

Anaphora — 'we will not wait, we will not defer, we will not apologise' — three clauses, rising cadence, climax on the least specific verb.

Antithesis, twice.

A rhetorical question whose answer was supplied within the same breath, which is not a question.

And a refrain of five words repeated at the head of each section, evocative and resonant and impossible to disagree with, since it contained no proposition.

None of this is illegitimate.

Ethos, pathos and logos were distinguished precisely because all three are needed, and a speech that galvanises is doing something a memorandum cannot.

To rally people is a real act.

My objection is narrower: of the three appeals, exactly one was present, and the compelling thing about the speech was that nobody noticed the absence, including — I suspect — its author.

Here is the test I would put, and it is not a hostile one.

Strip the cadence.

Set the content in a numbered list, in the flattest prose available, and read what remains.

If a call to action survives, the rhetoric was carrying an argument and doing it a service.

If what remains is a list of intentions and a date, the rhetoric was the argument, and the standing ovation was for the punchline of a joke about the future.

We came, we saw, we circulated a summary.

It sways nobody, but it is at least the right length.

at least the right length.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What is the writer's objection to the speech?

The speaker used too few rhetorical devices
The speech was too short to persuade
Of the three classical appeals, only one was present and nobody noticed

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What test does he propose?

إيه الاختبار اللي بيقترحه؟

Count the rhetorical devices per minute
Strip the cadence, set the content as a flat numbered list, and see what survives
Ask the audience what they remember

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What does the writer do in his own first paragraph and final line?

الكاتب بيعمل إيه في أول فقرة وآخر سطر؟

He uses the same devices he is criticising, and says so
He quotes the speech verbatim
He apologises for his tone

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What does he concede about rhetoric?

بيقرّ بإيه عن البلاغة؟

It is always a substitute for argument
A speech that galvanises does something a memorandum cannot
It belongs only in political speech
