Rhetoric and persuasion

البلاغة والإقناع

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

Tricolon: three parallel items, third heaviest · Parallelism: repeated structure across clauses · Antithesis: two balanced opposed halves · Climax: escalating series · Anaphora: repeated opening words

[It was slow], [it was expensive], [and it did not work]

⚡ Three items, third heaviest. Parallel items must match grammatically. Antithesis needs balanced halves. A climax must escalate. Use sparingly.

ثلاثة عناصر والثالث أثقل. والعناصر المتوازية تتطابق نحويًا. والمقابلة تحتاج نصفين متوازنين. والذروة تتصاعد. واستخدمها بقلّة.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

In speeches, arguments, closing statements and any writing meant to move rather than merely inform. A tricolon gives a claim a sense of completeness. Parallelism makes repetition deliberate. Antithesis sharpens a contrast into an argument. A climax builds pressure toward a conclusion. Used sparingly, these convince; used constantly, they exhaust.

في الخطب والحجج والبيانات الختامية وكل كتابة تريد التأثير لا الإخبار فقط. الثلاثية تمنح الادّعاء اكتمالًا، والتوازي يجعل التكرار مقصودًا، والمقابلة تُحوّل التقابل إلى حجّة، والذروة تبني الضغط.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic rhetoric is highly developed and shares all of these figures, so recognition is rarely the problem. Two issues arise instead. First, Arabic tolerates and admires far higher rhetorical density, so a direct transfer can sound overwrought in English, where restraint is expected. Second, English parallelism demands strict grammatical symmetry: if the first item is a noun phrase, all three must be, and mismatched items read as carelessness rather than as style.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

It was slow, it was expensive, and it did not work.

كان بطيئًا ومكلفًا ولم ينجح

[tricolon, third item heaviest]

We asked for time. We asked for detail. We asked for honesty.

طلبنا وقتًا. طلبنا تفصيلًا. طلبنا صدقًا

[parallelism / anaphora]

We were promised reform; we received rearrangement.

وُعدنا بإصلاح؛ فتلقّينا إعادة ترتيب

[antithesis, balanced halves]

Not this year, not next year, not ever.

لا هذا العام ولا العام القادم ولا أبدًا

[climax, escalating]

The plan was clear, fair and final.

كانت الخطة واضحة وعادلة ونهائية

[tricolon of adjectives]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ It was slow, expensive, and it did not work.

✅ It was slow, it was expensive, and it did not work. / It was slow, expensive and ineffective.

Parallelism requires matching grammatical forms; mixing adjectives with a clause breaks the pattern.

❌ We asked for time, detail, and we wanted honesty too.

✅ We asked for time, detail and honesty.

All items in a list must share the same grammatical shape.

❌ We were promised reform, but what we got was just a rearrangement of things.

✅ We were promised reform; we received rearrangement.

Antithesis depends on balance; padding one half destroys the symmetry that makes it work.

❌ Not ever, not next year, not this year.

✅ Not this year, not next year, not ever.

A climax must escalate; starting with the strongest item leaves nowhere to build.
