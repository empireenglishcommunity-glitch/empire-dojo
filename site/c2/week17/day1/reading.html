To Be Sure

لا شك في ذلك

نص بيبني حجة عن طريق التنازلات: بيدّي كل نقطة للخصم وبيكسب برضه.

📄 Read / اقرا

Admittedly, the case against the scheme is stronger than its advocates concede.

Granted that the costs have risen; granted, too, that the original business case was flawed; and while it is true that the consultation was perfunctory, I do not propose to defend any of that.

Critics might argue that this is a rhetorical manoeuvre.

To be sure, it is.

But the manoeuvre is only dishonest if the concessions are fake, and mine are not: each of those three objections is valid, and I would sign a paragraph saying so.

What I want to test is whether they are sufficient, because this overlooks the fact that every one of them is a criticism of the scheme's history rather than of its present alternatives.

It could be objected that a flawed origin taints what follows.

Even granting that, the question a committee faces in November is not whether the scheme should have been approved in 2021.

Nobody can vote on 2021.

The question is whether cancelling it now produces a better outcome than completing it, and on that the critics have been conspicuously quiet — not evasive, I think, but genuinely uninterested, because the case they have built is a case about process.

Here is where I would concede something more substantial, since a rebuttal that gives no ground is not even-handed and will not persuade anybody who was not already convinced.

If the completion estimate is as tenuous as the audit suggests, my argument fails.

I have not seen the figure.

Neither has anybody arguing with me, which is the actual state of the disagreement and is not a flattering description of either side.

So: a middle ground, offered conciliatorily and not as a compromise.

Publish the completion estimate.

If it holds, the process objections are real and insufficient.

If it does not, I will have been comprehensively wrong in public, and the critics may quote this paragraph.

quote this paragraph.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What is the writer's actual argument?

إيه حجة الكاتب الحقيقية؟

The critics have fabricated the cost increases
The objections are valid but concern the scheme's history, not the present choice
The scheme should be cancelled immediately

❓ Question / سؤال 2

How does he answer 'this is a rhetorical manoeuvre'?

بيردّ إزاي على 'دي مجرد مناورة بلاغية'؟

He agrees, and argues it is only dishonest if the concessions are fake
He denies using any rhetorical devices
He refuses to engage with the objection

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What would make his argument fail, on his own account?

إيه اللي هيفشّل حجته بإقراره هو؟

If the consultation was perfunctory
If the 2021 approval was flawed
If the completion estimate is as tenuous as the audit suggests

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What is notable about his description of the disagreement?

إيه اللافت في وصفه للخلاف؟

He says neither side has seen the key figure, which flatters nobody
He claims his side has all the evidence
He says the critics are being deliberately evasive
