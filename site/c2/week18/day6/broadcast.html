👂 Before you listen / قبل ما تسمع

A negotiation in which almost nothing is said directly. Refusals arrive dressed as agreement, and every number is offered sideways.

تفاوض مفيهوش كلام مباشر. الرفض بيجي بشكل موافقة، وكل رقم بيتقال بطريقة غير مباشرة.

Ms Sabbagh
Before we start — is Tuesday still realistic for you, or are we being optimistic?
Mr Kettani
Tuesday's realistic. Tuesday's realistic if the schedule attached on Thursday is the schedule we're working from.
Ms Sabbagh
I'd have thought so. Broadly. There's a question about item nine that our technical people would like to be comfortable with, and I don't think it's a large question.
Mr Kettani
Item nine is the indexation.
Ms Sabbagh
It is.
Mr Kettani
Then it's a large question. I'd rather say so now than at four o'clock.
Ms Sabbagh
That's fair, and I'd rather you did. Look — we can live with indexation. In principle. What we're finding difficult is indexation from year one, with no collar, on a seven-year term. If somebody put year three in front of me I don't say I'd sign it today, but I'd take it to Hisham without a covering note explaining why I was wasting his time.
Mr Kettani
Year three. ... We're some way apart on that. Year two I could probably defend. Year three, I'd be going back to my board and telling them I'd given away two years of the thing I was sent here to protect, and they'd ask me what I got.
Ms Sabbagh
Well. What would you want to have got?
Mr Kettani
Off the top of my head — and this is me thinking aloud, not an offer — the volume commitment in schedule two is currently a target. If it were a commitment, with the termination right falling away, then the indexation date becomes a much smaller conversation for me.
Ms Sabbagh
Mm. The termination right is not mine to give away in this room.
Mr Kettani
No. I didn't imagine it was.
Ms Sabbagh
But I could take the volume commitment on its own, with the termination right surviving, and I think that lands. And if that's where we are, then I'd want the collar as well as the date.
Mr Kettani
A collar at what?
Ms Sabbagh
Nought. A floor at nought.
Mr Kettani
...That I can do standing here. That costs me nothing I can think of, which probably means I should think about it for another minute. No — I can do it. Year two, floor at nought, volume becomes a commitment, termination survives.
Ms Sabbagh
Then I think we're closer than either of us expected to be before lunch. I'll have that written up and with you by six, and Tuesday stays Tuesday.

Tuesday stays Tuesday. And Farida — thank you for saying 'large question' rather than letting me find out at four.
