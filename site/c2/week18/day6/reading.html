Words I Have Cut

كلمات شيلتها

نص عن الإيجاز مكتوب بإيجاز، وبيسخر من نفسه لو طوّل.

📄 Read / اقرا

This paragraph had ninety words.

It has thirty-one.

What went was deadwood: two instances of 'it is important to note', one 'in terms of', a 'the fact that', and an entire sentence whose function was to announce the next one.

Concision is not brevity.

Brevity is short; concision is short having said everything, and the difference is that one is a length and the other is an achievement.

Verbiage is easy to spot in other people.

Padding in your own prose reads as rhythm.

Prune the intensifiers first — very, really, quite, rather — because they are the cheapest and their removal is never mourned.

Then excise the throat-clearing: every clause that tells the reader what you are about to do instead of doing it.

Then, and this is the painful one, distil the sentence you are proudest of.

Elegance is often a symptom.

A clause you would defend at length is a clause you have stopped reading.

A caution against the opposite failure, since I have taught this badly before.

Terse is not pithy.

Prose trimmed past its meaning is clunky in a new way, and a reader who has to reconstruct what you tightened out has been given work, not economy.

The lean sentence and the flabby one both fail; the crisp one is a third thing and it is harder than either.

I would offer a rule but the rule is unglamorous: write it long, then cut it by a third, then read it aloud.

Anything you stumble over was convoluted before it was spoken.

Understated as a principle, admittedly.

This piece is four hundred and two words and I have refined it eleven times, which is either evidence for the method or the funniest thing in it.

he funniest thing in it.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What distinction does he draw between concision and brevity?

إيه الفرق بين الإيجاز والقِصر عنده؟

They are simply two interchangeable words for one and the same writing quality
Brevity is a length; concision is being short having said everything
Concision applies to speech and brevity to writing

❓ Question / سؤال 2

Why does he say the sentence you are proudest of should be distilled?

ليه بيقول إن أحلى جملة عندك لازم تتقلّص؟

Elegance is often a symptom — a clause you would defend is one you have stopped reading
Proud sentences are usually grammatically wrong
Readers dislike ornamentation

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What is the opposite failure he warns about?

إيه الفشل المعاكس اللي بيحذّر منه؟

Using too many intensifiers
Writing long first drafts
Trimming past the meaning, so the reader must reconstruct what was cut

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What is self-deprecating about the final sentence?

إيه السخرية من النفس في الجملة الأخيرة؟

He admits the rule does not work and that he no longer uses it in his own writing
A piece on concision took eleven revisions and four hundred words
He says he has never taught the subject
