Operations
Karim? Karim, you're breaking up. Say the number again.
Karim
...four one... no, hold on, that's the bay. Four one seven. Pallet four one seven. Can you—
Operations
Four seven?
Karim
Four ONE seven. There's a one in the middle. It's the one with the seal gone on the short side, not the top. Short side.
Operations
Short side, seal gone, four one seven. And that's the Meridian consignment.
Karim
No. No, that's the whole reason I'm ringing you. The paperwork says Meridian. The crate stencil says Volkert, and the temperature strip inside is a Volkert strip, they're the green ones with the— ...are you still there?
Operations
I'm here. Paperwork Meridian, crate Volkert. Read me the strip.
Karim
Strip's showing... it's sat at minus eighteen for most of it, then there's a window where it goes up. I want to say six hours. Could be eight, the marks are close together and this torch is— hang on— ...right, eight. Eight hours, up to minus four.
Operations
Minus four for eight hours. That's over.
Karim
It's over. And before you ask, no, I can't tell you when. The strip doesn't timestamp, it just draws. But the port stamp on the outside is the nineteenth and it landed here Tuesday, so the window's inside those five days somewhere.
Operations
Tuesday was the twenty-third.
Karim
Then nineteenth to twenty-third, and the eight hours is in there. There's one more thing and then I'll lose you, I've got one bar. There are two pallets with the seal gone, not one.
Operations
Two? Karim, two?
Karim
Four one seven and four two one. Twenty-one, not seventeen, on the second one. Same stencil, same green strip. I've only read the strip on seventeen. I'm not opening the other one on my own and without somebody from quality standing next to me, and I'd put that in writing if you want.
Operations
Don't open it. Don't touch it. Photograph both stencils, both seals, and the strip you've already read, and stay off the radio about it until I've spoken to Nadia.
Karim
Understood. Photographs, no radio, wait for Nadia. And Ops — put in your note that I said two pallets at twenty past eleven. Because if this turns into a thing, I don't want to be the man who mentioned the second one on Thursday.
