But for a Single Line

لولا سطر واحد

نص افتراضي مكثّف: قرار واحد كان يمكن يتجنّب الكارثة، والافتراض ده مفيد ومضلّل في نفس الوقت.

📄 Read / اقرا

Were it not for a supposition nobody troubled to test, the eastern network would have been commissioned on schedule.

That is the counterfactual on which the entire inquiry has been predicated, and it is worth asking whether it can bear the weight.

Had it not been for the omission of a right of way from the 2019 tender, the contingency would have been priced.

But for that omission, the delay was conceivable rather than inevitable, and every subsequent eventuality — machinery routed nine kilometres about, a trench unopened before the rains, pumping stations commissioned out of sequence — hinges upon it.

Should the need arise to apportion blame, the line is short and it is clear.

And yet.

Were she to be asked, the lead engineer would say, as she said at the time, that a project of this scale forestalls nothing by listing risks it cannot avert; that the reprieve everyone imagines was contingent upon a municipality behaving as it had never behaved; and that a document which anticipated every dire eventuality would be a document nobody read.

Save for one thing, she would be right.

The omitted item was not a risk in the ordinary sense but a prerequisite — something the schedule was predicated on and which lay outside the contractor's control.

Hypothetically, a prerequisite can be insured; a hazard can only be reserved against.

The distinction is not academic, and had they known it, the reviewers would have looked for the provision rather than the reserve.

What precipitated the downfall, then, was neither negligence nor bad luck.

It was a taxonomy: the thing was filed as a contingency, and contingencies are priced, whereas prerequisites are secured.

Providential, in a sense, that it failed so publicly.

A quieter failure would have taught nobody the difference.

❓ Question / سؤال 3

Between negligence and bad luck
Between a tender and a schedule
Between a prerequisite, which can be secured, and a hazard, which can only be reserved against
