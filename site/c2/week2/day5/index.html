💬 This Week's Pattern / نمط الأسبوع

Literary conditionals with inversion

الشروط الأدبية بالقلب

Were it not for + noun, … · Had it not been for + noun, … · Should it transpire/prove/emerge that + clause, … · But for + noun, … · Were + subject + to + verb

📍 For elegant, formal conditionals that name a decisive factor. 'Were it not for X' covers present or general time; 'Had it not been for X' covers the past; 'But for X' is the most compressed of all. 'Should it transpire that' expresses a formal contingency. These belong to literary prose, formal reports, legal drafting and considered speech.

💡 "Were it not for her support, we would have failed."
