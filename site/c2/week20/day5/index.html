💬 This Week's Pattern / نمط الأسبوع

Full mastery: flexible, effortless and precise use

الإتقان الكامل: استخدام مرن ومتقن ودقيق

All C2 structures integrated: subjunctive (w1) · literary conditionals (w2) · stylistic inversion (w3) · information packaging (w4) · cleft mastery (w5) · grammatical metaphor (w6) · concessives (w7) · meta-discourse (w8) · evidentiality (w9) · lexical precision (w10) · evaluative choice (w11) · idiom (w12) · allusion (w13) · irony (w14) · register control (w15) · rhetoric (w16) · rebuttal (w17) · concision (w18) · repair (w19)

📍 Everywhere, and selectively. Mastery is not using every structure but having all of them available and choosing the one the moment requires. The CEFR describes C2 as flexible, effortless and precise use, and each word matters: flexible means you can switch, effortless means retrieval is automatic, precise means you choose the exact form your meaning needs rather than the nearest one you can produce.
