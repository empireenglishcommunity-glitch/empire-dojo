The Consummate Thing

الشيء المكتمل

نص ختامي: التمكّن مش إنك تعرف كل حاجة، لكن إنك تعرف إيه اللي المقام محتاجه.

📄 Read / اقرا

Mastery is the most oversold word in language teaching, and I have sold it myself, so let me try to say what it is not.

It is not range.

A learner with an enormous vocabulary and no sense of occasion is not accomplished; he is equipped.

It is not precision either, though precision is nearer.

The most precise speaker I ever taught was incomprehensible at parties, because he brought the same finesse to a joke that he brought to a specification, and finesse in the wrong register is just conspicuousness.

What consummate command looks like, as far as I can tell, is dexterity in the choosing.

The versatility to hear what a situation wants — brevity here, elaboration there, and in a surprising number of cases nothing at all — and then to do that, seamlessly, without the doing being visible.

Which means mastery is mostly invisible, and therefore mostly unrewarded, and therefore not what anybody trains for.

There is a hallmark, though, and I would offer it as the capstone of everything in this course.

An assured speaker is one who can be brief without seeming curt, elaborate without seeming long-winded, and — this is the rare one — silent without seeming absent.

Those three are not separate skills.

They are one skill, which is the integration of everything else, and it is the culmination of a great deal of orchestration nobody sees.

So the authoritative version of the advice is disappointing.

Consolidate.

Adapt.

Notice what the room needs.

And accept that the more polished you become, the less anyone will remark on it, because the distinguished quality of really accomplished English is that it draws no attention to itself at all.

Which is, I concede, a difficult thing to put on a certificate.

put on a certificate.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What does the writer say mastery actually is?

إيه التمكّن فعلًا حسب الكاتب؟

An enormous vocabulary combined with precision
The ability to speak fluently and at length on virtually any topic at all
Dexterity in choosing what the situation wants, done invisibly

❓ Question / سؤال 2

Why was his most precise student incomprehensible at parties?

ليه أدقّ طالب عنده مكانش مفهوم في المناسبات؟

He had too small a vocabulary to manage the demands of informal conversation
He brought the same finesse to a joke as to a specification
He refused to speak informally

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What is the rare third element of his hallmark?

إيه العنصر التالت النادر في علامته؟

Being brief without seeming curt
Being silent without seeming absent
Elaborating without seeming long-winded

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What is the irony of the closing line?

إيه السخرية في السطر الأخير؟

The best English draws no attention, which makes it hard to certify
Certificates are worthless
He has never issued a certificate
