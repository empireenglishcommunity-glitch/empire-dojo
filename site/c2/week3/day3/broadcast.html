Yousef (night)
Right, I'll do this once because I want my bed. Pumps: three and four are fine, don't touch them. Two is the one. Two has been cavitating on and off since about half one, and it's not the impeller because I pulled the inspection plate and it's clean. My money is on the suction side — I think we're drawing air past the gland, and the reason I think that is the noise changes when the header tank drops below about a third. So: keep that tank above a third and it behaves. It's a bodge, not a fix. Day shift can have the fix.
Rania (day)
Above a third. And if it goes below?
Yousef (night)
Then it'll start hammering, you'll hear it from the door, and you drop it to manual and bring it down over about ninety seconds. Don't trip it. If you trip it you'll take the line with it, because the interlock on two is still wired to the old logic — that's job number four-oh-nine, it's been open since February, and no, nobody is doing it this week either. Second thing. The chiller. Ignore the alarm on the panel, the alarm is the sensor and not the chiller. Actual outlet temp is reading seven-point-two on the local gauge and the panel is telling you eleven. The gauge is right. I put a bit of tape on it with 'GAUGE IS RIGHT' on it so nobody panics at ten in the morning.
Rania (day)
Panel eleven, gauge seven-two, believe the gauge. Have you logged that?
Yousef (night)
Logged it, and I've raised it as a sensor swap, but it's low priority so it'll sit. Third thing, and this is the one I'd actually worry about. Somebody has changed a setpoint on line two overnight and it wasn't me. Fill weight was two hundred and fifty grams at ten last night and it is two hundred and forty-six now. Four grams. Nobody has logged it, there's no initials against it, and four grams across a night is roughly — I did the sum at four in the morning so check me — about eleven kilos of product we have not put in a pot.
Rania (day)
You're saying it drifted or you're saying somebody set it?
Yousef (night)
I'm saying I don't know, and that's exactly why I'm telling you rather than just putting it back. If I put it back the evidence goes with it. So it's still sitting at two-four-six, the line is still running, and I've written the time I found it — three-fifty — on the sheet. If it drifted, we have a controller problem. If somebody set it, we have a different problem entirely, and I'd rather the person who has to answer for it isn't whichever of us happens to be standing here.
Rania (day)
No, that's right. Leave it. I'll get Nadia down before I change anything.
Yousef (night)
Good. So: tank above a third, gauge over panel, don't touch two-four-six. Everything else is boring. Sign here and I'm gone.
