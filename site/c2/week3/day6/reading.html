Down Came the Ceiling

وقع السقف

نص ساخر: الوصف الدرامي المهيب بيتصادم مع حقيقة تافهة، والسخرية في الفجوة.

📄 Read / اقرا

So great was the confidence in the new civic hall that never before had a building been opened with such ceremony.

Gone are the days, said the mayor, of provincial timidity.

Into the room walked the regional minister, and such was the poise of the occasion that hardly had the ribbon been cut when the photographers began to applaud.

Little did they know.

Down came the ceiling — not all of it, and not at once, but a panel roughly the size of a door, which descended with what witnesses described as immense dignity onto the buffet.

Here comes the part the report is stark about.

No sooner had the dust settled than the architect's office issued a statement of profound regret, in which the word 'unforeseeable' resounded through four paragraphs.

Not until the eleventh of that month did anyone mention the survey of the previous spring, which had recommended, in prose of some cadence, that the suspended sections be reinspected before any event at which the room would be full.

The minister was unhurt.

The buffet was not.

Spellbound is the word the local paper used, and one takes it to describe the guests rather than the ceiling, though the syntax leaves the matter open.

What unfolds from here is momentous only in the sense that everything is momentous in a town this size.

An inquiry will prevail.

Someone will be found to have been responsible for a document, though not for the ceiling.

And the hall will reopen in the spring with a plaque commemorating its opening, which will be the second one, and which will not mention the first.

mention the first.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

Where does the humour of this piece come from?

السخرية في النص جاية منين؟

The writer insulting the mayor directly
A series of exaggerated statistics
Grand ceremonial language applied to a small, undignified event

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What is the effect of 'The minister was unhurt. The buffet was not.'?

إيه تأثير 'الوزير مأذاش. البوفيه اتأذى.'؟

It establishes the seriousness of the injuries
It deflates the drama by equating a person with a table of food
It criticises the minister's conduct

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What is implied by the survey of the previous spring?

إيه المعنى الضمني في تقرير الربيع السابق؟

That the architect had never been consulted
That the ceiling had already been repaired
That 'unforeseeable' was untrue

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What is the joke in the final sentence?

إيه الطرفة في الجملة الأخيرة؟

The plaque will be installed on the ceiling
The town will commemorate an opening while quietly erasing the first one
The inquiry will not reach a conclusion
