🎯 The main point / الفكرة الأساسية

Answer this from listening. The transcript unlocks afterwards. / جاوب من السمع. النص بيتفتح بعد كده.

What is the argument really about?

Whether the timeline recorded in the minutes is factually accurate and complete
Who will attend the meeting in April
Whether Bilal should have been at the October meeting
Whose work Sabah credits, and therefore who will be asked in future

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What concrete change do they agree on?

Bilal will stop attending any meetings that Sabah is also present at
Bilal will count to five and then offer the handover out loud
Samira will circulate the timeline before each meeting
The minutes will be rewritten to credit Samira

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What does Bilal realise about his own account of his behaviour?

That he had the wrong date in front of him after all
That he had been asked by Sabah to answer on her behalf
That 'covering for her' was a flattering description of taking something from her
That he was repeating something he learned in October

❓ Question / سؤال 3

Why does Samira insist it is 'absolutely not about the minutes'?

Because the minutes are only the record of a pattern she is objecting to
Because the minutes were never circulated
Because she wrote the minutes herself
Because Bilal is not named in the minutes

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What does Samira mean by distinguishing 'a handover' from 'a substitution'?

In one she visibly gives the answer away; in the other it is simply taken
One is minuted and the other is not
One happens before the meeting, the other during it
One is permanent and the other temporary
