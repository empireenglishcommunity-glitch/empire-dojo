Cleft mastery across all types

إتقان الجُمَل المشقوقة بكل أنواعها

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

it-cleft: It + be + focus + that/who · wh-cleft: What + clause + be + focus · all-cleft: All + clause + be + focus · reversed wh-cleft: That/This + be + what + clause · there-cleft: There + be + noun + that + clause

[It was the timing] [that caused it] · [What worries me] [is] [the silence]

⚡ it-cleft isolates, wh-cleft anticipates, all-cleft limits, reversed points back, there-cleft introduces. Verb after What/All is always SINGULAR.

it تعزل، وwh تُشوّق، وall تحدّد، والمعكوسة تشير للخلف، وthere تُقدّم. والفعل بعد What/All مفرد دائمًا.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

To choose precisely which element your listener treats as the point, and with what rhetorical effect. An it-cleft isolates one element against alternatives. A wh-cleft delays the point to build anticipation. An all-cleft limits and often mildly complains. A reversed wh-cleft points back at something just said. A there-cleft introduces a new item into the discussion.

لتختار بدقّة العنصر الذي يعتبره المستمع النقطة، وبأي أثر بلاغي. الجملة بـ it تعزل، وبـ wh تُشوّق، وبـ all تحدّد وتشكو بلطف، والمعكوسة تشير للخلف، وبـ there تُقدّم عنصرًا جديدًا.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic marks focus through word order and emphatic particles and has no direct equivalent of the 'it … that' frame, so learners either omit the linking word or stress a word instead of building the structure. The commonest grammatical error is a plural verb after a wh-cleft ('What I need are time'), because the following noun feels plural. The subtler C2 weakness is using it-clefts for everything, which is repetitive and loses the distinct effects of the other types.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

It was the timing that caused the problem, not the plan.

التوقيت هو ما سبّب المشكلة لا الخطة

[It + be] + focus + [that] + clause

What worries me is the silence that followed.

ما يقلقني هو الصمت الذي تبع ذلك

[What + clause] + [is] + focus

All I asked for was a straight answer.

كل ما طلبته جواب صريح

[All + clause] + [was] + focus

That is exactly what worries me.

هذا بالضبط ما يقلقني

[That + is] + [what + clause]

There is one detail that still troubles me.

هناك تفصيل واحد ما زال يقلقني

[There + is] + noun + [that + clause]

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ What I need are more time.

✅ What I need is more time.

The verb agrees with 'what', which is singular, regardless of the following noun.

❌ It was the timing caused the problem.

✅ It was the timing that caused the problem.

An it-cleft needs 'that' or 'who' to link the focus to the clause.

❌ What worries me is that the silence.

✅ What worries me is the silence.

Do not add 'that' before a noun phrase; it belongs only before a clause.

❌ All I asked for were a straight answer.

✅ All I asked for was a straight answer.

'All' in this pattern is singular, like 'what'.
