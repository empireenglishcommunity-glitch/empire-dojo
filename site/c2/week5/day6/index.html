💬 This Week's Pattern / نمط الأسبوع

Cleft mastery across all types

إتقان الجُمَل المشقوقة بكل أنواعها

it-cleft: It + be + focus + that/who · wh-cleft: What + clause + be + focus · all-cleft: All + clause + be + focus · reversed wh-cleft: That/This + be + what + clause · there-cleft: There + be + noun + that + clause

📍 To choose precisely which element your listener treats as the point, and with what rhetorical effect. An it-cleft isolates one element against alternatives. A wh-cleft delays the point to build anticipation. An all-cleft limits and often mildly complains. A reversed wh-cleft points back at something just said. A there-cleft introduces a new item into the discussion.

💡 "It was the timing that caused the problem, not the plan."
