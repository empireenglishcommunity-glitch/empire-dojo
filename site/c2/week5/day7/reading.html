What It Comes Down To

الموضوع بيرجع لإيه

نص بصيغ التركيز: النقطة الجوهرية إن كل صيغة تركيز بتفترض حاجة القارئ بيقبلها بدون فحص.

📄 Read / اقرا

It is not the evidence that persuades a committee; it is the sentence the evidence arrives in.

What most drafters do is assemble the facts and trust them, and the thing that matters is precisely what they leave to chance.

Every cleft carries a presupposition, and the presupposition is where the work is done.

'It was the delay that caused the overspend' does not argue that there was an overspend; it assumes one, and invites the reader to dispute only the cause.

All the sentence has conceded is the part nobody now examines.

That is the hallmark of the construction and, one might add, its whole utility: the crux is smuggled into the frame.

The reason this is worth naming is that the technique is invisible in exactly the way a good technique is.

What a reader does is arrive at the contrast the writer has set up — delay, or something else — and the place the argument is won is not there at all.

It is in the clause that established the overspend as given.

There is a discipline that pinpoints this, and it consists of one question: what has this sentence assumed in order to ask its question?

It is not until you apply that test to your own drafting that its reach becomes uncomfortable.

The essence of most contested paragraphs I have read is a presupposition nobody chose deliberately.

What single them out is that they survive review — the nub of the disagreement is never in the disputed clause, and reviewers, being trained on claims, attend to claims.

The linchpin, then, is not honesty.

It is noticing.

A writer who clefts on purpose has decided what to make undisputed, and can be asked to defend it.

A writer who clefts by habit has made that decision anyway.

hat decision anyway.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What does the writer say a cleft sentence does?

بيقول إن جملة التركيز بتعمل إيه؟

It emphasises the most important fact
It shortens a long argument
It smuggles a presupposition into the frame, leaving only the contrast open to dispute

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What single question does he propose as a discipline?

إيه السؤال الواحد اللي بيقترحه كمنهج؟

Is this claim supported by the data, and would a hostile reviewer accept it?
What has this sentence assumed in order to ask its question?
Would a committee find this persuasive?

❓ Question / سؤال 3

Why do such paragraphs survive review?

ليه الفقرات دي بتنجح في المراجعة؟

Reviewers rarely read the whole paragraph, so the buried presupposition is never reached at all
Reviewers are trained on claims, and the disagreement is not in the disputed clause
The presuppositions are usually correct

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What is his final distinction?

إيه الفرق الأخير عنده؟

Between clefting on purpose, which can be defended, and by habit, which decides anyway
Between honest and dishonest writers
Between claims and evidence
