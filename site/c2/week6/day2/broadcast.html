📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Departing director
Thank you. Thank you, and thank you particularly to Hisham for an introduction of which I recognised most of the details. Fourteen years. I've been asked to be brief, by the same office that asked me to be brief in 2019, so at least there is continuity somewhere in this building. I want to say three things. The first is about the people in this room, and I mean it without qualification, which is why I'm putting it first and separately: the scientific staff of this institute are the best I have worked with anywhere, and everything that has been achieved here was achieved by them, frequently in spite of arrangements that were not designed with them in mind. The second thing is about the arrangements. I have been fortunate to serve under four board chairs, each of whom brought a fresh strategic vision, and I mean four entirely fresh ones. I have signed off eleven organisational restructures. I am told that the twelfth is in preparation, and I am confident it will be received with all the enthusiasm the previous eleven enjoyed. I should say that I have never once been asked whether the eleventh had finished before the twelfth was drafted, and I want to record my gratitude for the consistency of that approach. The third thing is a request, and it is the only request I'm going to make, so I'd be glad if it were minuted. When my successor asks for the figures on the Aleppo collection — and she will ask, she's rather good — somebody in this room will be tempted to give her the summary. I'd ask you not to. Give her the spreadsheet. All nine tabs of it, including the tab that nobody has been willing to put a name to since 2021. She will draw her own conclusions, which she is perfectly capable of doing, and she will draw them in her first year rather than her fourth. That is the entire favour I would like, and it costs nobody anything except a certain amount of discomfort in about March. Right. I'm told the food is excellent, and unlike the restructures, I've seen the plan for it. It has been the privilege of my working life. Thank you all.
