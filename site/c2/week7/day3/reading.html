Much as One Admires It

مع كل الإعجاب

نقد مهذّب بشكل مبالغ فيه: كل تنازل بيسبق طعنة، والمدح هو الوسيلة.

📄 Read / اقرا

Much as one admires the ambition of this proposal, and for all the evident labour behind it, a reader may be forgiven for wondering whether the labour was the point.

Granted, the framework is comprehensive.

However hard one looks, one will not find a consideration it has failed to consider.

Whatever the topic, wherever the reader turns, there is a subsection — and be that as it may, the effect is of a map drawn at a scale of one to one, which is admirable, and which is also the size of the territory.

Try as one might, it is difficult to identify the recommendation.

There are forty.

Notwithstanding that each is defensible, and irrespective of the merits of any given one, a document that recommends forty things has, paradoxically, recommended nothing, since the reader is left to choose and the choosing was the difficult part.

Even so, one concedes the steadfastness: nobody could accuse the authors of having taken a position they might later regret.

The downside, if it must be named, is counterintuitive.

It is not that the report says too little.

It is that in saying everything it has transferred the entire burden of judgement to a committee which commissioned the report precisely in order not to bear it.

Regardless of intention, that is what has happened, and the committee will now do what committees unwaveringly do, which is to select the three recommendations that were already policy.

One does not wish to appear ungenerous.

The prose is unfailingly courteous, the evidence is real, and the authors have plainly withstood a great deal of consultation.

It is a considerable achievement, and it will prevail on the shelf for years.

e shelf for years.

❓ Question / سؤال 1

What is the writer's actual verdict?

إيه حكم الكاتب الحقيقي؟

The report is comprehensive and should be adopted
The report is damning: by recommending everything it recommends nothing
The report is too short to be useful

❓ Question / سؤال 2

What is the function of the concessions ('granted', 'much as one admires')?

إيه وظيفة عبارات التنازل زي 'مع كل الإعجاب'؟

They express genuine approval of the framework
They precede and soften each criticism, making the attack deniable
They are required by the review format

❓ Question / سؤال 3

What does 'a map drawn at a scale of one to one' mean here?

إيه معنى 'خريطة بمقياس واحد إلى واحد' هنا؟

It is as large and unwieldy as the thing it describes, so it is useless
It is exceptionally accurate and therefore valuable
It was drawn without consulting the territory

❓ Question / سؤال 4

What is implied by 'it will prevail on the shelf for years'?

إيه المعنى الضمني في 'هيفضل على الرف لسنين'؟

It will remain influential
It will be ignored
It will be reprinted repeatedly
