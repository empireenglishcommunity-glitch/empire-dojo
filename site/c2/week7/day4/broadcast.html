Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Host
Eighteen minutes past. We're on the parking scheme, the lines are jammed, and I'll be honest with you, about nine in ten of you are against it. Line four, Mahmoud in Dokki, you're through.
Mahmoud
Yes, morning. Now I've read the consultation document, all sixty pages of it, which I'd wager is more than the councillor who's coming on after me—
Host
She's listening, Mahmoud.
Mahmoud
Good, I hope she is. My objection is this. They say the scheme is revenue-neutral. Now, revenue-neutral over what period? Because if you read annex C, the capital cost of the barriers is amortised over twelve years and the income projection runs for seven. You cannot call that neutral. You can call it neutral for seven years and then a bill.
Host
That's a specific point and I'll put it to her. But can I ask you — if she came back and said 'you're right, we'll amortise it over seven', would you then support the scheme?
Mahmoud
...I'd have to look at the— no. No, I wouldn't.
Host
So it isn't the accounting.
Mahmoud
It isn't only the accounting. It's that nobody asked us and now they're asking us about the numbers so that the answer has to be about numbers.
Host
Right. Thank you, that's a much better argument than the one you started with. Line two, Fatima, you're a resident of the same street I think?
Fatima
I am, and I'll say something unpopular. I'm for it. And the reason I'm for it isn't the traffic, it's that I've got a mother who's eighty-one and a wheelchair that needs a metre and a half of clearance to get out of the car, and at the moment I have that clearance roughly one morning in five.
Host
And under the scheme?
Fatima
Under the scheme there's a marked bay outside number forty. Which I would have. Now — I want to be fair to Mahmoud, because he's my neighbour and he's not wrong about the consultation. Nobody knocked on my door either. I found out from a lamp post. But I'd rather be presented with a bad process and a bay than a good process and no bay, and I think a lot of people who feel like me aren't ringing you, because it sounds like you're admitting you'll take anything.
Host
That's — hold on, that's the most interesting thing anybody's said this morning. Say that last bit again.
Fatima
If you say out loud that you'll accept a scheme that was pushed through badly, you sound like somebody who can be pushed. So people like me stay quiet, and then the phone-in tells you nine in ten are against, and the nine in ten is real but it isn't everybody.
Host
Councillor, you've been listening. You've got ninety seconds after this.
