📄 Transcript / النص

Now read while you listen again — check what you missed. / اقرا وإنت بتسمع تاني — شوف إيه اللي فاتك.

Counsel
Mr Rashad, before the break you told my learned friend that the inspection regime was, and I wrote it down, 'broadly compliant'. Do you stand by that?
Mr Rashad
I do.
Counsel
Would you help me with 'broadly'? Is that a term of art in your industry, or is it a word you chose this morning?
Mr Rashad
It's — it isn't a defined term, no. It's a fair summary.
Counsel
A fair summary of what proportion? Half? Nine tenths?
Mr Rashad
I couldn't put a figure on it standing here.
Counsel
No. But somebody did, didn't they. Bundle four, tab nineteen. That's your own internal quarterly, your name at the top. What figure does it give for completed inspections against schedule?
Mr Rashad
...Sixty-one per cent.
Counsel
Sixty-one. Is sixty-one 'broadly'?
Mr Rashad
In the context of a regime that was being rebuilt at the time, I'd say it—
Counsel
I'll come to the rebuilding. My question was whether sixty-one per cent is 'broadly compliant'.
Mr Rashad
On its own, no, I wouldn't describe sixty-one as broadly.
Counsel
Thank you. Now the rebuilding, since you raise it. The regime was being rebuilt because of the 2019 findings.
Mr Rashad
Partly, yes.
Counsel
And the 2019 findings concerned the same three sites we are concerned with today.
Mr Rashad
Two of the three.
Counsel
Two of the three. I'm grateful — that's a correction I'll accept. Which two?
Mr Rashad
Harrow Bank and the Quay.
Counsel
So by the time of this quarterly, you knew that two of these three sites had been the subject of adverse findings four years earlier, you knew your own completion rate stood at sixty-one per cent, and the word you selected for the board was 'broadly'. That's right, isn't it?
Mr Rashad
The word I selected was — yes. I wrote that word. I'd say in fairness the table was in the same document, two pages later.
Counsel
It was. Two pages later, in nine-point type, in an appendix. And the board minute for that meeting records what, on the inspection regime?
Mr Rashad
...'Noted as broadly compliant.'
Counsel
Your word, in their minute. Nothing further, my lord.
