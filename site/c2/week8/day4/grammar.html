📅 This week's pattern / نمط الأسبوع

One pattern per week — it stays the same all week, so practise it every day. / نمط واحد كل أسبوع — بيفضل نفسه طول الأسبوع، فاتمرن عليه كل يوم.

Meta-discourse and sophisticated cohesion

ما وراء الخطاب والتماسك المتطوّر

🔎 In short / بإيجاز

Signal your own discourse and guide the reader: as noted above, it is worth stressing, to digress briefly, by way of illustration, in parenthesis.

الإشارة إلى خطابك وتوجيه القارئ: as noted above، it is worth stressing، by way of illustration، in parenthesis.

🧩 The Pattern / النمط

Retrospective: as noted above, as I have argued, as we have seen · Prospective: as will become clear, I shall show · Emphasis: it is worth noting that, I would draw attention to · Return: to return to my earlier point, leaving that aside · Parenthetical asides between commas

[The figures], [as I noted earlier], [are provisional]

⚡ Signpost backwards, forwards, and when you return. Asides take commas on BOTH sides. Keep 'that' after emphasis markers. One signpost at a time.

أشِر للخلف وللأمام وعند العودة. والاعتراضات بفواصل على الجانبين. وأبقِ 'that' بعد علامات التوكيد. وإشارة واحدة في المرّة.

📍 When to use it / امتى تستخدمه

To talk about your own argument so a reader or listener can navigate it. Retrospective signposts point back, prospective ones point forward, emphasis markers raise importance, and return phrases recover the thread after a digression. This is what makes a long paper or speech followable, and its absence is the commonest reason complex writing feels impenetrable.

للحديث عن حجّتك نفسها ليتمكّن القارئ أو المستمع من التنقّل فيها. إشارات للخلف وأخرى للأمام وعلامات توكيد وعبارات عودة بعد الاستطراد. وهذا ما يجعل الورقة أو الخطاب الطويل قابلًا للمتابعة.

⚠️ Why this is tricky for Arabic speakers / ليه دي صعبة على العربي

Arabic academic prose signals structure with explicit connectors attached to clauses rather than with bracketed parenthetical commentary, so learners often omit the commas around an aside or write it as a separate sentence, which breaks the flow. The more damaging habit is omitting meta-discourse entirely: the argument may be excellent, but with no signposts the reader cannot tell which claim is central and which is background.

💡 Examples / أمثلة

As noted above, the figures remain provisional.

كما ذُكر أعلاه، تبقى الأرقام مؤقّتة

[retrospective signpost], + clause

The figures, as I noted earlier, are provisional.

الأرقام، كما ذكرت سابقًا، مؤقّتة

subject, [aside], + verb

It is worth noting that nobody was consulted.

من الجدير بالذكر أن أحدًا لم يُستشَر

[emphasis marker] + [that + clause]

To return to my earlier point, the timetable is the real issue.

بالعودة إلى نقطتي السابقة، الجدول الزمني هو المسألة الحقيقية

[return phrase], + clause

As will become clear, my argument does not require a large effect.

كما سيتّضح، لا تتطلّب حجّتي أثرًا كبيرًا

[prospective signpost], + clause

🚫 Common mistakes / أخطاء شائعة

❌ The figures as I noted earlier are provisional.

✅ The figures, as I noted earlier, are provisional.

A parenthetical aside needs commas on both sides, or the sentence becomes hard to parse.

❌ As noted above the figures remain provisional.

✅ As noted above, the figures remain provisional.

An initial signpost is followed by a comma.

❌ It is worth noting nobody was consulted.

✅ It is worth noting that nobody was consulted.

In formal writing keep 'that' after the emphasis marker.

❌ As I have argued before in the previous section above earlier.

✅ As I have argued, …

One signpost is enough; stacking them is redundant and clumsy.

Fill in the blank. Completing all of them marks this exercise done. / املا الفراغ. لما تخلص كلهم التمرين يتحسب.

🧠 Remember it / افتكرها

Tell the reader where they are.

🎙️ Use it when you speak / استخدمها وانت بتتكلم

In accent week 8 you drilled asides in a lower pitch band with pauses on both sides. Those written commas are audible boundaries, and without the pitch drop commentary and content become indistinguishable.
